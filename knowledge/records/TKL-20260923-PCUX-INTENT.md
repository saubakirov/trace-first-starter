## Source and decision context

The owner confirmed these six related aspects of one intended experience:

1. **Replace mechanics, not judgment.** In authorized gates-only work, the parent performs the owner's ordinary launch: exact TFW command plus task, with context obtained from task files. Waiting prompts, solution hints, retold conversations and extra micromanagement defeat that equivalence. The absence of chatter does not suppress required gates or reserved owner decisions.
2. **Protect the owner's context.** A selected GATEWAY is the stable owner interface, not a collector of worker traffic. Phase Coordinators absorb operating detail and return bounded gates/results; workers return to their own Coordinator. This separation exists to preserve the owner's useful conversation, not to multiply supervisory roles.
3. **Preserve the Coordinator's thinking in every mode.** Manual, autonomous and explicitly permitted dialogue modes change mechanics, not the Strategic Architect duty. Working Backwards, constructive critique, consequential owner-visible hypotheses, concrete future-result visualization and Saint-Exupery judgment remain valuable. Simplification removes unnecessary control without discarding meaning or proof.
4. **New emphasis is not an exhaustive restatement.** The owner highlighting a new requirement does not silently waive existing obligations. The owner must not have to recall the complete TFW contract to prevent regressions; per-launch model and reasoning-effort selection was the explicit example. This does not freeze obsolete wording or bar explicitly approved changes.
5. **Account for context cost as well as tool availability.** Subagent traffic can burden the Coordinator's conversation and context in the owner's intended usage. That is a reason to limit the compact route to genuinely small one-phase work or a small debt/fix. It is not a universal measured performance claim, evidence of browser support or permission to weaken the complete TFW roles and independent review.
6. **Judge preservation of intent, not artifact completion alone.** A procedurally complete result can still lose the owner's purpose. The owner-held frozen-conversation clone is PCUX's optional diagnostic for such loss, distortion and unnecessary additions. It is not an extra mandatory TFW stage, a guaranteed impartial oracle, a replacement Reviewer or a pre-DONE gate. The team does not inspect, brief or dispose of that protected clone.
