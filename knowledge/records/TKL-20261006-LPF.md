## Source and decision context

The owner set the goal and value plainly: less junk in the repository, because it keeps growing, and
the value is speed — faster work with the repository, easier search, fewer tokens during analysis.
Evidence was meant to let people and agents verify a result; once they have approved it, the raw
material serves no one. Daily records were the worst case: results and leftovers mixed in one folder.

The comment rule is a consequence, not a goal. Comments had turned into a slow, growing
correspondence between agents, a place to leave work undone with a note, and extra tokens, including
inside TFW's own prompts and configuration. Research showed that 150 of 152 "historical" lines in
`.tfw/` are protective rules rather than chatter, so the lasting effect is the forward rule and an
optional, explained cleanup for receivers, not a rewrite of the past.

## Relations and current use

Read with D52 and D53. The operative text is `conventions.md` §12 `Working material` and `Comments`,
the `Evidence subfolder` section, the EV template, `handoff.md` steps 8–11, `verify.md` and the Daily
skill; this record explains why they read as they do. The location rule names the system temporary
directory, not a TFW root, because only the former is writable by default in every observed agent
sandbox.
