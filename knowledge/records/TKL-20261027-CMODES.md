## Source and decision context

Removing the phase-count restriction makes one-chat completion explicit without collapsing role
judgment. Same-phase corrections retain the Executor and independent Reviewer; each new phase
defaults to a fresh Reviewer. Any cross-phase exception needs the exact governing discretion and
receiving checks. The actual launcher retains model/effort judgment within supported controls.

One existing catalogue governs both transports. Research preserves stage decisions in its files;
human–Coordinator discussion remains substantive. Returned evidence is used at its stated level;
only required independent checks or concrete changed inputs/gaps justify repetition. Ordinary
receivers validate files, history and authority without installing upstream Python tools.

The existing execution–review–ruled correction cycle and phase readiness remain. No minimum-three
rule, general retry cap, Ralph runtime, new lifecycle or second accounting system is introduced.
Accepted ECFIX is unchanged and includes failed/auxiliary sources without double counting.
