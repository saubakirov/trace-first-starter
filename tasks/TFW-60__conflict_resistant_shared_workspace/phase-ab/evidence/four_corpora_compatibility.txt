TFW-60 / Phase AB — identifier compatibility at four pinned commits
Date: 2026-08-29

Method
------
For each pinned commit, `git ls-tree -r --name-only <commit>` supplied candidate directory
names beneath the commit's configured task containers. The pre-change two-form parser was
implemented inline from its anchored legacy and dirty-clock regexes. Its result for every
previously recognized directory was compared with the new shared dispatcher. Live working
trees and their HEADs were not used or modified.

Notation: every identifier below satisfied
`before(kind, normalized identifier) == after(kind, normalized identifier)`.
