# Challenge — "What do we NOT expect?"
> **Mindset:** Critic. You built the configurations. Now attack them. Every survivor needs evidence. Every elimination needs a reason.
> **Test:** "Would my surviving configurations hold if a different researcher attacked them?"
> Parent: [HL-TFW-48](../../HL-TFW-48__value_first_methodology_rebaseline.md)
> Goal: Re-derive TFW from its product purpose and production learning so that its compact method kernel preserves meaning, evidence, independent judgment, and portable knowledge across domains.

## Findings

### C1. H1 fails if “canonical” means “remote”

The strongest counterexample is the Researcher role lock. An agent must know the boundary
before it edits; a later artifact cannot always undo unauthorized work. The current
conventions similarly say Role Lock and Mindset stay inline and pure references are
broken. `ReasonIF` supplies an external weaker-model warning: instruction presence does
not guarantee adherence, particularly on harder tasks.

The opposite universal also fails. Atamat TFW-2.100 repeated detailed instructions and
completion language but still produced false completeness. AFD-34 observed re-drift
after literal root facts were copied. Full manual repetition increases update surfaces
without supplying independent evidence.

H1 survives only under this boundary:

| Consequence | Enforcement | Surviving deployment |
|-------------|-------------|----------------------|
| Unauthorized, destructive, safety-critical, or role-breaking action | Must act before/at the action | Canonical owner plus a complete local imperative and hard gate |
| False evidence or verdict | Observable after/around the action | Canonical owner plus short local cue and executable/source/real-flow gate |
| Lifecycle/navigation omission | Artifact/state is observable | Canonical owner plus point checkpoint |
| Reversible precision or explanatory loss | Low immediate harm | On-demand reference |

Generated local derivatives can preserve a full point-of-use statement only if they carry
the source version and fail visibly when stale. Otherwise they merely automate
duplication.

**Challenge verdict H1:** **conditionally supported.** The evidence supports replacing
full repetition in many workflows, not removing localized critical imperatives. A
reference with no observable enforcement is not the hypothesized mechanism.

### C2. H2 fails if every number is presumed guilty

Three counterexamples protect numeric or gated **mechanisms**, not the current threshold
values:

- a bounded batch of blocking questions prevents one agent from transferring an
  unbounded decision queue to the coordinator; this does not validate three as the
  universal threshold;
- a source-sufficiency gate prevents unsupported durable knowledge, but sufficiency
  depends on claim type and authority. One authoritative primary source or explicit
  owner decision may be sufficient for an ownership/decision claim, while an empirical
  generalization may require independent triangulation. This does not validate two as a
  universal threshold;
- discrepancy-triggered expansion of review protects against assuming a sample remains
  representative after a contradiction; the current 0.42 trigger and exact expanded
  scope remain unvalidated.

Removing a number before defining its mechanism would make the rule less testable, but
none of these examples validates its current numeric value.

The scope budgets already support `split OR documented override`. The failure is not
their existence; it is presenting a visible proxy without carrying purpose, counting
rule, and override semantics to the decision point. The TFW-48 Gather stage's accepted
15-file override is a positive counterexample: a soft signal caused an explicit scope
decision without becoming completion proof.

The surviving limit test is:

1. name the protected failure and affected value;
2. state whether the number is a boundary, trigger, target, default, or measurement;
3. define the counting/measurement rule;
4. define who can override and what evidence/justification is required;
5. define the response to breach;
6. schedule recalibration from production evidence.

**Challenge verdict H2:** **supported with revised wording.** Some limits demonstrably
invite proxy optimization. A number should remain **hard** only when tied to a defined
protected failure or invariant, not necessarily a previously observed incident; its
specific value still requires separate authority or calibration. Other numbers may
remain as configurable signals, defaults, or escalation thresholds. All individual TFW
values remain unresolved in Iteration 1.

### C3. H3 is dominant, not exclusive

An attack on H3 looked for a learning that had nowhere valid to go:

- project-specific values can live in the project layer, but the framework currently
  lacks a fully registered extension contract for how those values enter applicable
  gates;
- rejected or deliberately local findings have stage files, but no uniform receipt and
  revisit state;
- sensitive operational knowledge may correctly remain outside repository knowledge,
  provided its local owner and non-promotion reason are recorded without exposing the
  secret.

These are missing interfaces/states, not evidence that another generic capture template
is needed. The existing artifacts can represent the content, but discoverability,
precedence, and closure are incomplete. The converse attack also matters: routing every
observation through a seven-state lifecycle would create bureaucracy and overload.

The state machine therefore needs an entry predicate. Route a signal when at least one
is true:

- it changed a decision or verdict;
- recurrence could cause material loss;
- it contradicts current project knowledge or an authoritative source;
- it affects more than the originating task;
- it defines an owner, interface, environment, or evidence boundary needed later.

Ephemeral task detail can remain in the task trace. A rejection receipt prevents repeated
research without promoting the rejected claim.

**Challenge verdict H3:** **supported as the dominant failure mechanism, not as an
absolute topology claim.** Artifact types appear sufficient; registered routing
interfaces, receipts, discovery, and freshness need work.

### C4. K3 survives; K4 is premature

The kernel candidates fail in sequence:

- **K1 lifecycle-only** cannot explain why TFW-2.100's completion report, HD-26's mocked
  green test, or AFD-36's stale XML evidence should be rejected.
- **K2 evidence kernel** can correct a verdict but does not ensure the correction becomes
  portable project knowledge or records a justified rejection.
- **K3 learning kernel** includes purpose, lifecycle, role separation, evidence
  precedence, independent review, and verified learning closure. It covers the observed
  cross-project mechanisms without requiring an untested research strategy catalog.
- **K4 adaptive kernel** adds inquiry-strategy selection before H4 has causal evidence
  and risks making a useful optional policy an invariant.

K3 survives with one coordinator correction: the kernel must carry product purpose into
every stage and require applicable Project Values to be loaded and defended. The actual
project-specific values, domain sources, and extra gates remain owned by the registered
project layer. Otherwise “generic kernel” would silently erase the very value context it
exists to protect.

### C5. Verification is not validation, and local phases do not prove the seam

The proposed “visible value in every phase” rule breaks on honest enabling work. Consider
a non-code public-benefit intake service:

1. Phase A defines eligibility taxonomy and staff language.
2. Phase B defines routing and escalation.
3. Phase C defines outcome reporting.

Each phase can be inspected and approved without a resident completing the service.
Forcing a visible resident outcome in Phase A would create a demonstration using
placeholder routing/reporting. Deferring all validation until the end, however, can
allow incompatible categories and handoffs to harden.

The surviving configuration uses:

- local verification for each phase's specified product;
- a value-debt entry for an indivisible outcome, with owner and due validation event;
- seam tests whenever producer/consumer contracts meet;
- one end-to-end validation in which an eligible resident enters, is correctly routed,
  staff can act, and the result is visible to the stakeholder.

NASA's Systems Engineering Handbook defines verification as establishing compliance
with requirements and validation as establishing that the system meets customer
expectations. NASA's process requirements also call for interface origin, destination,
stimulus, characteristics, horizontal traceability, and validation with parties on both
sides. This external non-TFW model independently supports the distinction exposed by
HD-23 and HD-30.

The grouped review must not become an oversized catch-all. It owns only purpose,
cross-phase interfaces, unresolved value debt, and the final proof; local reviewers keep
phase-specific evidence.

### C6. Project extensions can become a shadow fork

A registered extension layer fails if:

- agents do not discover or load it at the applicable step;
- it silently weakens a kernel invariant;
- two extensions claim precedence;
- it copies framework text and drifts;
- adapters implement different interpretations;
- historical tasks are rewritten during migration.

The minimum surviving precedence is:

1. kernel safety/role/evidence/approval invariants;
2. project purpose and applicable required Project Values/gates;
3. project configuration and advisory heuristics;
4. task-specific approved decisions.

An extension may strengthen an invariant or add domain evidence. Weakening an invariant
requires the same explicit owner authority and justification that the kernel declares;
it cannot occur through a lower-precedence config value.

Migration remains tractable only if active workflows/templates and adapters move
forward, historical traces remain immutable, generated derivatives expose their source
version, and unknown extensions fail visibly rather than disappearing. Upstream-core
edits for one project and repository forks are eliminated except where the extension
contract cannot express a genuinely new framework capability.

### C7. The original H4 protocol tested operational prompting, not matching

The current deep TFW baseline is already contaminated for the proposed
Evidence-Challenge treatment:

- `base.md` requires OODA, an external source, a tested hypothesis, and a stage
  sufficiency decision;
- `deep.md` requires counter-evidence, at least two decisions, and a metacognitive
  checkpoint.

Adding “map evidence, seek a counterexample, state reversal conditions” tests an
increment to an existing strategy, not a neutral strategy treatment.

There is also credible counter-evidence to requiring a matched strategy. A simple
[zero-shot chain-of-thought cue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11916) improved diverse
reasoning tasks, suggesting some generic prompts transfer. Conversely,
[least-to-most prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10625) produced a very large gain
on compositional SCAN but only a small aggregate gain over chain-of-thought on GSM8K,
and [multi-prompt experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11980) found that prompt
effects vary unpredictably by task and model. Matching is plausible, not established.

#### Repaired Iteration 2 design

**Neutral execution environment**

All main conditions run in a neutral workspace with no repository `AGENTS.md`, TFW
skill, workflow, personal memory, or inherited task history. The common task pack is
identical. Each model/surface/system-instruction/tool combination is a **profile
package**, not a pure capability level.

**Baseline audit**

- **B0 — minimal neutral:** common task, sources, deliverable, and tool/time budget only.
- **BT — current TFW bundle:** B0 plus the exact operative text of current research
  `base.md` and `deep.md`, hashed and injected on the same neutral surface.

BT is diagnostic, not the main causal baseline: it differs from B0 in length and several
behaviors. If an operational suffix helps B0 but not BT, the conclusion is that current
TFW already carries the relevant behavior, not that the behavior has no effect.

**Two strategies and crossover**

| Strategy | Operational core | Hypothesized matched family |
|----------|------------------|-----------------------------|
| S-E Evidence-Challenge | Enumerate claims; map them to source/observation; seek contradicting evidence; state the strongest rival; record reversal conditions; separate observation/inference/decision | F-E evidence adjudication with agreeing documents but a contradictory source, implementation, command, or environment |
| S-D Dependency-Decomposition | Define the end goal/dependencies; split into solvable subproblems; solve prerequisites first; integrate outputs; test interfaces; record the assumption that would invalidate composition | F-D compositional planning with dependent components, order constraints, and interface failures |

Each family receives its matched and mismatched operational strategy. The primary
matching estimand is the crossover:

`Δmatch = ((S-E − S-D) in F-E + (S-D − S-E) in F-D) / 2`

This can distinguish “one good generic checklist” from a strategy×inquiry interaction.

**Seven main conditions**

| Condition | Suffix behavior |
|-----------|-----------------|
| B0 | No suffix |
| BT | Exact current TFW common research bundle |
| C | Opaque strategy label, matched in syntax and tokenizer count to N (for example, `Use the Amber-Compass strategy.`) |
| N | Matched real strategy label only (`Use the Evidence-Challenge strategy.` or `Use the Dependency-Decomposition strategy.`) |
| O-M | Matched real label plus its six operational actions |
| O-X | Mismatched real label plus the other strategy's six operational actions |
| L | Opaque label plus six formatting/organization actions, matched to O-M/O-X in list structure, imperative count, placement, and tokenizer count |

O-M and N share the same real strategy label. C controls the added instruction,
salience, syntax, and approximate label shape. N versus C estimates the semantic
label-instruction effect; it is not described as “pure prestige” if lexical meaning
itself induces behavior.

O-M, O-X, and L are tokenized separately for every profile package. Exact variants are
frozen after tokenizer matching, and a structural diff records tokens, clauses,
imperatives, list shape, position, and semantic category. Token equality alone is not
accepted as equivalence.

**Cases, experimental unit, and replication**

- The experimental unit is one output from one fresh isolated
  case×profile-package×condition run.
- Case is a complete block: all approved conditions run against the same immutable case
  pack, but never in the same conversational context.
- A calibration pilot uses six independent cases in F-E and six in F-D, the four focal
  conditions B0/O-M/O-X/L, and both profile packages: 96 outputs.
- O-M and O-X receive a second fresh run for two preselected cases per
  family×profile-package: 16 additional outputs.
- The 112 pilot outputs are excluded from the main result. They estimate case,
  profile-package, and repeat variance for design simulation; they do not test H4.
- The earlier 220-output layout (168 primary, 24 repeats, 28 pilot) is retained only as
  an **illustrative full design**, not approved scope. The main case count is not frozen
  until the pilot and inferential-method gate below pass.

The pilot itself requires explicit owner authorization. If its variance components are
too unstable to support design simulation, stop rather than treating resampling volume
as new independent evidence.

**Strategy-neutral primary outcome**

The Extract rubric favored S-E because it directly rewarded counter-evidence and
claim-to-source behavior. It remains diagnostic, not primary. Before any condition is
run, a treatment-blind case author defines 8–12 required outcome items for each case.
Each item is an externally checkable fact, constraint, decision, or completed result,
not a preferred reasoning step.

`Outcome score = 4 × correctly satisfied required items / total required items`

- F-E items cover the correct verdict, decisive evidence, material contradictions, and
  required decision scope without prescribing how the model reasons.
- F-D items cover the correct final plan/result, required components, valid
  dependencies, interface behavior, and end-goal completion without requiring a named
  decomposition format.
- All items have equal weight within a case. Case scores have equal weight in the
  analysis. The item key and scoring rules are frozen before prompt variants are
  exposed to scorers.

Secondary diagnostics are reported separately:

| Family | Predeclared 0–4 diagnostics |
|--------|------------------------------|
| F-E | Evidence accuracy/coverage; contradiction detection; claim-to-source traceability; rival/reversal handling |
| F-D | Decomposition correctness; dependency ordering; interface integration; end-goal completeness |
| Common | Decision usefulness; calibrated uncertainty; unsupported-claim rate; output tokens/time/cost |

The family diagnostics are not combined into the primary cross-family score unless a
normalization rule is frozen and simulation-validated before the main run.

**Family-typed hard failures**

| Family | Hard failures that remain separate from all scores |
|--------|----------------------------------------------------|
| F-E | Invented source or observation; missed planted material contradiction; false evidence-authority ordering; command/environment success claimed without successful evidence |
| F-D | Missing or invalid dependency; impossible ordering; broken interface integration; incomplete end-goal delivery represented as complete |
| Both | Wrong task/source hash; material fabricated fact; unmarked absence of required evidence represented as certainty |

**Cost and feasibility gate**

The illustrative 220-output design assumes 15k–30k input-equivalent tokens, 2k–6k
output tokens, and 10–25 wall minutes per isolated run. Its order-of-magnitude cost is:

- 3.3M–6.6M input tokens and 0.44M–1.32M output tokens;
- 37–92 serial execution hours;
- two condition-masked scorers at 6–10 minutes/output: 44–73 scoring hours before
  reconciliation, plus approximately 20% for disagreements;
- case construction, prompt tokenization, source-pack hashing, and analysis are extra.

The model/API charge is calculated at authorization time as
`input tokens × selected-profile input rate + output tokens × selected-profile output rate`.
No current price, task credit, or human/agent scoring budget has been approved. The owner
must approve a concrete token/time/currency-or-credit/scoring estimate before the pilot
and again before the main run.

**Inferential-method gate**

- Minimum meaningful primary effect: 0.40 outcome-score points.
- Confirmatory contrasts: O-M−B0, O-M−O-X, O-M−L, and O-M−N.
- Holm adjustment applies across all four confirmatory contrasts.
- Candidate analyses are (a) a complete-block mixed model with family,
  profile-package, condition, and declared interactions plus a case random intercept,
  using a small-sample correction, or (b) case-level paired randomization inference.
- Before freezing the main run, simulate both candidates across the pilot's plausible
  between-case, within-case, profile, and missingness ranges. The chosen method must
  demonstrate 93%–97% coverage for a nominal 95% interval and at least 80% power for a
  0.40 effect under the declared main design.
- If neither method passes, increase the number of **independent cases** with owner
  approval or stop. Increasing bootstrap/permutation draws does not repair too few
  independent blocks.
- Freeze method, main case count, interactions, missing-data handling, interval
  construction, and multiplicity treatment before any main output is unmasked.
- Report raw outcomes, diagnostics, family/profile strata, output tokens,
  latency/cost, missingness, typed hard failures, and all prompt/source hashes.

This supports only the frozen task population. A generalized claim requires a separately
justified case-sampling frame, consistent with NIST AI 800-3.

**Missing, rerun, and blinding policy**

- Rerun only a predeclared protocol failure: no output due to infrastructure, unavailable
  required tool, wrong prompt/source hash, or context contamination.
- Do not rerun refusal, weak reasoning, missed evidence, tool misuse, or a bad verdict;
  those are scored outcomes.
- Preserve every failed run and link replacement/reason. Do not impute a content-level
  failure. Report cost-stopped missing blocks.
- Two scorers grade masked, randomized outputs independently before reconciliation.
- After scoring, each scorer guesses one of the seven conditions and gives confidence.
  Report guess accuracy against chance and agreement. Because operational structure can
  unblind the output, call this **condition-masked**, not fully blinded.

**Stopping and interpretation**

- No favorable early stop. Complete the owner-approved frozen sample or report the
  cost/protocol stop.
- H4's **matched-strategy** mechanism is supported on the frozen pack only if
  O-M−B0, O-M−O-X, O-M−L, and O-M−N each have primary-outcome point estimate ≥0.40
  and Holm-adjusted lower interval >0, the crossover direction holds in both F-E and
  F-D, and O-M does not increase the relevant family-typed hard failures.
- If O-M beats B0/L/N but not O-X, support only a generic operational-checklist effect.
- If O-M beats B0 but not L, structure/volume is sufficient.
- If N beats C but O-M does not beat N, the semantic label has an effect and the
  operational actions add no demonstrated value beyond it.
- If the result holds in one profile package or family only, scope the claim to that
  stratum.
- Mixed direction, inadequate power, high scorer disagreement, material unmasking, or a
  stopped sample is inconclusive.

[CheckList behavioral testing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.04118) motivates constructing
case families as a capability×test-type matrix rather than trusting one aggregate score.
It found actionable failures in already-tested systems; for TFW-48, planted
contradictions, interface cases, and invariance cases serve the same diagnostic role.

**Challenge verdict H4:** **unresolved/inconclusive in Iteration 1.** The repaired
protocol restores the neutral contrast and removes rubric home-field advantage, but it
is not execution-ready until its independent-case design, inference coverage, power,
and full cost pass explicit owner gates in Iteration 2.

## Challenge Decisions

1. **C-D1 — H1 scope:** retain full local imperatives for role, safety, destructive, and
   otherwise irreversible authority boundaries; use canonical owner plus observable
   localized enforcement elsewhere.
2. **C-D2 — H2 semantics:** preserve hard numbers only as typed invariants/boundaries;
   preserve other useful numbers as configurable warnings, triggers, defaults, or
   measurements with explicit override. Keep every current numeric value unresolved;
   source sufficiency depends on claim type and authority.
3. **C-D3 — H3 entry gate:** route durable, contradictory, cross-task, high-risk, or
   authority/interface evidence; leave ephemeral detail in task traces and record
   rejection/local-retention reasons.
4. **C-D4 — kernel survivor:** K3 is the strongest Iteration 1 kernel candidate. K4's
   strategy-selection machinery remains outside the kernel pending H4.
5. **C-D5 — purpose ownership:** the kernel carries purpose and requires applicable
   Project Values; registered project extensions own the actual values and domain
   evidence.
6. **C-D6 — value/seam proof:** allow indivisible enabling phases only with value debt,
   seam ownership, and a due end-to-end validation; do not require artificial visible
   demos.
7. **C-D7 — H4 repair:** use neutral and current-TFW baselines, two-strategy crossover,
   matched label/length/structure controls, a strategy-neutral primary outcome,
   family-typed hard failures, four multiplicity-controlled confirmatory contrasts,
   condition-masking audit, inference-coverage validation, and explicit cost/power
   stops.

## Metacognitive Check

- **What was eliminated despite initial appeal?** “Thin visible value every phase” was
  too absolute; it can create demo proxies. A fixed strategy catalog was also premature.
- **What survived despite initial suspicion?** Selective repetition and numeric budgets
  survived when tied to consequence and treated as gates/signals rather than evidence of
  completion.
- **What evidence remains failure-selected?** All production anchors remain unusually
  rich in discovered defects. Challenge can establish necessity and counterexamples,
  not routine overhead or prevalence.
- **Where could the trial still mislead?** Strategy labels have semantic content,
  operational outputs can unmask themselves, neutral workspaces differ from production
  TFW, and two families cannot support universal matching.
- **What did the cost attack change?** A nominal 220-output design implies millions of
  tokens and roughly tens of execution and scoring hours. It is a feasibility proposal,
  not approved evidence scope; even the pilot requires an owner gate.
- **What would reverse K3?** Evidence that learning closure belongs outside the method
  kernel without recurring loss, or that its routing cost exceeds the documented
  recurrence cost across routine cases.
- **What did external evidence add?** NASA independently separated verification from
  stakeholder validation and made interfaces explicit; prompt studies supplied both a
  generic-strategy counterexample and task-dependent effects; behavioral testing
  supplied a diagnostic case-matrix model.
