> Goal: Re-derive TFW from its product purpose and production learning so that its compact method kernel preserves meaning, evidence, independent judgment, and portable knowledge across domains.

## Research Plan

### Gather

- Map TFW values and success criteria to retained mechanisms, their canonical owners, point-of-use enforcement, and observed production outcomes.
- Inventory fixed numeric limits, instruction repetitions, references, workflow branches, Fact Candidate routes, and project-local `.tfw/` modifications.
- Sample Atamat, Helpdesk, and AFD traces across model-capability eras at the purpose→HL, HL→TS, TS→execution, RF→review, evidence→verdict, and finding→knowledge boundaries.
- Compare repository-native knowledge with available personal Claude memory, treating memory as discovery input and repository traces or observed reality as verification authority.
- Run matched inquiry tasks under neutral-base, strategy-name-only, and operational-strategy conditions; gather external research on instruction locality, cognitive strategy effects, case-study inquiry, and model-evaluation confounds.

Expected decision factors to decompose include rule authority, semantic owner, point-of-use locality, model-era sensitivity, failure prevented, override semantics, evidence topology, and domain transferability.

### Extract

- Derive candidate method-kernel configurations linking purpose, lifecycle, roles, authorities, evidence, review, and learning.
- Cross-reference instruction locality with rule criticality and model-era evidence to test H1 without assuming that either duplication or references are universally superior.
- Classify each numeric constraint by authority, observed effect, ownership, and stop/continue signal to test H2.
- Model the learning loop from signal through verification, promotion or rejection, and pruning; locate the actual loss points to test H3.
- Separate research intent, operational inquiry strategy, intensity, and optional techniques; compare minimum extension contracts and selection mechanisms to test H4.

### Challenge

- Seek counterexamples where duplicated inline instruction prevented a real failure, where a fixed limit protected scope or attention, or where a missing capture destination—not routing quality—caused knowledge loss.
- Red-team apparent model-era improvements against task mix, project maturity, prompt length, evaluator expectation, and survivorship bias.
- Distinguish operational strategy effects from prestigious-name priming and additional prompt volume; test whether a stronger neutral workflow explains the same gains.
- Replay proposed removals and authority changes against documented Atamat, Helpdesk, and AFD failures plus a non-code product scenario.
- Challenge migration cost, weaker-agent compatibility, project-extension boundaries, reviewer authority, and cargo-cult risks before recommending a kernel.
