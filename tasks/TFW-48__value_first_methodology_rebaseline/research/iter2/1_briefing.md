## Research Plan

### Gather

- Define auditable success, routine, and low-risk selection criteria; sample software and non-code traces without treating absence of recorded failure as proof of low risk.
- Inventory representative rule deployments across semantic-owner, full-local, cue-plus-observation, and reference-only patterns; record risk, reversibility, profile assumptions, evidence of use, and maintenance cost.
- Build an individual threshold ledger for the approved numeric scope, including semantic type, owner, protected failure, counting rule, breach behavior, exceptions, observed use, and threshold-specific evidence.
- Collect routine-case evidence for K3 obligations, registered project extensions, learning dispositions/receipts, and local-versus-seam/value review across software and non-code work.
- Specify H4 feasibility inputs without executing comparisons: treatment cells, independent cases, output count, model/token assumptions, scoring and reconciliation effort, contamination controls, stopping rules, and owner decision gates.

Expected dimensions include task risk and reversibility, domain, rule class and locality, model/profile dependence, limit semantics, lifecycle frequency, evidence observability, authority/precedence, maintenance cost, reviewer span, and inference strength.

### Extract

- Build a rule-locality configuration space and identify the minimum local enforcement justified for each tested rule/risk class, including constrained-profile fallbacks.
- Produce threshold-by-threshold dispositions—retain, change, make adaptive, move to project calibration, convert to a warning/escalation signal, or retire—while separating evidence about a mechanism from evidence about its current numeric value.
- Derive minimum viable K3, registered-extension, and learning-receipt contracts with explicit entry predicates so routine work does not inherit unnecessary ceremony.
- Compare local verification, narrow seam/value review, and end-to-end validation schedules across the software and non-code cases; identify where grouped review reduces cost without creating blind seams.
- Turn the H4 proposal into costed owner options with assumptions, inferential limits, prerequisites, stop/go criteria, and an explicit “do not run” state until separate authorization.

### Challenge

- Seek successful routine counterexamples where K3, receipts, extension registration, or seam/value review adds no decision value, creates false positives, or costs more than the failure it prevents.
- Test every locality and threshold recommendation against weaker/constrained profiles, irreversible actions, ambiguous counting rules, Goodhart effects, and cases where a hard boundary or fuller local instruction demonstrably helped.
- Red-team the extension and learning contracts for duplicate authority, stale generated derivatives, hidden local policy, circular receipts, filler candidates, upgrade conflict, and non-code mismatch.
- Pairwise-challenge review configurations for missed adjacent seams, reviewer scope creep, repeated end-to-end cost, deferred value debt, and stakeholder-validation gaps.
- Red-team H4 feasibility for treatment contamination, non-independent cases, prompt-length confounds, scoring leakage, evaluator reliability, model/surface drift, underpowered inference, and disproportionate cost; do not execute a trial.
