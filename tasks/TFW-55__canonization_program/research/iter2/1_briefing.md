## Research Plan

### Gather

- Build a primary/official adjacent-practice comparison for decision/provenance records, docs-as-code, knowledge externalization and distributed cognition, human–AI responsibility, methodology/reference-framework boundaries, authority/version models, and progressive adoption. Compare objects governed, minimum required composition, exclusions, authority/update rules, and claim limits rather than collecting borrowed terminology.
- Preserve C1/C3/C4/C9 unchanged and unranked at entry. Gather evidence both for a reusable discipline/methodology and for the strongest framework-only explanation; component overlap is not by itself a verdict on operational composition.
- Define matched test fixtures before critique: D9 one-common-core vs separate-parity vs task-traces-first with C3 or C4 held constant; `self-aware` vs non-anthropomorphic wording with identical capabilities; and Light → Assisted → Full vs direct-to-method/specification vs role/risk branching with identical content budgets.
- Construct four authority scenarios against the same corpus/essay/spec contract: a direct contradiction, a semantic update, a Russian derivative, and an external-author citation. The expected answer must come from explicit source status/precedence, not founder interpretation.
- Select representative Light, Assisted, Full, and non-code traces to attack the candidate semantic floor while keeping evidence, review, and verified knowledge separate risk-scaled layers.

### Extract

- Reuse and deconfound Iteration-1 dimensions: D1 category, D2 authority, D3 trace floor, D4 terminology/capability, D7/D8 exposition and Editions routing, and D9 human/agent consumption. Promote a new factor only if the external evidence introduces an independently variable decision.
- Normalize adjacent sources into a comparison matrix covering purpose, mandatory elements, exclusions, human responsibility, provenance/continuity payload, current-authority selector, update model, and whether the practice claims a method, methodology, framework, standard, or vocabulary.
- Build matched configurations instead of comparing packages with multiple simultaneous changes. Score immediate definition, exclusion, capability, precedence, rule, load, and drift accuracy separately; keep preference and durable-learning claims outside the score.
- Map each result to C1/C3/C4/C9, H1–H4, and pending A1–A3 without treating frozen-HL compatibility as evidence of viability.

### Challenge

- Apply one predeclared falsification rule set to C1/C3/C4/C9. The candidate loss rule is that a higher category must state distinctive exclusions and minimum composition and outperform the full-strength C4 explanation without depending on TFW artifact names or owner intent; its adoption is pending Guiding Question 1.
- Attack the layered authority survivor with all four scenarios; distinguish a content defect repairable inside the existing essay/spec contract from evidence that a new authority surface is necessary.
- Red-team matched D9, terminology, and bounded exposition results for hidden content/load differences, answer-key leakage, evaluator preference, and founder-as-oracle assumptions.
- Attempt to falsify the minimum selected trace across all Editions and non-code work; reject both provenance-only underspecification and Full-assurance universalization.
- Produce final or explicitly unresolved H1–H4 statuses, C1/C3/C4/C9 dispositions, mature/immature A1–A3 classification, and the exact reason for a third iteration only if H1 or H2 remains genuinely undecidable.

## Scope Intent

- **In scope:** primary/official adjacent sources; explicit category/exclusion/composition tests; matched D9, terminology, and exposition variants; independent bounded cold-reader and agent-orientation critiques; authority contradiction/update/derivative/citation scenarios; cross-Edition and non-code trace-floor comparison; final H1–H4 and A1–A3 classification.
- **Out of scope:** editing frozen or free HL sections, §12, READMEs, specification, Editions, TS, Task Board, or `iterations.yaml`; recruiting the founder as an answer key or conducting a broad speculative interview; claiming durable learning efficacy, retention, transfer, instructor independence, or causal superiority; legal, market, certification, product, BoK, governance, brand-redesign, or implementation work; treating source prestige, participant preference, or component novelty as a category decision.

## Guiding Questions

1. For the H2 loss rule, should C1/C3/C9 lose to C4 unless they identify at least one defensible exclusion and minimum composition beyond adjacent practices **and** independent readers can distinguish that higher category without TFW artifact names or founder intent?
2. For the focused D9 test, should C4 be held constant as the primary low-assumption control, with C3 used only as a sensitivity check if the D9 result depends on category wording?
3. May “independent” bounded critiques be run in fresh isolated Codex tasks using fixed source packets and rubrics, with no project/thread history and no human recruitment, measuring only immediate definition, capability, precedence, rule, load, and drift accuracy?
