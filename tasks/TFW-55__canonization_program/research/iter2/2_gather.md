# Gather — "What do we NOT know?"
> **Mindset:** Explorer. You're mapping unknown territory. Widen before you narrow. Every assumption is a question.
> **Test:** "Can I name every dimension and its alternatives without checking my sources?"
> Parent: [HL-TFW-55](../../HL-TFW-55__canonization_program.md)
> Goal: Map external boundary evidence and matched-test alternatives for TFW's category, authority, trace floor, terminology, exposition, and human/agent consumption without selecting a winner.

## Findings

### G1 — Adjacent primary/official controls map overlapping parts, not a ready-made TFW equivalent

| Adjacent source | Object and required composition | Boundary or exclusion | What it controls in TFW-55 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Michael Nygard, [“Documenting Architecture Decisions”](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions) | One architecturally significant decision recorded as Title, Context, Decision, Status, and Consequences; superseded records are retained | Deliberately scoped to architecture decisions, not an end-to-end work-continuity contract | Refutes novelty of short durable decision records and retained superseded history; leaves purpose/delegation/result/continuation and current semantic authority open |
| Write the Docs, [“Docs as Code”](https://www.writethedocs.org/guide/docs-as-code/) | Documentation uses familiar software-development tools and workflows: plain text, version control, issue tracking, review, and automation | A tooling/process philosophy; it does not by itself define a semantic trace floor or choose current official meaning among conflicting claims | Refutes novelty of repository-native prose, Git, review, and automated documentation checks |
| W3C, [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) | Interchange vocabulary for Entity, Activity, Agent, derivation, attribution, delegation, revision, and related provenance relations | An ontology for provenance representation, not a general work method and not a purpose/acceptance/continuation rule | Refutes novelty of provenance and attribution components; tests whether provenance-only D3-A is too weak |
| NIST, [AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) | Risk-scoped Govern/Map/Measure/Manage functions with documented roles, responsibility, oversight, monitoring, and risk responses | AI-risk management scope; it does not prescribe a general durable handoff trace for arbitrary delegated work | Refutes novelty of human oversight, differentiated roles, go/no-go and deactivation controls; tests whether TFW adds a narrower continuity composition rather than relabeling governance |
| Scrum Guides, [The Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html) | A purposefully incomplete framework whose essential elements form a defined whole; other practices may be added | Omitting core elements means the result is not Scrum | Critical counterexample to category-by-label reasoning: a framework can have mandatory composition, explicit boundary, and neutral application rules without unique components or a “higher” category |
| Daniele Procida, [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) | Systematic documentation architecture organized around tutorial, how-to, reference, and explanation needs | Defines content-purpose distinctions while leaving implementation open | Supports role/need-specific human surfaces; does not establish D9 semantic parity or agent consumption by itself |
| Ikujiro Nonaka, [“A Dynamic Theory of Organizational Knowledge Creation”](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.5.1.14) | Organizational knowledge grows through interaction between tacit and explicit knowledge and amplification beyond an individual | Theory of organizational knowledge creation, not a trace workflow, authority selector, or acceptance contract | Supports the broad externalization/compounding rationale while preventing it from being claimed as a uniquely TFW mechanism |
| Edwin Hutchins, [“How a Cockpit Remembers Its Speeds”](https://pages.ucsd.edu/~c8johnson/COGS102B/Hutchins95.pdf) | Cognitive/memory work can be distributed across people and representational devices | Descriptive distributed-cognition analysis, not a normative project workflow | Supports the possibility that a project/crew can carry memory beyond one mind; does not establish TFW's category or mechanics |

The controls weaken novelty claims for every individual component. They do **not** yet show whether TFW's mandatory operational composition is distinguishable, useful, or best named a framework, methodology, or discipline. Scrum makes the converse equally important: a defensible boundary does not automatically justify a category above “framework.”

### G2 — Candidate H2 boundary is compositional and consequential, but still provisional

The strongest non-circular candidate found in this loop is not a list of TFW filenames. It is a work contract in which all of the following are required:

1. An explicit human purpose, current context, intended result, and acceptance/stop responsibility.
2. A bounded AI delegation: task, role, constraints, and the limit of the agent's authority are visible.
3. A selected durable trace records material sources/findings, decisions and rejected alternatives, the result, and current state; raw chat and hidden model reasoning are not the default trace.
4. The trace identifies the authoritative result and enough continuation state for another human or agent to resume without reconstructing the conversation.
5. Evidence, independent review, and verified project knowledge remain distinct layers that scale with risk; they are not silently collapsed into the minimum trace or universally required at Full strength.

This is a **candidate boundary for Extract and Challenge**, not a Gather decision. It must be applied to new cases without TFW artifact names or founder intent:

| Neutral case | Provisional boundary result | Consequence / when to apply |
|---|---|---|
| Repository has ADRs, Git history, and docs CI, but no current delegated-work boundary, acceptance owner, result, or continuation state | **Not TFW** on those facts | Keep the adjacent practices; add TFW only when AI-delegated work must remain governable and resumable across sessions/people |
| Research assignment states purpose and human acceptance, bounds an AI role, records sources and rejected interpretations, preserves the verified result/current state, and names the next action | **TFW candidate**, even with ordinary filenames and no code | Use a proportional Light-like trace; a software repository or TFW artifact names are not required |
| Complete provenance graph identifies entities, activities, agents, and derivations but omits why the work exists, who accepts/stops it, and how to continue | **Not TFW** | Provenance can be a layer inside the trace but is insufficient alone |
| AI risk register defines roles, monitoring, and deactivation but contains no selected task/result/decision/continuation trace | **Not TFW** | Governance remains useful and combinable; TFW is relevant only when durable delegated-work continuity is also needed |
| Scrum team uses all Scrum elements but material AI-delegated decisions and continuation state disappear into chat | **Not automatically TFW** | The methods can coexist; neither label entails the other |
| Four-file manual project records purpose, bounded AI work, sources, decisions, result, current state, and next action | **TFW candidate** | Tests the non-code and low-machinery floor; assurance grows only if risk demands it |
| Raw transcript is archived with timestamps but no selected decisions, authority, result, or continuation summary | **Not TFW** | Treat the transcript as possible source evidence, not as the useful trace itself |

The loss rule remains exactly the coordinator's rule: C1/C3/C9 lose to C4 as the **primary category** unless this kind of minimum composition plus explicit exclusions survives and blinded independent readers consistently apply it. Readers are not asked to guess “discipline,” “methodology,” or any founder-preferred term. Component uniqueness is not required, and even a successful boundary does not by itself ontologically prove a new category.

### G3 — The authority survivor yields four testable expected resolutions

These fixtures hold the corpus/essay/spec architecture constant and make the claim domain explicit. A failure is not “the reader chose a different prose style”; it is an inability to reach the declared current answer from source status and precedence.

| Fixture | Frozen facts | Expected resolution under the Iteration-1 survivor | Failure signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contradiction** | Canonical essay says a trace is selected continuity evidence and excludes raw transcript-by-default; current mechanics accept a transcript alone as complete | Essay selects stable meaning; specification/mechanic is defective and must be corrected; corpus preserves the conflict and repair | Reader treats newest file/history/founder statement as automatic semantic authority or cannot identify the defect |
| **Semantic update** | An approved change narrows the stable definition of trace | Update the essay for meaning and the specification only where mechanics change; retain earlier corpus versions as history/evidence | Old and new statements remain equally official, or history is overwritten to manufacture consistency |
| **Russian derivative** | Fixture status declares the English essay canonical and the Russian text a versioned derivative | Resolve semantic mismatch to the declared English source until the derivative is corrected; do not ask the founder | Language, recency, or owner authorship silently overrides declared source status |
| **External-author citation** | Author needs to support a stable meaning claim, a current-mechanics claim, and a historical-evidence claim | Cite the versioned essay, current versioned specification, and dated corpus artifact/commit respectively | One undifferentiated “repository” citation cannot identify which claim is current or historical |

These are tests of H1's selector contract, not evidence that the contract already works for outsiders. D2 alternatives remain open until the fixtures are instantiated and scored.

### G4 — Cross-Edition inspection narrows the semantic floor and exposes a missing real non-code sample

- [Light README](../../../../editions/01-light/README.md), [Light agent rules](../../../../editions/01-light/AGENTS.md), [Light task board](../../../../editions/01-light/TASKS.md), and [Light project memory](../../../../editions/01-light/memory/PROJECT.md) use four initial files plus a per-task `TRACE.md`. The task trace carries goal/readiness, inputs/sources, findings, decisions/assumptions, verification, results, and next step. This is materially richer than provenance-only but does not require Full's complete assurance chain.
- [Assisted README](../../../../editions/02-assisted/README.md), [Assisted project contract](../../../../editions/02-assisted/PROJECT.md), and [Assisted agent rules](../../../../editions/02-assisted/AGENTS.md) retain the primary task trace while adding status-by-folder, a plan-before-work rule, participant attribution, risk checks, and controlled knowledge candidates. [Assisted knowledge](../../../../editions/02-assisted/knowledge/INDEX.md) explicitly keeps the task trace as primary and does not auto-promote candidates; [migration](../../../../editions/02-assisted/MIGRATION.md) preserves the original trace and maps old to new paths. These are evidence/authority safeguards above the Light floor, not proof that all work requires them.
- Full TFW adds formal HL/TS/ONB/RF/REVIEW and knowledge gates. Iteration 1 already showed that universalizing that chain would overfit Full and contradict proportional Editions.
- The repository contains completed Full research/development traces and Edition contracts, but no completed Light/Assisted trace for a genuinely non-code domain was found in the focused file scan. The Light README's claim that the method applies to documents, finance, education, and research is therefore a design claim, not demonstrated cross-domain evidence. Extract must freeze neutral synthetic non-code cases (research synthesis, policy memo, and financial comparison are viable alternatives) rather than invent a success example.

The emerging D3 test is therefore two-sided: provenance-only must fail if it cannot preserve purpose/decision/continuation, while universal Full assurance must fail if a neutral low-risk non-code case cannot be valid with the selected continuity trace alone.

### G5 — Matched critique fixtures are preregistered; no model critique has run in Gather

The independent critiques will be **model-based bounded critiques**, never human cold-reader evidence. No isolated Codex task was run during Gather because the exact packets, answer keys, rubrics, labels, and order must first be instantiated and frozen.

| Fixture family | Held constant | Only manipulated variable | Primary measures | Claim limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D9 A/B/C** | C4 definition and category wording, semantic propositions, source authority, neutral task, total content budget, questions, answer key, rubric | Consumption contract: one common core vs separate semantic-parity surfaces vs task-traces-first/essay-optional | Immediate definition, capability, precedence, rule accuracy; objective load and semantic drift | C3 rerun only if a C4 result plausibly depends on framework-level wording; no human-comprehension or preference claim |
| **Terminology pair** | Six operational capabilities, non-sentience boundary, prose length, source status, task, and rubric | “Self-aware project” vs a non-anthropomorphic label; labels blinded | Immediate definition/capability errors and model-induced anthropomorphic/category ambiguity | Cannot settle human reception, memorability, or A2 if A2 depends on human reaction |
| **Exposition variants** | Same semantic units, content budget, authority declarations, neutral cases, questions, answer key, and rubric | Ordering/routing: Editions bridge vs direct-to-method/spec vs role/risk branch | Immediate definition, exclusion, precedence, rule accuracy, input load, drift | Cannot demonstrate durable learning, transfer, retention, or causal superiority |
| **Category boundary** | Same neutral cases, no TFW filenames, no founder intent, randomized labels/order | C1/C3/C9 boundary statement vs full C4 explanation/control | Consistent `TFW / not TFW / when apply` classification plus stated reason | Tests operational applicability of the boundary, not ontology or label guessing |

Before any run, Extract must record and freeze the exact packet and source versions, prompt, answer key, rubric, randomization seed/order, and variant labels; a content hash is the preferred integrity check. Every run must start in fresh isolated context with no project/task history. Prompts, packets, raw outputs, scores, and evaluator reasoning must remain traceable under one evaluation procedure.

Proposed common scoring atoms for Extract are deliberately mechanical:

- `0` = missing or materially wrong; `1` = partial/ambiguous; `2` = correct and correctly applied, scored separately for definition, exclusion, capability, precedence, and rule questions.
- **Load** = packet input tokens and number of required source units, not a subjective ease rating.
- **Drift** = count of material contradictions with the frozen answer key across answers, reported separately from accuracy.
- Founder preference, label preference, style, durable learning, human reception, and “guessed the intended category” are not scores.

Primary/official adjacent-source comparison remains the independent evidence lane. Model critiques cannot replace it or convert source overlap into a novelty verdict.

### G6 — Focused OODA result

**Observe.** Adjacent sources cover every major TFW component separately: short decision records, repository-native documentation, provenance, human oversight, distributed memory, knowledge externalization, role-specific documentation, and compositional framework boundaries. Light and Assisted also demonstrate that semantic continuity and assurance depth are separable.

**Orient.** The H2 question is narrower than “are the parts new?” and stricter than “can we write a boundary?” TFW needs a non-arbitrary composition with consequential exclusions that independent readers can apply, while Scrum prevents treating such a boundary as automatic proof of a discipline/methodology. H1, D9, terminology, and exposition can be tested without changing the category at the same time.

**Decide.** Gather is sufficient for focused mode. Seven independent dimensions have at least three alternatives; C1/C3/C4/C9 remain intact; the authority and boundary fixtures are concrete; the trace-floor attack includes both underspecification and over-engineering controls; and the model protocol is preregistered without overstating its evidential reach. The lack of an actual non-code Edition completion is an explicit fixture gap, not a reason for another Gather loop because neutral cases can test the semantic rule without claiming observed adoption.

**Act.** Recommend `close stage`. Extract should normalize the eight external controls, instantiate matched configurations, freeze the exact critique artifacts before any run, and preserve separate verdicts for evidential viability and frozen-HL compatibility.
