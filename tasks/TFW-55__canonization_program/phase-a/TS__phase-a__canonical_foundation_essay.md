## 1. Objective

Rewrite `.tfw/README.md` as a coherent English Project North Star essay of at most 2,000 words. It must lead a reader from the problem of invisible, discontinuous AI-delegated work to the Philosophy of Trace, define TFW as the methodology built on that philosophy, designate citable purpose/principles/non-goals, and route readers unambiguously to current mechanics, history/evidence, and the future BoK without creating that BoK.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

### AC-1: Problem-led definition and architecture

The essay earns one functional definition through the problem before it explains machinery.

- [ ] The opening makes invisible/discontinuous AI-delegated intellectual work concrete before naming framework artifacts.
- [ ] One concise definition identifies Philosophy of Trace as foundation, TFW as methodology, and workflows/implementations as realizations.
- [ ] The repository is a self-applying reference implementation, not the only realization or proof that every historical file is normative.
- [ ] `Methodology` is presented as the owner-selected position supported by function, composition, and exclusions—not as a taxonomy or novelty proven by research.
- [ ] The essay does not begin with lifecycle stages, artifact names, installation, or code regeneration.

Gate: EV maps the opening and definition to these five units and records the exact section/anchor for each.

### AC-2: Trace and continuability

The essay distinguishes selected material continuity from chat history and from automatic truth.

- [ ] Trace is a selected, durable, inspectable material record rather than raw transcript, hidden chain-of-thought, complete memory, or guaranteed truth.
- [ ] Ordinary language covers the internal F1–F4 floor: human purpose/authority; bounded delegation; selected material trace plus result/current state; continuation or explicit close.
- [ ] Goal, Value, Question, Task, Context, and Boundaries are introduced only to the extent needed to show orientation, uncertainty, bounded work, and drift control.
- [ ] A “good Trace” enables inspection, reconstruction, comparison, and continuation; it does not promise identical deterministic reproduction.
- [ ] Evidence, review, and verified knowledge are risk-proportional assurance layers, not universal minimum artifacts.

Gate: EV maps each F1–F4/R1 semantic unit to public prose without exposing those labels in the essay.

Evidence: N/A — this is a semantic document criterion; the required EV mapping provides the reproducible inspection record.

### AC-3: Human/agent authority boundary

Delegation remains useful without transferring legitimate human responsibility to an agent.

- [ ] Human purpose, legitimate authority, judgment, acceptance, accountability, and stop responsibility are explicit.
- [ ] Agents receive bounded work with purpose/context, scope/boundaries, and acceptance authority.
- [ ] The essay does not require complete domain mastery before delegation; it requires enough understanding to define purpose, bounds, acceptance, authority, and escalation.
- [ ] Observability is necessary for inspection and correction but is not presented as a guarantee of correctness.
- [ ] Ownership of organizational memory is described contextually; employee, employer, client, privacy, security, and IP interests are not erased by an absolute slogan.

Gate: EV records the exact authority-bearing sentences and a disposition for the three narrowed source claims: delegation/understanding, observability/correctness, and memory ownership.

## 7. Technical Guidance

- Treat the Phase HL narrative map as a content skeleton, not mandatory final headings or sentence wording.
- Use the mini-essays for the human explanatory path; use F1–F4/R1 privately to test completeness, never as reader-facing jargon.
- Use `methodology` consistently with master A3. Let the function and observable composition do the explanatory work.
- Use current `.tfw/README.md` as rewrite material, not a structure to preserve. Link detailed mechanics instead of abbreviating them.
- Keep `NS1`, `NS2`, and `NS3` stable and directly linkable; headings may add ordinary-language subtitles but not change the IDs.
- Do not edit a specification file to make an essay sentence true. Qualify the prose or record a follow-up finding.
- Keep examples domain-neutral. A software example may illustrate but may not define TFW.
- Do not use long quotations from the owner corpus. Synthesize, preserve provenance in EV, and retain the owner's conceptual voice without copying a draft book into the README.

## 8. Definition of Failure

- ❌ The essay starts with Full mechanics, artifact names, or installation rather than the reader's discontinuity problem.
- ❌ TFW is called novel or categorically superior to a framework as if research established it.
- ❌ Philosophy of Trace, TFW methodology, and a concrete realization collapse into synonyms.
- ❌ Trace means transcript, hidden reasoning, provenance alone, complete memory, or deterministic reproduction.
- ❌ Human authority/accountability becomes implicit while agents are described as independent responsible actors.
- ❌ `NS1`–`NS3` are absent, decorative, uncitable, or unable to reject an excessive proposal.
- ❌ The working BoK becomes authority, a deliverable, or a public link in this phase.
- ❌ `self-aware` is anthropomorphic or Editions become a universal maturity/learning ladder.
- ❌ The essay exceeds 2,000 words, breaks local links/anchors, or requires another authority surface.
- ❌ Any framework mechanics, root README prose/translations, course/book material, or brand design is changed in this phase.
- ❌ EV cannot account for a source claim or overclaim disposition, an AC gate fails, or EV/RF statuses disagree.
