=== 10. PROBE - what does ^ actually anchor to? ===
Rule 15 states a known limit. This is the measurement behind it.
TD-137 appears in 267bd06 only as the first token of a BODY line.
PS> git log -E --oneline --grep="^TD-137"
267bd06 [claude-code/TFW-53/phase-a/executor] second pass on review findings 1-4
-> 1 commit. So ^ anchors to the start of ANY line, not to the subject.
   The anchor removes mid-line mentions (the observed f379c5e failure).
   It does NOT remove a body line that begins with a conforming prefix.
   Rule 15 ships that limit rather than implying selectivity it lacks.
