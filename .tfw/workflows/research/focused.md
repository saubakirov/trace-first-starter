# Research Intensity: Focused

> **Mindset Override:** "Scan → Assess → Report"
> Deliberately bounded investigation. Resolve the named comparison without widening
> the corpus or uncertainty beyond what can change the decision.

## When to Use
- The Comparative Decision Procedure fits
- The decision and material alternatives/relationships are bounded
- Risk is contained and a deliberately narrow declared corpus is defensible
- Important exclusions and residual uncertainty can be made explicit

## Stage Behavior
- Use only evidence families needed to support or limit the bounded decision
- Cross-check technical and factual claims according to claim risk
- Seek proportionate counter-evidence for the key assumption
- Record exclusions and stop widening when new evidence no longer changes a material disposition

## Checkpoint Criteria
- [ ] Declared evidence/corpus coverage and exclusions are visible
- [ ] The key assumption received a proportionate countercheck
- [ ] Decision effect or explicit unresolved gap is stated
- [ ] Further available evidence is not changing a material disposition
- [ ] Learning selection recorded, or **“No selected signal.”**

Focused is a qualitative [Research
Intensity](../../conventions.md#research-intensity-and-closure), not a single-pass
quota, method choice, or completion proof.
