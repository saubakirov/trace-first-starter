# Updating to TFW 3.5.1

RVAG — Reviewer Value-Assurance Gate. This patch restores Review as a gate on purpose, accepted
behavior, safety, authority and material assurance instead of treating process measurements or
record polish as product quality. It removes the hard changed-file ratio and blanket discrepancy
escalation, adds per-item material routing inside the existing REVIEW carrier and defines one terse
addressed terminal signal after the durable verdict.

## 2. Replace process-proxy verification with material claim selection

Refresh these canonical files as one connected Review group:

- `.tfw/workflows/review.md`;
- `.tfw/templates/review/map.md`;
- `.tfw/templates/review/verify.md`;
- `.tfw/templates/review/judge.md`;
- `.tfw/templates/REVIEW.md`;
- `.tfw/conventions.md`;
- `.tfw/glossary.md`.

Remove `tfw.review.min_verify_ratio` from `.tfw/project_config.yaml` and
`.tfw/templates/project_config.yaml`. Refresh `.tfw/workflows/config.md` so the old key is retired
migration state, not a live Sync Registry entry. Preserve every unrelated project choice.

The retired canonical rule was:

> `Verify at least ceil(files × tfw.review.min_verify_ratio) (default 0.42). Any discrepancy escalates to 100%.`

Its successor selects verification by the material claim, risk/criticality, affected behavior and
dependencies, relevant environment, oracle/authority, evidence gaps and explicit limits. A local
discrepancy reopens its affected claim and dependencies; it does not automatically make every
changed file relevant. Safety/security, human authority and accepted-result identity remain
mandatory floors.

Historical Judge labels such as `16.1% — the highest-firing check in TFW review`, `8.5%` and `4.0%`
are no longer verdict inputs. Retain historical measurements only when clearly labelled as historical
evidence; do not use firing frequency as a current quality weight.

## 3. Adopt per-item routing and one terminal verdict

Every finding that can block or return work now records:

1. subject and affected claim or authority;
2. observed fact and oracle;
3. concrete harm and material consequence;
4. owner and observable completion condition;
5. route or rung and Candidate effect.

A breached criterion, citation, count or process record alone is insufficient. Product/specification
defects retain their existing rungs; assurance-only gaps preserve independently established VALUE;
material authority/identity defects route to the exact ruler; non-material TRACE uses finite
current-carrier repair or observation. In a mixed round, the highest required authority sequences
the next act but never reclassifies lower items or drags unchanged VALUE into execution.

Keep one aggregate verdict. Do not add a second record-health verdict, score, registry, stage or
artifact. Permanent guards require a protected behavior/invariant, failure consequence and
demonstrated relevant counterfactual detection. Positive controls, temporary diagnostics, counts,
commit/artifact existence and scope arithmetic retain their limited labels and are not quality goals.

## 4. Install the compact Reviewer return without duplicating transport

After the durable REVIEW and authorized status/journal effect, the Reviewer sends exactly one
logical envelope to the task's exact `coordinator_route`:

```text
REVIEW · <reviewer-unit> · <task-or-phase> · <verdict> · <review-artifact@ref>
```

Preflight the route and immutable REVIEW ref. The exact tuple is one logical signal: a duplicate
creates no second verdict, ruling, lifecycle act or notification. Retry identical bytes only under a
provider-specific bound after the provider confirms non-application. Ambiguous delivery is neither
claimed nor blindly retried. This is semantic idempotence, not a universal exactly-once guarantee.

Do not change accepted AGSK transport, `.agents/rules/tfw.md`, its adapter template/README, manifests
or thin Codex/Antigravity skills for this payload. They already supply provider-owned routing. Update
`.claude/commands/tfw-review.md` and `.claude/commands/tfw-config.md` as byte-identical full copies of
their canonical workflows. Other providers receive no automatic-delivery claim by analogy.

5. Accepted AGSK transport, thin skills, manifest and Handoff are unchanged by this patch.
6. The receiving project's applicable checks pass. Upstream maintainer tests remain maintainer
