## G1 Evidence Contribution

Iteration 1 contributes an adversarial basis but not a G1-ready final recommendation:

- **What is disproved:** safe immediate deletion, manifest-only retirement, wholesale copy into Plan, Plan-owned closing/recovery writes, permanent redirect/tombstone, renamed/generic hidden helper, global text scrubbing, and automatic word-count success.
- **What remains viable:** C2 — one Plan continuation entry that inspects and routes only, with all lifecycle and Coordinator control owners preserved.
- **Value case observed:** Resume owns no unique product transition and has no canonical workflow caller; the public command can potentially disappear while its protections remain, reducing user choice and live distribution surface.
- **Cost/risk observed:** at least 23 live paths, four adapter layouts including an absent local Cursor receiver, a stale-file update migration, executable state fixtures, a split 182-path history oracle, and a reproducible surface counter are required. Fresh-session record recovery is the strongest residual value for keeping Resume.
- **North Star constraints:** NS1 requires inspectable state/authority and continuability; NS2 permits subtraction only after correctness, modularity, inspectability, and continuation survive; Structural Enforcement requires scenario and no-mutation oracles; D15 requires thin adapters and one canonical owner; D31 keeps research-local continuation independent; D68 forbids a replacement global state layer; F3 requires the keep-Resume countercase; F45 keeps subtraction preferred but not presumed.
- **Why G1 cannot fire yet:** H3/H4 are blocked, H2 lacks empirical evidence, and the configured two-iteration minimum has not been met.

## Conclusion

Iteration 1 established that `/tfw-resume` is not a duplicate implementation and cannot be deleted safely today, but it also found no unique product transition that requires a second public command. C2 survives as a bounded architecture in which Plan performs only exact inspection and routing while every lifecycle Role Lock and the shared Coordinator close/recovery contract remain intact. The research prevented three likely false proofs—manifest-only deletion, text scrubbing, and mismatched word arithmetic—and converted them into five explicit iteration-2 obligations. Its main limitation is empirical: all 46 focused current-state tests passed, but no post-retirement candidate exists, so no-mutation, update deletion, fresh-session recovery, and retained-surface claims remain unproved. Research iteration 1 is therefore complete but insufficient for G1.
