## 1. Vision — frozen

TFW economics helps people make management and product decisions, compare comparable work by people, teams and tools, and improve their practice through retrospectives and reflection. A reader sees the achieved result, approximate resources, common cycle boundary and material uncertainty together, and can decide what to keep, change or investigate. Collecting another digit is justified by the decision it can change.

**Impact:** The owner can discuss the cost of an accepted result and grounded improvements; the Coordinator can reconcile coverage without endless recapture; each producer has a truthful finite return. Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity can be compared where their work, outcomes and measurement meanings are comparable. This is decision support, not invoicing or a performance score inferred from tokens.

> **Explicitly imagined stakeholder quote:** “I can see what we achieved, what the estimate includes, and whether a difference deserves action or another experiment.”

### 3.2 Value Flow

```text
Management/product/retrospective question
  -> select work, actual result and people/team/tool membership
  -> use a common completed-cycle boundary and comparable metric meanings
  -> reconcile unique sources; show material missing/excluded coverage
  -> compare resources together with scope, quality and uncertainty
  -> keep the practice / change it / run a bounded follow-up / decline a weak ranking
  -> inspect the effect in later comparable work
```

**Common reference point:** a role's final durable result return bounds its contribution; the Coordinator's final integration/close capture occurs after required role results and relevant checks, immediately before the final owner-facing delivery. This is the same semantic checkpoint for all units, not one simultaneous wall-clock instant. A report from planning or an unfinished cycle is explicitly interim and is compared only as such. Final message, short subsequent human/agent exchanges and safe cleanup are disclosed excluded tails. Substantive new work/reopening creates a separately bounded continuation and is not hidden in the tail. Existing acceptance gates and truthful lifecycle states remain authoritative.

**Approximation:** the owner accepts practical error around 5–7%; this is a decision tolerance, not cent/token-level billing precision and not a guaranteed statistical confidence interval. Retain actual measured categories and provenance; round display without altering facts. A material missing role, stale selected revision, incompatible metric or duplicate count cannot be dismissed as a small tail. If error magnitude is not established, say so; do not attach a fabricated ±7% band. Differences plausibly explained by this tolerance do not support a confident ranking.

**Proportional obligation:** perform ordinary own-source collection and reconcile the actual returns at existing gates. A truthful binding-gap disposition completes accountability without pretending to provide measurement, provided the economics is not itself an explicit acceptance-critical deliverable. Missing required product evidence or an approved measurement deliverable retains its actual blocking route. Further collection/precision needs a concrete decision it can change; the default is a concise cycle estimate plus coverage, with deeper retrospective/comparison on a selected question. No new recurring gate or numerical quality score is created.

## 5. Definition of Done — frozen

1. The approved contract states the management/product/retrospective decisions economics supports and connects selected people/team/tool comparisons to result, scope, quality and actual membership.
2. One common cycle cutoff and the owner's 5–7% practical tolerance are intelligible and consistent across the selected economic consumers, without an unsupported accuracy guarantee or recursive tail capture.
3. Required binding unknown before extraction, actual known-bound failure, optional missing metric, incomplete return and finite tail each have a truthful outcome and correct continuation/acceptance effect. Accountable gaps do not manufacture measurement.
4. Examples distinguish received from counted, selected-bound completeness from lifecycle completion, and overlapping successors from disjoint usage; the existing reconciliation protections remain intact.
5. The report presents result, approximate resource meanings, selected scope and material uncertainty before proposing a decision. It includes comparable and non-comparable people/team/tool cases, small differences within tolerance, and justified “no conclusion / no change”.
6. The exact approved delivery is independently reviewed; relevant existing checks pass; owner acceptance and applicable reference/changelog/finite-close effects are complete. No version bump, release, distribution or measured savings is claimed without its separate evidence and authority.

## 6. Definition of Failure — frozen

1. A guessed source, failed attempt, attribution, precision band, missing-as-zero amount or historical completeness is presented as fact.
2. A material coverage gap is hidden by the 5–7% tolerance; duplicate/overlapping data is added; distinct price/time meanings are collapsed.
3. Tokens or API equivalents alone rank human competence, team productivity, tool quality or ROI, without comparable accepted outputs and the necessary supporting data.
4. Accounting creates an endless final-tail loop, an obligatory new registry/service/form, or a broad data-collection exercise without a decision; real acceptance-critical evidence is waived in the name of approximation.
5. The task changes numeric v1/helper semantics, other role communication, memory design, subagent policy, historical FLC/GSC records, private products, version or release effects outside approved scope.

**On failure:** retain the truthful current state and evidence, stop the affected action, and return the concrete boundary/decision through this Coordinator. Do not patch around the failure with invented identifiers, another record type or a hidden workflow exception.
