## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

TFW releases reach existing projects as coherent, safe and understandable improvements. The owner understands what their project gained, what changed in their work, any material limitation and the next useful action without learning TFW's internal file topology or implementation vocabulary. AI agents can produce this experience from the shipped instructions and available project evidence, without maintainer coaching or inventing missing authority.

**Impact:** Receiving owners spend attention on consequential project choices; agents perform technical discovery, migration and verification; maintainers catch contradictions before distributing them. TFW's own methodology values travel with the framework while receiving-project purpose, data and history retain their own authority.

> Target stakeholder statement, not an observed result: “I understand what became better in my project, know whether the update is complete, and can continue working without becoming a TFW maintainer.”

## 5. Definition of Done (DoD) 🔒 FROZEN

- ✅ 1. Both independent research passes have their own stage traces and RES; the Coordinator records how their findings affect the design, including disagreements and rejected alternatives. Independence is evidenced, not inferred from different session names.
- ✅ 2. The final design accounts for each material source-report finding and the owner's explicit requirements; implementation addresses causes and interactions, not only the three known textual/test defects.
- ✅ 3. Release rules, declared migration actions, live instructions, templates, installed adapter routes and verification claims agree at one immutable candidate, including existing installations rather than only empty receivers.
- ✅ 4. Current TFW methodology values are distributed in `.tfw/README.md` without a separate receiving-owner approval for those values. Legacy project-specific material and actual project-purpose authority survive the transition; project config and state are not overwritten as payload.
- ✅ 5. Agent instructions distinguish established choices, discoverable facts, new project decisions and separately authorized effects. Ordinary updates do not require repeated technical configuration interviews or approval of mechanical copy details.
- ✅ 6. A necessary owner question states its project consequence, evidence, recommendation and tradeoff in ordinary language. The full technical record remains inspectable without becoming the required approval interface.
- ✅ 7. New update records have an unambiguous location compatible with the project's creation and archive rules. An additional resolution container alone is neither a blanket write grant nor proof that every task in it is frozen.
- ✅ 8. Onboarding communicates applicable benefits, actual project setting/behavior changes, verified completion or noncompletion, material limitations/deferred work and the next useful action. Every factual claim has release or run evidence; positive framing hides nothing material.
- ✅ 9. Supported older-version routes, interrupted/repeated application and recovery have defined behavior that preserves history and avoids claiming success from partial installation.
- ✅ 10. Verification catches the known retired-wording/root inconsistencies and prevents inappropriate starter-corpus tests from being mistaken for receiver-project failures. No universal capability claim rests on a narrower check.
- ✅ 11. Evidence includes representative receiving-project scenarios and a fresh agent following the shipped instructions without corrective maintainer coaching; any comprehension or behavioral advantage is measured on declared cases and qualified to the observed environment.
- ✅ 12. Migration, compatibility and release notes ship with the changed behavior; the role artifacts, evidence and independent review leave a complete result ready for a separately authorized release.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Users must understand TFW internals to approve an ordinary update, or materially necessary decisions are hidden to minimize question count.
- ❌ 2. The agent claims completion despite an unresolved required check, partial migration or incompatible adapter route.
- ❌ 3. Current TFW values are withheld because the receiving owner did not separately approve the framework philosophy; or project-owned text/state/history is silently lost while distributing those values.
- ❌ 4. Positive messaging omits a material limitation or invents a benefit, verification result, user reaction or agent capability.
- ❌ 5. A green starter suite or byte-copy comparison is presented as proof of the complete existing-project update experience.
- ❌ 6. The independent pass consumes the main pass's research artifacts/results before delivering its own synthesis, or is reduced to validating the Coordinator's proposed fixes.
- ❌ 7. Conventional engineering patterns are transferred as if prompts were deterministic programs; a new script/runtime system substitutes for making agent instructions intelligible without an explicit, justified scope decision.
- ❌ 8. An approved historical contract, released tag, unrelated task or receiving project is changed outside this task's prospective authority.

**On failure:** Stop the affected action, preserve observed state and evidence, state the concrete impact and route to the proper decision owner. Recovery may restore the approved framework surface only where that is demonstrably safe; never erase project history or fabricate a successful gate.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Owner attention follows materiality.** Agents own technical discovery; humans retain consequential project choices and authority. Neither needless questions nor hidden decisions count as convenience.
2. **The project is the user's subject.** Explain what changes in the owner's work, not how TFW happens to encode that change.
3. **Instructions address reasoning agents.** Apply engineering lessons through agent interpretation, uncertainty, context and observable behavior, not by assuming a Markdown instruction executes like code.
4. **One coherent delivery.** Framework rules, migration, adapters, validation and onboarding belong to the change that requires them.
5. **Explicit ownership.** TFW philosophy belongs to TFW; receiving-project purpose, configuration, knowledge and history remain project-owned.
6. **Candor and continuity.** A useful next action and honest limitation are part of the result. Positive framing is compatible with, and depends on, truthful reporting.
7. **Evidence is scoped.** Distinguish declared, installed, byte-consistent, behaviorally observed and user-understood outcomes; do not promote one into another.
8. **Saint-Exupéry as judgment.** Seek the simplest complete coherent form without damaging purpose, value, correctness, architecture, modularity, inspectability or continuation.
9. **Independent exploration earns its conclusions.** Shared owner goals and safety bounds do not prescribe a design. Novelty is not proof, and a no-better-alternative finding is legitimate.
10. **Prospective change, preserved history.** Supersede incompatible current rules explicitly; never rewrite earlier decisions to pretend the new interpretation was always in force.

## 10. RESEARCH Case 🟢 FREE

### Blind Spots

- Which minimal decision context lets an agent reliably reuse established project choices while recognizing genuinely missing authority?
- Which release/update representation prevents contradictions across multiple prompt consumers without creating another competing registry?
- How do legacy customized methodology documents, designated project purpose and adapter paths migrate coherently?
- What evidence distinguishes a comprehensible instruction from an agent that only appears compliant in one maintainer session?
- Which engineering ideas survive adaptation to probabilistic language-model readers, and which become misleading ritual?

The owner's policy about framework values and the project's user-facing purpose is settled input, not a hypothesis to ask them again.

### Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Status |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | A facts/established-choices/new-decisions distinction can remove routine repeat interviews without losing material owner decisions across representative receiver cases. If false, the decision model must change rather than hide questions. | needs-research |
| H2 | A coherent release-to-receiver contract can keep migration and validation consistent without adding a new mutable registry. If false, compare the justified alternatives and their maintenance/agent-reading cost. | needs-research |
| H3 | Evidence-backed release benefits and actual receiver outcome/continuation can form one clear final communication without either technical overload or hidden failures. If false, revise the communication structure. | needs-research |
| H4 | An independent prompt-native reframing can yield a simpler complete approach than incremental repair of the existing procedure. If no alternative survives challenge, retain that negative result rather than manufacture novelty. | needs-research |

### Risks of Not Researching

Known textual/root/test defects can be reproduced without a broad research program, but fixing only them leaves unresolved the agent's decision model, ownership migration and user comprehension. Uncritically importing software-engineering patterns may build an elaborate mechanism that a reasoning agent misreads; preserving the current prompts may preserve the same burden.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

Two independent passes start from the same approved owner purpose and safety boundaries. They run in parallel, not as a main pass followed by a review of it.

| Pass | Question and entry | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| `iter1` — evidence-led system design | H1–H3: trace the actual release/update path, reports, existing decisions and applicable primary references; distinguish source defects, missing semantics and interaction failures. | Competing coherent designs, scenario coverage, preserved authority, tradeoffs and testable recommendations. |
| `iter2` — independent first-principles reframing | H4: “How would we design this if the operational reader is an AI agent interpreting prompts, while the beneficiary is a person working on their own project?” Do not start from the Coordinator's fix list or treat H1–H3 as a required solution. | A genuine alternative framing or a justified finding that none improves the baseline; engineering-practice → agent adaptation → benefit → failure boundary → test mappings, with primary sources and counterevidence. |

**Mental direction for the independent pass:** Read engineering best practices through the distinction between a deterministic program and an agent interpreting language, evidence, context, uncertainty and authority. Propose useful transfers and explicitly reject misleading analogies. Neither more prose, more commands, a new script nor fewer files is intrinsically an improvement. Produce enough concrete prompt/interaction examples to test the idea; do not turn the research into implementation.

**Independence and dispatch contract:**

- Both receive the frozen HL and its owner requirements, but neither receives the other's briefing, stages, RES, private progress or conclusions before delivering its own RES. The independent pass must be free to challenge the HL's proposed representation through the normal amendment channel.
- Use two fresh native Codex sessions, not forks or subagents, each running `/tfw-research` in its own worktree. They report through threads to this Coordinator, not to each other. Shared task purpose is not shared reasoning.
- **Owner-directed task-local exception, 2026-09-06:** the explicit parallel-independent request overrides sequential predecessor loading for these two assigned passes only. `iter2` does not wait for or read `iter1`; its folder number is an assignment, not evidence of a predecessor. Dispatch must name its assigned folder and supersede automatic highest-folder/first-pending selection for that session. This exception does not change the framework workflow globally.
- Only the Coordinator writes master `status.md`, journal routing events and `research/iterations.yaml`. Each Researcher owns only its assigned `research/iterN/` and research commits; the Coordinator verifies and lands those results.
- All other research stage, source, role-lock, evidence and completion requirements remain. Routine research checkpoints go to the Coordinator within the approved questions; new owner-only decisions remain explicit unresolved questions, never guessed to preserve independence.
- After both RES outputs exist, the Coordinator compares them against the same owner outcome and cases. Each recommendation keeps its original proposer and its refinement/amendment classification. No “merge all ideas” default.
- Two completed independent passes satisfy the proposed two-pass minimum under the explicit parallel exception. Additional sequential research is justified only by surviving material gaps. No extra task/session is created just to synthesize the two results.

1. **Gather:** Establish real receiver failure/decision cases and primary-source engineering mechanisms appropriate to each pass.
2. **Extract:** Compare coherent alternatives; name authority, ownership, uncertainty and user-information consequences.
3. **Challenge:** Seek failures, false analogies, safety regressions and unsupported benefit claims; return accepted/rejected alternatives with evidence and costs.

Research begins only after owner approval and a committed freeze of this HL. The control file and assigned stage directories are created at their workflow gates, not as placeholders.

### Why Not Just...?

- **Patch the three confirmed defects only?** Necessary, but it does not change repeated interviews, obscured choices or incomplete user understanding.
- **Ask nothing and preserve everything?** Missing authority can remain unresolved, while obsolete workarounds and framework philosophy stay frozen.
- **Show the owner the full technical checklist?** It preserves detail but has already failed to make a consequential archive write visible.
- **Turn update into a conventional installer?** That is a different product/architecture; it does not by itself make an agent's judgment or communication reliable.
- **Let the second researcher review the first?** It loses the independent framing the owner explicitly requested.
