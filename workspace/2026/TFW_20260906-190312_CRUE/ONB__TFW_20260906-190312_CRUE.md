## 5. Risks Found (edge cases, potential issues not in TS)

1. Current `update.md` makes installed/target version equality an early completion path while the same workflow writes provenance before final verification, briefing, and cleanup. A crash between those points can be misread as success unless re-entry re-observes the receiver.
2. A `.tfw/README.md` can be tracked, untracked, customized, or explicitly project-purpose-bearing. Git absence at a freeze revision cannot prove absence, and a preserved byte attachment must not silently become current project authority.
3. A source-blocked attempt may still write diagnostic staging or preservation attachments. Evidence must disclose those writes without calling them target application or claiming preservation that was not proved.
4. `.agent` and `.agents` may coexist with foreign/unowned files. A plural self-install correction must not delete singular rules or infer workflow discovery from rule discovery.
5. The six native subjects can have provider authentication, shared-object, absolute-path, hook, symlink, remote, or service escape routes that a prompt and post-run hash do not detect. If effective containment is not demonstrable, the field campaign is blocked rather than simulated.
6. An exact-path selected sibling TRACE can be harmless in one hunk and operational or authority-bearing in another. Whole-file classification and mixed-hunk refusal are needed to avoid both blanket refusal and unsafe inclusion.
7. The approved soft prompt is reached at 35 logical VALUE rows / 5000 forecast LOC, but the owner ceiling remains 60/8000 against the immutable 30/4000 denominator. This is not permission to add convenience paths.
