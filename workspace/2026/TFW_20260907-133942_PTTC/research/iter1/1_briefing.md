## Research Plan
### Gather
- Reconstruct the CRATM and CRUE causal timeline from repository records and attributed reports, separating reported outcomes, observed durations, implementation mistakes, stale oracles, protocol gaps and unmeasured costs.
- Trace the actual fixture and command paths around `docs/scripts/test_integration.py`, the Phase E knowledge check, the current-release checks and the doctor gate; record where MkDocs, corpus state or repeated process work is introduced.
- Trace capture-to-close ownership through the existing review, output, status and knowledge carriers, using the recorded runs and the immutable CRUE repair source without rerunning an expensive probe.

### Extract
- Decompose each material cause into relevant inputs, environment assumptions, setup/body/process/attention cost, affected outputs and protected consequence.
- For each candidate mechanism, compare KEEP / REWORK / MOVE / REMOVE and state the simplest adequate alternative, including the evidence boundary that would invalidate reuse.
- Map finite post-capture checks, evidence applicability and material-change return conditions to existing owners without introducing a selector, cache, counter or parallel closure system.

### Challenge
- Challenge the causal account with the B–D → B–E failure, the historical 3.0.0/current 3.1.0 release mismatch and the retained historical doctor finding; distinguish false rejection from a real defect and from a disclosed corpus condition.
- Test H1–H3 against the observed evidence and at least one concrete counterexample per claim, preserving uncertainty where no same-environment measurement exists.
- Compare the combined design against H4: determine whether removal or consolidation preserves meaningful defect detection and finite closure, and record unresolved material questions for a separately authorized iteration 2 rather than running it.
