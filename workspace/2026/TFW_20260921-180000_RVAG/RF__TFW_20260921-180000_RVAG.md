## 2. Key Decisions

1. A finding, not a file, discrepancy or review round, is the unit of classification and completion.
   A breached criterion/citation/count/process record has no verdict effect without an affected
   accepted claim/authority, fact+oracle, harm, material consequence, owner, completion, route and
   Candidate effect.
2. Safety/security, human acceptance authority and accepted-result identity are mandatory review
   floors. Materiality cannot waive severe harm or supply a missing ruler.
3. Highest authority sequences only the next authorized act. It does not reclassify lower items,
   drag unchanged VALUE into execution or move another item's Candidate.
4. Evidence is bound to accepted subject, Candidate/revision, environment, oracle/authority and
   dependency state. A false sole carrier is ASSURANCE; the same redundant carrier can be
   non-material TRACE after independent proof.
5. The terminal tuple is semantically idempotent, not a universal exactly-once transport claim.
   Accepted AGSK/Antigravity and Codex mechanics are named only from their own evidence.
6. No second verdict, score, registry, carrier, stage or permanent test was introduced.
