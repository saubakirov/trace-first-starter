## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

TFW review is an independent value-assurance gate. It determines whether the accepted result serves
the approved purpose, behaves correctly in its real domain, is safe and architecturally sound, and
is supported by evidence strong enough for the claims that matter. Process traces remain necessary
for authority and continuation, but their polish, counts and internal bookkeeping are never mistaken
for the quality of the result they describe.

**Impact:** A correct product no longer enters repeated execution rounds because a derived record has
an immaterial count, stale label or wording defect. A real business, functional, safety, authority or
acceptance-critical evidence defect still stops acceptance immediately, even when every TFW artifact
is structurally perfect.

> “Show me whether the result works, serves the task and can be trusted. Do not call document churn quality control.”

## 3. Target State (To-Be) 🔒 FROZEN

Review answers three distinct questions in a fixed order:

1. **Value:** Is the accepted result fit for purpose and correct in its business or domain behavior?
2. **Assurance:** Is the material value claim established strongly enough for its risk?
3. **Trace:** Are authority, result identity and continuation truthful and usable?

Only a material failure in those subjects may return work. A trace defect blocks only when it
invalidates authority, accepted-result identity, safety, reproducibility, or the evidence needed to
establish a material claim. Other record defects are visible observations or bounded record-only
corrections and do not restart product execution.

Permanent tests and checks name the observable behavior or invariant they protect, the harm they
detect, and evidence of detection power. For newly corrected behavior this normally includes a
red-before-green result; for existing behavior it may use a relevant mutant or other demonstrated
counterfactual. Positive controls are labelled as controls and are not counted as independent guards.

Under `tfw-gates-only`, a Reviewer completes its role with two deliberately different outputs. The
durable REVIEW remains the full evidence and reasoning record. After that record and its authorized
status/journal effect exist, the Reviewer sends exactly one compact terminal envelope directly to
its recorded `coordinator_route`; the transport message does not repeat the review narrative and
never routes to the owner, Executor, peer role or GATEWAY.

### 3.1 Result Visualization

```text
BEFORE

any discrepancy
      ↓
100% file audit → breached process AC → REVISE → more TRACE → another review


AFTER

accepted result
      ↓
┌─ VALUE: purpose, domain behavior, architecture, safety ─ defect → REVISE / REJECT
│
├─ ASSURANCE: does evidence establish the material claim? ─ gap → REVISE / BLOCKED
│
└─ TRACE: authority, result identity, continuation
       ├─ material corruption → REVISE / BLOCKED
       └─ immaterial record defect → observation or finite record-only correction
```

| Stakeholder question | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| “Does it solve the real problem?” | One checklist row among process checks | First and governing judgment |
| “Do the tests protect behavior?” | Passing tests and counts can stand as proof | Protection subject, harm and detection power are explicit |
| “Does this typo require another execution round?” | Often yes if an AC can be cited | Only if it changes a material acceptance conclusion or action |
| “Why is this number here?” | Count may become its own objective | Unit, selector, reference revision and decision use are required |
| “What happens to a harmless record error?” | Full REVISE route | Bounded record-only correction or documented observation |
| “How does the Reviewer finish?” | A long final answer may remain inside the Reviewer task without an addressed return | One direct line: `REVIEW · <unit> · <task/phase> · <verdict> · <artifact@ref>` to `coordinator_route` |

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. A product can be wrong, unsafe or beside the point while a polished TFW record earns approval.
- ❌ 2. An immaterial TRACE discrepancy can still force a new execution round without a named change to an acceptance conclusion or authorized action.
- ❌ 3. File ratios, checklist firing rates, test counts, commit counts, artifact counts or scope budgets are treated as quality goals rather than bounded decision inputs.
- ❌ 4. A test is retained merely because it exists or is green, without a protected behavior, consequence and demonstrated ability to detect the relevant failure.
- ❌ 5. A useful characterization, conformance or contract test is deleted solely because it was already green on the historical baseline; red-before-green is made the only universal admissibility rule.
- ❌ 6. Review independence, human authority, Purpose Check, evidence sufficiency, safety or security is weakened in the name of reducing ceremony.
- ❌ 7. A hard revision-count limit hides unresolved material defects instead of fixing the false-return predicate.
- ❌ 8. MFX history or any prior verdict is rewritten to make the new rule appear retroactive.
- ❌ 9. The solution adds a new score, registry, mandatory artifact or permanent test suite whose own maintenance becomes another quality proxy.
- ❌ 10. Code-specific terminology makes the contract unusable for non-code results.
- ❌ 11. A Reviewer leaves the verdict only in its own task, sends a narrative dump instead of the compact envelope, or routes material output to the owner, Executor, peer role or GATEWAY under `tfw-gates-only`.
- ❌ 12. Automatic addressed return is claimed for a provider whose native send/readback mechanism was not established independently.
- ❌ 13. RVAG overwrites an unlanded AGSK Phase B change, edits AGSK task artifacts, or prepares `3.5.1` from a composition that does not contain the accepted `3.5.0` migration result.
- ❌ 14. A release tag, push, publication, deployment or notification is performed merely because preparation of the `3.5.1` release commit was authorized.

**On failure:** stop the affected route, preserve the observed evidence, and return to the owner or
the authorized contract ruler rather than weakening the materiality or independence boundary.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Value before ceremony** — process exists to protect purpose and accepted value; it is never the product by default.
2. **Materiality with a named harm** — a finding changes a verdict only when its consequence is concrete and material.
3. **Evidence follows the claim** — verification depth is chosen by the claim, risk, oracle and changed dependency, not by a percentage of files.
4. **Detection power over test presence** — assurance earns permanence by detecting a relevant failure, not by increasing a count.
5. **Semantic classification** — VALUE, ASSURANCE, TRACE and DERIVED are determined by purpose, never by filename or folder.
6. **Acceptance and record quality are distinct** — an untidy record can require correction without making a correct result incorrect.
7. **Independent review remains independent** — the Reviewer verifies and proposes but does not repair its own findings or inherit acceptance authority.
8. **Subtraction before accretion** — remove false gates and redundant measurements before inventing new machinery.
9. **Domain-agnostic assurance** — business rules, analytical claims, documents, operational effects and code behavior receive the same materiality test.
10. **Detailed record, terse signal** — evidence and reasoning live in REVIEW; coordination receives only the smallest addressed terminal envelope needed to continue safely.

### Why Not Just...?

- Why not cap the number of review rounds? — A count hides unresolved material defects and does not stop the first false return.
- Why not make TRACE findings non-blocking unconditionally? — Some trace failures invalidate authority, result identity, safety provenance or reproducibility.
- Why not require every test to be red on the historical baseline? — Characterization and contract tests may protect behavior that was already correct; detection power is the governing property.
- Why not add a second score for “product quality”? — Another score risks recreating the same metric substitution and reporting burden.
