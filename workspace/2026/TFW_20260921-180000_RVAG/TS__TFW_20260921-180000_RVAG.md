## 1. Objective

Restore Review as an independent gate on purpose, domain behavior, safety, authority and material
assurance. Replace process-proxy returns with ordered VALUE→ASSURANCE→TRACE judgment, classified
per-item consequences and routes, one aggregate verdict, and one compact addressed terminal signal.
The change must reduce false execution loops without weakening rejection of wrong, unsafe,
unauthorized or materially unproved results.

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Make each finding—not its filename, artifact class or whole review round—the unit of classification,
  consequence, ownership, completion and payment.
- Replace hard file-ratio verification and blanket discrepancy escalation with recorded
  claim/risk/dependency/environment/oracle selection, explicit limits and mandatory safety/authority
  boundaries.
- Define admission for permanent guards by protected behavior/invariant, failure consequence and
  demonstrated relevant counterfactual detection; distinguish diagnostics, positive controls and
  governance assertions.
- Preserve one terminal verdict while keeping per-item product, assurance, authority and finite
  record-only routes and Candidate effects visible.
- Fix the compact Reviewer return envelope, durable-before-send ordering, exact route/ref preflight
  and provider-bounded semantic idempotence.
- Propagate the semantics through the twelve approved existing files and preserve canonical/Claude
  full-copy parity.
- Verify the result with existing repository tests and bounded scenario replay; add no permanent
  test merely for workflow evidence, coverage or count.

### Out of Scope

- Any change to accepted AGSK transport, `.agents/rules/tfw.md`, its adapter template/README,
  Antigravity manifest/skills, thin Codex skills or `3.5.0` history.
- Any change to `.tfw/workflows/handoff.md`, root coordination rules or task history outside RVAG.
- A second verdict, score, registry, schema, stage, artifact or permanent test suite.
- Rewriting MFX, FRATS, AGSK or prior REVIEW history.
- Release-owned `.tfw/VERSION`, config-version fields, `.tfw/CHANGELOG.md` or
  `.tfw/migrations/3.5.1.md`; those belong to the later authorized `/tfw-release` effect.
- Tag, push, publication, deployment or notification.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

### AC-1: Material value-assurance judgment

Canonical Review and its Map/Judge carriers enforce ordered VALUE→ASSURANCE→TRACE reasoning and
one aggregate verdict after classifying each finding.

- [ ] Every blocking/returning item records subject, affected claim or authority, observed fact and
  oracle, concrete harm, material consequence, owner, completion condition, route and Candidate
  effect.
- [ ] A breached criterion, discrepancy, citation or process record cannot change the verdict
  without those fields and a material accepted claim or authority consequence.
- [ ] Purpose, domain behavior, safety/security, human acceptance authority and accepted-result
  identity remain explicit non-waivable review subjects.

Gate: inspect the canonical workflow plus Map/Judge/REVIEW templates for one consistent item grammar.
Evidence: replay MFX, TLD, CRATM, FRATS, wrong-purpose non-code and iter2 mixed-round cases in EV.

### AC-2: Per-item completion routes and mixed rounds

- [ ] VALUE/contract defects use the existing product/specification rungs; assurance-only gaps
  preserve an independently established Candidate; material authority/identity defects route to the
  exact ruler; non-material TRACE receives finite current-carrier repair or observation.
- [ ] Highest required authority controls sequence and the next authorized act only; it does not
  reclassify lower items, drag unchanged VALUE upward or force every item through the highest rung.
- [ ] One REVIEW can dispose mixed findings without splitting into duplicate verdicts or full-audit
  rounds.

Gate: compare workflow, conventions, glossary and REVIEW template routing statements.
Evidence: EV records one mixed material/non-material round and one assurance-only round.

### AC-3: Claim- and risk-selected verification

- [ ] `tfw.review.min_verify_ratio` is absent from both config copies and its Config Sync Registry
  row is retired without disturbing unrelated values.
- [ ] Canonical Review/Verify contains no active “any discrepancy → 100%” or universal changed-file
  ratio rule.
- [ ] Verification records material claims, risk/criticality, affected dependencies/behavior,
  relevant environment, oracle/authority, evidence gaps and unresolved limits.
- [ ] Safety/security, authority and identity floors remain mandatory regardless of sampling.

Gate: exact key/rule search across the twelve VALUE files plus semantic inspection.
Evidence: EV binds selected checks and limits to the replayed material claims.

### AC-4: Meaningful test and check admission

- [ ] A permanent guard names protected behavior/invariant, failure consequence and demonstrated
  relevant counterfactual detection; historical red-before-green is one strong form, not the only
  universal form.
- [ ] Positive controls, temporary diagnostics, counts, commit/artifact existence and scope arithmetic
  are labelled for their actual use and cannot masquerade as product-quality guards.
- [ ] No new permanent test is added by RVAG.

Gate: inspect canonical Verify/Judge/REVIEW wording and Candidate file list.
Evidence: EV classifies representative regression, characterization, positive-control, diagnostic
and governance checks without rewarding test count.

### AC-5: Compact addressed Reviewer return

- [ ] The logical envelope is exactly
  `REVIEW · <reviewer-unit> · <task-or-phase> · <verdict> · <review-artifact@ref>`.
- [ ] It is sent once as a logical signal only after durable REVIEW and authorized status/journal
  effect, directly to exact `coordinator_route`; it never carries findings prose or routes to owner,
  Executor, peer or GATEWAY under `tfw-gates-only`.
- [ ] Route and immutable REVIEW@ref are preflighted. The exact tuple is the logical identity;
  duplicate receipt creates no second verdict/ruling/lifecycle act. A provider-confirmed non-applied
  failure may use only a provider-specific bounded identical retry; ambiguity is neither claimed nor
  blindly retried.
- [ ] Codex and accepted AGSK/Antigravity behavior are named only from their own evidence; no

Gate: inspect canonical Review terminal step, REVIEW template and provider-bound wording.
Evidence: EV replays success, wrong route, stale ref, duplicate, confirmed non-application and
ambiguous-delivery cases without performing an external effect.

## 7. Definition of Failure

- ❌ A polished process record can approve wrong-purpose, incorrect, unsafe, insecure or unauthorized
  work, or an immaterial record defect can restart unchanged product execution.
- ❌ A discrepancy, file ratio, firing rate, test/commit/artifact count or scope budget becomes a
  quality objective or standalone return reason.
- ❌ A second verdict, score, registry, schema, stage, artifact or permanent test suite is added.
- ❌ Mixed-round authority recasts lower items or moves unchanged VALUE instead of sequencing acts.
- ❌ Useful characterization/contract assurance is deleted solely because it was already green, or
  test presence/count substitutes for demonstrated detection power.
- ❌ The envelope is narrative, precedes durable state, uses a wrong route/unresolved ref, creates a
  duplicate act, or claims universal exactly-once/retry behavior without provider evidence.
- ❌ Accepted AGSK transport, thin skills, manifest, Handoff, unrelated config or prior history is
  modified without a demonstrated AC gap and owner-approved scope change.
- ❌ Candidate includes release `3.5.1` effects or any external tag/push/publish/deploy/notify action.
- ❌ Any VALUE path outside the twelve-file selector or more than 2,000 touched text LOC is used
  without the owner-reserved prospective scope ruling.

## 8. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Materiality becomes discretion to waive severe but rare harm | Keep safety/security, authority, identity and human acceptance explicit item fields and blocking floors. |
| Per-item detail recreates form-filling | Require only fields that determine consequence, owner, completion, route or Candidate effect; add no new carrier. |
| Removing the ratio yields arbitrary verification | Require a replayable claim/risk/dependency/environment/oracle selection and explicit limits. |
| One verdict hides mixed obligations | Preserve item-level dispositions; aggregate only after classification. |
| Compact return is mistaken for evidence or authority | Durable REVIEW/status/journal remain governing; the envelope is signal only. |
| Retry produces duplicate effects | Exact tuple identity, duplicate no-op semantics and provider-confirmed bounded retry only. |
| Canonical and installed copies drift | Regenerate and byte-compare the two Claude full copies. |
| Release changes contaminate the reviewed Candidate | Keep `/tfw-release` after independent acceptance and use the bounded affected follow-up for config version writes. |
