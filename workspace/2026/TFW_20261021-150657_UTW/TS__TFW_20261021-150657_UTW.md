## 1. Objective

Embed a small domain-neutral understanding obligation in TFW's existing purpose, work and evidence owners. At a consequential decision, a responsible human can obtain the relevant whole, inspect important relationships and consequences of change, see why work is expected to serve its governing purpose, and identify missing or contrary grounds without mastering implementation detail.

Deliver the canonical changes and one usable [pilot result](pilot-result.md) covering a TFW process and this repository's documentation build. Demonstrate bounded actual human use and change handling, then decide which added structure earns retention. A coherent explanation, source link or successful build alone is not evidence of human understanding or strategic contribution.

## 7. Definition of Failure

- An adequate-looking account leaves the participant unable to reason about a relevant new change; correct repetition, assistance or confidence is substituted for the required human observation.
- Material source/meaning/runtime limits are hidden, an exposed answer is called unfamiliar reasoning, or a common explanation/key error passes as independent evidence.
- Purpose is represented only by a link/score, contrary grounds cannot be surfaced, or the goal is silently changed to rationalize activity.
- Added records, exams, roles or infrastructure become universal requirements without decision value; core authority is duplicated or made software/vendor-specific.
- Work stops at research or a concept paper, omits an actual pilot/required category, or expands into unapproved products, permanent tests or external effects.
- A required check/authority/accounting boundary remains invalid, or the participant allowance is exceeded without prior owner approval.

Return the exact failed claim and evidence through the existing Coordinator route. A transparent negative or inconclusive experiment is useful evidence, but cannot falsely close a failed frozen value obligation. Preserve failed observations and valid unaffected evidence; revise within authority or seek the necessary owner decision.
