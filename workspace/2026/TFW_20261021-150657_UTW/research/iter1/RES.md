### Minimal core and practical domain form

The proposed core is an outcome obligation in existing framework locations, not a new universal document type. Its minimum content is the relevant whole and boundary, important parts/relations, purpose, material decision/change grounds, source applicability and unknowns, and an owner for refresh. A reader must be able to inspect why a consequential claim is made without reconstructing the original chat.

| Work moment and existing location | Proposed content / producer | Reuse and cost boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Framing: current Plan and HL context/result/alternatives sections | Coordinator identifies responsible reader, question, boundary, visible main relationships, expected value and uncertain assumptions | Reuse approved sources; choose sufficient explanation/visual form rather than create a parallel model of all facts |
| Entry and material decisions: current ONB understanding/questions and execution decision grounds | Executor checks its working account against source authority; retains consequential reasons as work proceeds | The agent's account is preparation, not evidence of human understanding; no pause after every mechanical action |
| Material return: current RF decision/diagram/handover sections | Producing author explains what changed, affected relationships/consequences and unresolved grounds; makes the overview usable for the next reader | Update affected meaning and source references; avoid duplicate authoritative facts |
| Verification: current EV and independent review, including Purpose Check | Executor records bounded human-use/behavior observations when authorized; Reviewer checks source fidelity, applicability and material claims | Applicable evidence, actual help and limits matter; independent role identity alone cannot repair a shared wrong answer key |
| Continuing use: current knowledge-use/handover ownership | Source/decision owner responds to material source or context change and names uncertainty | Byte change is a signal, not semantic proof; periodic review needs an expiring consequential assumption |

These are integration hypotheses for later TS selection, not edits or additional workflow gates. A small relation table, labeled path or diagram can be part of C1* when needed for an adequate overview; C2* denotes additional maintained/derived representation whose benefit must be tested. The comparison must preserve meaningful visible relationships in both arms.

Optional specialization can be an ordinary example or local instruction naming domain vocabulary, relevant sources, a consequential scenario, acceptable grounds/unknowns and refresh ownership. Business specialization can use process handovers and outcomes; software can use source/configuration/observed execution; personal work can use a self-selected aim and attempted application. The business and personal-work examples demonstrate expressibility only. A plugin SDK, graph store, daemon, universal analyzer or employee-exam system has no demonstrated necessity.

### Costs and simplify/omit decisions

Initial source selection, explanation and semantic checking remain necessary even without tooling. C2* adds selection/layout/upkeep; C3* adds facilitation and exposure effects; C4* adds setup, coverage checking and curation. Possible benefits include lower reconstruction/search effort and clearer consequential decisions, but none has been measured here. If equivalent simple material serves the bounded decision equally well, omit the extra view or extractor. If people need an initial whole-system account, retain it even when longer. If the least sufficient form cannot meet the frozen outcome at acceptable cost, return the evidence and alternative through the existing amendment route; do not silently remove the outcome.

The later pilot still owes distinct observations for initial preparation, actual human use and one real or explicitly controlled update: correctness/omissions, disclosed assistance, human effort, subjective clarity, model/tool usage, semantic-repair work and an adoption/subtraction decision.
