- Compare a short unfamiliar-scenario discussion, explanation/prediction tasks and recognition/recall items. Specify assistance and scoring grounds, relevance to responsibility, practice effects and limits; distinguish a tool-supported decision from independent retention or transfer.
- Expose setup, maintenance and attention drivers alongside usable native token evidence. Keep estimates, measured facts, unavailable categories and API-price scenarios distinct. Identify the conditions under which added structure should be simplified or omitted.
