### C5 — Fairness and proportionality of the later pilot

The minimal proposal retains B0/current means and adequate C1*. Add C2* only for an observed named relationship difficulty; compare matched content/help. Capture actual human reasoning about a genuinely unexposed change as well as ordinary supported use. A first unfamiliar response may use sources; unfamiliar is not synonymous with unaided. If a live assistant supplies the reasoning, report that as assisted performance and keep the person's contribution visible.

For one person, repeated exposure/order effects cannot be balanced away. Use different matched scenarios, disclose order/familiarity/help, and limit inference to feasibility and observed performance. Do not call a faster second response a causal format benefit. If separating C1*/C2* benefit matters to acceptance but the comparison is confounded, the result is inconclusive, not success. A broader study is a separate owner decision, not an automatic research iteration.

Remove the routine unaided retention arm, mandatory whole reconstruction and extraction infrastructure from the minimal proposal. They answer additional questions, consume time and are unnecessary to demonstrate source-supported reasoning in a new scenario. Keep explanation access unrestricted. Keep only decision-relevant observations: material reasoning/uncertainty, help and exposure, active preparation/use/update time, consequential searches/rework, and the material/nonmaterial refresh disposition. Self-reported effort/confidence is optional context, not a success metric. No permanent instrumentation or test suite is required by this design.

A single compact record in existing evidence can capture these observations for each selected case. Prepare/check it once; do not create a parallel scoring or knowledge registry. If C1* is adequate and C2* adds maintenance without an observed decision benefit, omit C2*. If benefit or cost is unmeasured, say so rather than select a winner by aesthetic preference. The owner/TS must still fix participant, actual software case, environment, time cap and acceptance basis before running the pilot.

### C6 — External counter-evidence and limits

Rechecked the original [Blunt and Karpicke (2014) paper](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2014/2014_Blunt_Karpicke_JEDP.pdf), abstract and Experiment 1 design, on 2026-10-21. Their educational experiments separated activity format from text availability; retrieval in map and paragraph formats did not differ in the reported delayed performance, and Experiment 1 counterbalanced order. This undermines a blanket assumption that a map itself supplies the benefit and supports treating access/activity/order as separate design variables. It does not validate this workplace pilot, prove all representations equivalent, or establish that withholding help is desirable at work. This stage reuses the prior evidence line rather than opening a new literature survey.
