## Research Context

This owner-requested supplemental iteration compares the original Karpathy LLM Wiki pattern, inspected TFW knowledge practices and current Structurizr. **No additional platform is yet justified by the demonstrated cases.** TFW can already retain sources, qualify claims and produce an explanation in an existing reference. Deliberate recurring topic maintenance can be useful without becoming a separate system. Structurizr is a conditional addition for several useful views of shared relationships; it can also display explanatory prose. Combining them requires both needs to survive simpler alternatives. ### Authority, grounds and maintenance

Sources retain their producer/epoch and scope. Accepted claims retain existing qualification authority; topics and models link to that disposition rather than accepting competing truths. A named producer checks a topic's explanation; the model maintainer checks its abstraction; rendered views remain derived. Agreement between prose and diagram is not independent corroboration. This can be carried by existing handovers/references, without a mandatory new registry.

| Model assertion | Grounds | Preserved limit |
|---|---|---|
| Approved intended design | Scoped authorized design decision | Does not prove implementation conforms |
| Structurally supported relation | Code/configuration at a revision and stated interpretation/extraction rules | Does not prove execution |
| Proposed or inferred relation | Explicit reasoning and assumptions | Does not establish approval or observation |
| Observed execution | Bound observation with environment, time and coverage | Does not establish universal behavior |

This replaces Extract E4's overly broad characterization of a model as interpretation unless observed. Review the assertion actually affected, not every diagram after every edit. A valid abstraction can survive a detailed source change; an explicit obsolete claim cannot.

[Documentation attachment](https://docs.structurizr.com/dsl/docs) is documented for workspace/system/container, with a relative directory alongside or below its parent file. Default import is alphabetical Markdown/AsciiDoc with supported images; arbitrary parent traversal or selective single-file inclusion was not established. A model file at a suitable ancestor of a deliberately selected directory, or a bounded derived presentation directory, are candidates. The latter adds stale-copy/regeneration risk. [ADR import](https://docs.structurizr.com/dsl/adrs) is format-aware; direct compatibility with TFW records remains unproven. An authorized local check must inspect exact imported scope, links and fidelity before claiming integration. The documentation-path source was successfully rechecked during Challenge.
