## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

An inter-role message identifies a permitted action and the exact durable record to read. The record supplies the substance and authority. Each checkpoint makes clear whether the current role continues, waits for a decision, or returns control to its Coordinator.

**Impact:** The owner no longer has to interpret a worker's conversational summary to pass the baton. The Coordinator can advance already authorized work from inspectable records, while material human decisions and independent review retain their force. No measured saving or guaranteed agent compliance is claimed.

> **Explicitly imagined stakeholder quote:** “I can see who must act next, and the files tell them everything needed.” This is an intended experience, not testimony.

### 3.2 Value Flow

```text
Owner needs reliable handover without conversational steering
  -> explicit checkpoint condition and authority
       [a successful check does not become a needless owner pause]
  -> one durable result with grounds, state and exact version
       [the recipient has an inspectable source]
  -> one fixed signal on the authorized vertical edge
       [no second account, verdict hint or copied working discussion]
  -> independent file read + current authority validation
       [routine advancement; only a genuinely missing decision returns upward]
```

```text
No blocker: Executor saves ONB -> ONB_READY -> continues approved work
Blocker:    Executor saves ONB -> GATE_REQUIRED -> waits
            Coordinator records valid gate_answer -> /tfw-handoff TASK REF
Complete:   Executor saves RF -> RF_READY -> Coordinator reads RF
            -> /tfw-review TASK -> Reviewer saves REVIEW -> REVIEW_READY
            -> Coordinator reads REVIEW -> authorized next act or owner decision
```

These are illustrative flows under the approved target contract. They do not activate an Executor or approve a TS.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

1. ❌ Fixed messages suppress questions, evidence, adverse findings, verdicts or grounds from their owned records; signal receipt is treated as approval or a completion verdict.
2. ❌ Terminology creates a new pause/role, lets a role cross its boundary or resolve its own missing authority, or removes independent judgment or a reserved human decision.
3. ❌ A second normative catalogue survives, a sender can append “necessary” substantive prose, or inaccessible references require fabricated records or repeated message loops.
4. ❌ Research's stage dialogue changes implicitly; economics changes, a runtime protocol service, topology redesign, subagent mode or permanent tests enter scope.
5. ❌ Saint-Exupéry becomes optional, loses a protected value or permits presenting a known materially deficient HL/TS; the final consistency pass is skipped or claimed from a pre-change Candidate.
6. ❌ The work rewrites accepted FLC, reads foreign unreturned work/transcripts, changes consumers, pushes/publishes, or claims guaranteed compliance, zero bias or measured savings without evidence.

**On failure:** Preserve valid work and evidence; record the exact violated claim and route the required decision. Do not fix a scope defect through an improvised chat exception.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Substance in records, signals in inter-role messages.** Preserve inspection and independent judgment while removing conversational retelling.
2. **A checkpoint has an explicit consequence.** A check, a wait and a role return must not look like the same instruction.
3. **One semantic owner, explicit use sites.** The catalogue is shared; a workflow names the form it fires; a template supplies the evidence fields. Repeating a token at its use site is useful, repeating its whole contract is drift.
4. **Constrain transfer, preserve necessary freedom.** The protocol closes message forms, not reasoning or in-scope implementation methods. Human planning and the separate Research flow keep their substance.
5. **Mandatory simplification preserves value.** Saint-Exupéry remains a pre-presentation requirement with a material failure consequence, independent of its label.
6. **Names guide, evidence decides.** Exact phrases expose deviations; they do not guarantee compliance or remove bias from authored artifacts.
