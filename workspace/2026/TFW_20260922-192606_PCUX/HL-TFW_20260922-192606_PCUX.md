## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

The Coordinator offers practical, provider-specific ways to organize and finish TFW work in Codex, Claude Code, and Antigravity while the same task-local contract remains understandable on every platform. A small one-phase task can run the full TFW role cycle through platform subagents when each role's skill, evidence, return, and independent acceptance requirements can actually be met; otherwise the Coordinator explains the exact owner action needed and continues from durable traces.

**Impact:** The owner sees the available autonomy and navigation options before choosing a route, receives fewer unnecessary interruptions, and can resume from TFW artifacts rather than another agent's conversation.

> “The Coordinator tells me what this tool can do now, offers a workable route for this task, and brings me back only for decisions that are mine.”

## 3. Target State (To-Be) 🔒 FROZEN

Three concise provider profiles sit under the respective `.tfw/adapters/` directories. The relevant persistent adapter directs the Coordinator to its profile at the Plan coordination gate. Each profile turns live capability inspection into a concrete offer: available navigation, role provisioning, addressed returns, autonomy suitable for the task, owner actions, and honest unavailable states. Profiles contain provider mechanics; the core retains role locks, authority, lifecycle, transcript isolation, and acceptance rules.

The Codex offer includes optional grouping of this task's known role sessions into one Section. Claude Code and Antigravity offers include a bounded compact route for small one-phase work if research establishes that a main Coordinator can launch subagents as complete Researcher, Executor, and Reviewer role units, each with its own exact `/tfw-*` activation, Role Lock, trace, return, and independent verdict. If that relation cannot be established, the profiles offer the best valid owner-assisted route and state the remaining human action plainly. Browser access, model suitability, creation, messaging, continuation, and title support are checked on the active surface before they are claimed. The compact route accounts for the Coordinator's context cost and chat noise.

## 5. Definition of Done (DoD) 🔒 FROZEN

- ✅ 1. A Coordinator on each of the three platforms reaches only its matching profile at the coordination checkpoint and can render a concrete offer from the mechanisms exposed in that session.
- ✅ 2. Codex can offer grouping of only this task's known role sessions in one Section; grouping failure does not change task state or authority.
- ✅ 3. Each Claude Code and Antigravity profile makes an evidence-backed decision: where the required capabilities exist, it offers a bounded small one-phase option in which subagents run exact TFW role skills, own their required artifacts, return through gates, and preserve independent review and closure; where capabilities are absent, it offers owner-assisted visible sessions without claiming a complete compact cycle.
- ✅ 4. No provider path uses another role's transcript, terminal, hidden reasoning, or unreturned work as coordination evidence; status, addressed gates, and returned artifacts are sufficient to continue.
- ✅ 5. The source profiles, adapter entry points, installed copies, and receiver update route agree; observed behavior is distinguished from untested platform claims.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. A provider profile grants authority, changes a Role Lock, or treats a UI affordance as activation.
- ❌ 2. Hidden subagents are counted as independently addressable TFW roles or an independent Reviewer without an approved and evidenced change to that contract.
- ❌ 3. A Coordinator reads, searches, resumes, or summarizes another active unit's conversation, terminal, logs, or unreturned work to monitor or direct it.
- ❌ 4. A capability report repeats a dated platform assertion as current fact without checking the active surface, including Antigravity title support and Claude subagent browser access.
- ❌ 5. Section grouping moves unrelated tasks or becomes a condition for substantive TFW work.
- ❌ 6. The profiles require a new maintained runtime registry, extra mandatory user interruptions, or provider-specific changes to the common authority model without an explicit decision.

**On failure:** Preserve the observed limitation, return the contested design to the Coordinator/owner decision route, and withhold the unsupported autonomy claim.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Core authority, local ergonomics** — Provider guidance describes how to use current mechanisms; task-local TFW state and human authority decide what work is valid.
2. **Capability claims are dated and scoped** — Observe the exact surface at the relevant gate, distinguish available from demonstrated, and state the limit of a test.
3. **Selected trace over transcript** — Progress and continuation use addressed messages, status, and returned artifacts; private in-progress conversations are outside the coordination surface.
4. **Proportional autonomy** — Offer a compact full-cycle path only for a bounded small one-phase task when uncertainty, tool needs, independent review, owner decisions, and the Coordinator's context load fit the proven route.
5. **Human-visible navigation** — Titles and Sections help the owner locate role work; failure to organize the UI never fabricates status or blocks the task.

## 10. RESEARCH Case 🟢 FREE

### Blind Spots

- The exact Helpdesk task, model, subagent permissions, role artifacts, independent review, and closure evidence behind the reported small-task success are not yet selected or verified.
- It is not yet established whether Claude Code or Antigravity subagents can satisfy Full TFW's direct-address, continuation, and independent-return requirements, or which exact core rule would need to change to admit them without weakening the cycle.
- The current Antigravity creation/title surface and Claude subagent browser permissions may differ from recent owner observations.

### Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Status |
|---|------------|--------|
| H1 | Subagents can run separate `/tfw-*` role skills with attributable artifacts, bounded gate returns, independent review, and valid closure despite their nested session topology. The result determines whether and how the common role-unit rule can be amended. | open |
| H2 | Claude and Antigravity expose enough current subagent capability for some small tasks, but browser use, continuation, or other tool-dependent checks may require owner-assisted visible sessions. The result determines each offer and fallback. | open |
| H3 | Three profiles can be loaded at Plan Step 5 and shipped through init/update with one lightweight adapter pointer per provider. The result determines the file and routing topology. | open |
| H4 | Codex Sections can group only this task's known role sessions without treating section membership as a source of authority. The result determines the grouping instructions. | open |

### Risks of Not Researching

The project could ship an autonomy claim that hides a missing independent Reviewer, or omit a usable compact route because an older provider observation was treated as permanent.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

1. **Gather:** inspect current adapter and core constraints, installed provider mechanisms, and the exact Helpdesk field trace if available; keep source, host, model, and date attached.
2. **Extract:** compare full visible-role chains and full role cycles through subagents against role address, skill activation, gate, artifact, review, browser, context cost, and closure needs.
3. **Challenge:** test the smallest valid route on each provider, including negative cases for transcript isolation, absent title support, missing browser access, and Section grouping scope.

### Why Not Just...?

- Why not add more prose to the three root adapter files? — It would keep discovery simple but increase always-loaded text and mix evolving UX guidance with entry routing. Three selected profiles cost more files and an exact loading step; research must verify that the net behavior improves.
- Why not mandate separate visible sessions everywhere? — That preserves the current role contract but may impose unnecessary owner clicks on a small task. Research must price this against the evidence, context, and independence cost of full TFW roles carried by subagents.
- Why not accept the Helpdesk success as proof? — It is valuable field input, but task completion, review independence, and provider-wide reliability are different claims.
