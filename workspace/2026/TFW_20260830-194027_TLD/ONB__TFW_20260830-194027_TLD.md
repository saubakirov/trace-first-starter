## 1. Understanding

`TECH_DEBT.md` leaves the project root and is sealed, unread, as `tasks/DEBT-SNAPSHOT.md` beside the
Task Board retired five days earlier. Every canonical surface that writes, reads, installs or names the
registry stops doing so, and `review.md` gains a disposition gate in place of its second write: each
captured item is paid as a phase, promoted to a task, or ruled not material on the record, before the task
can close. Receiving projects get prose in `.tfw/migrations/2.0.0.md` that lets their own agent do the same
move — carrying the goal, the value and the problem the file represented, not only the steps — with one
carve-out for safety rules written after an incident, routed through a question to the user. Nothing is
created to replace the registry: the net count of maintained project artifacts falls by one and rises by
none. The task ships as one release on the 2.x line, and this repository is its own first consumer.
