## 2. Key Decisions

1. **The seal is a `git mv`, not a delete-plus-create.** Byte-identity is then a fact the log can show
   rather than a claim in a header, and `--follow` keeps 76 commits of history. Approved as ONB
   Recommendation 1. Proof recorded twice: `md5sum` equality before the header, zero `diff` after it.
2. **Manifest row 9 was deleted rather than repointed** — TS AC-6 bullet 2 as amended after the ONB. The
   task-container glob already compiles `tasks/DEBT-SNAPSHOT.md`; keeping row 9 would have compiled one
   source to two URLs and left a manifest row nothing needs. `BOARD-SNAPSHOT.md` set the precedent.
3. **The disposition gate is written as prose in `review.md` and a column in `REVIEW.md`.** No new file,
   template, script, check or configuration key — the DoF 5 line. Format lives in the template, the rule
   and the test live in the workflow, which is the §11 design split.
4. **The existence test is the part an executor could not infer, so it is written out.** *A disposition
   names something that already exists.* The coordinator's answer to ONB Q1 supplied it; the corpus
   confirmed why it is needed (see §3 AC-4).
5. **§5's heading keeps the words *Tech Debt Collected*.** Every REVIEW file already written carries them
   and the replacement search matches on them. Renaming would have made the search miss the entire corpus
   — judge row 9's *document anchor* case, caught before it shipped.
6. **The `TD-{N}` link stays page-level, not anchor-level.** It pointed at the page before; it points at
   the page now. Deepening it to `#td-59` would have been an unrequested improvement with a new failure
   mode. Row anchors still generate on the snapshot, so a hand-written `#td-59` keeps working exactly as
   it did.
7. **Two un-gated implications found by the dry run were closed in the prose, not just reported.** The
   move mechanism and the source revision. Both fixes are clauses, not commands — AC-7's *nothing to run*
   holds.
8. **`AGENTS.md` was not edited.** Neither it nor `.tfw/adapters/codex/AGENTS.md.template` ever named the
   registry, so AC-10's `TFW:CODEX` clause is satisfied by a no-op. Stated rather than manufactured, per
   the coordinator's ruling on ONB inconsistency 2.
9. **The migration step went in at 6, renumbering old 6 and 7.** The consistency check stays last and now
   sees the finished tree; every numeric cross-reference in the guide points at steps 2, 3a or 5, so
   nothing broke.
10. **Line endings were normalized back to LF after the edits.** Python's `write_text` silently produced
    CRLF on Windows across 38 files. Caught by `git`'s own warning, fixed, and `cmp` re-run — see §4.
