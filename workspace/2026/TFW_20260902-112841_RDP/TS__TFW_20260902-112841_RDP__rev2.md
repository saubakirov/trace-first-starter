## 1. Why this revision exists

**The task's true goal was that nobody guesses what to do after a review. It was not achieved, and the
reason is mechanical.**

Revision 1 built *the reviewer marks and proposes, the coordinator rules* — **for dispositions.** It did
not build it for **the round itself.** So the round's order — which items are commanded, on what basis,
after whom — was written by the coordinator into `REVIEW` §4 and §5: **the reviewer's artifact.** The
coordinator has no artifact of its own recording that a round was ordered.

Every symptom of 2026-09-02 descends from that one misplacement:

```text
  the round's order sits in the reviewer's file
        │
        ├─► the executor needs a detector to find it        ← rev-1 item 6, a patch
        ├─► status.md cannot say a round is open            ← rev-1 item 7, a patch
        ├─► the owner cannot see the round in the directory
        └─► the coordinator explains it in chat, and chat does not persist
```

**Move the order into the TS and all four fall away.** The executor is *already* obliged to read the TS —
it is the declared input of `handoff.md` and the obligation is held by Role Lock. A round's order placed
there cannot be missed without failing to read what must be read. That is the structural guarantee this
revision delivers, and it needs no detector, no new field and no new entity.

**It is inside the frozen contract.** HL deliverable 8 requires *"revision artifacts named: which artifact
is appended to, which gets a sibling, which is never overwritten."* Revision 1 named the RF and the REVIEW
and skipped the **TS** — the one of the three the coordinator owns. This revision finishes deliverable 8.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

### AC-13: The round's order is the coordinator's artifact, and it is the TS

- [ ] A revision round is ordered by a **TS revision** — `TS__{ID}__rev{N}.md`, a **sibling**; the earlier
      revision is never edited
- [ ] **The highest-numbered TS revision governs.** Stated once, in `conventions.md` §3, so two files can
      never both be live
- [ ] The TS revision states: the round number, **who ordered it and on what basis**, the prior review it
      answers, the items ordered, **what is not re-done**, and the budget standing
- [ ] `review.md` Step 6 no longer writes a bound into the REVIEW. The reviewer **proposes** items in its
      own file and returns the work to the coordinator
- [ ] `templates/TS.md` carries the round header, so a revision cannot be written without those fields

write an ordered bound.
Evidence: **This round is its own evidence.** This file is `TS__…__rev2.md`, written by the coordinator,

under it.

### AC-14: After a review, no role guesses  [depends: AC-13]

The guarantee the owner asked for, stated as a chain in which each step is an existing obligation:

- [ ] **Reviewer** finishes → `conventions.md` §15 tells it: state the verdict, that the items are
      **proposals**, and **return the work to the coordinator**. It does not order a round
- [ ] **Coordinator** receives → `plan.md` names the post-review act as exactly two writes: promote what
      needs its own contract, **each with a PROPOSAL**; and write the TS revision. Then its own Hard Stop
      entry sends the work on
- [ ] **Executor** receives → reads the TS, **which it is already obliged to read**, and finds the round
      there. No detector, because nothing is hidden
- [ ] **Reviewer of round N** → reviews `RF__…__rev{N}` against `TS__…__rev{N}`, and writes
      `REVIEW__…__rev{N}`
- [ ] Every one of those four steps is a **write into the role's own artifact.** No role writes into
      another's, which is what happened on 2026-09-02 and is the defect this closes
- [ ] The round count is visible by listing the directory — TS, RF and REVIEW each carry `rev{N}`

Gate: walk the chain on paper from a REVISE verdict to the next review, naming for each step the file read
and the file written. Any step where a role must infer, ask, or read another role's artifact fails this AC.
Evidence: Walk it for **this** round, and record it: which file each role read, which it wrote, and where
the coordinator's order lives. State plainly whether a reader who was not in the conversation could follow
it.

### AC-15: The artifact grammar for a round is complete  [depends: AC-13]

- [ ] `conventions.md` §3 states, for each artifact: **TS — sibling per revision. RF — appended, `§4.N`

## 6. Technical Guidance

- **`PROPOSAL__TFW-58__revise_protocol` measured this exact loss**: *"the TS was overwritten in place;
  revisions 2 → 3 → 4 recorded only as header prose — no way to diff what the executor was told between
  rounds."* The sibling rule is the answer to a finding three weeks old.
- **The pattern is already in the corpus** — `REVIEW__…__rev2.md` and `REVIEW__…__rev3.md` are how the
  owner used to read a round count off a directory listing. This extends it to the TS, which is the file
  that carries the order.
- **Do not add a detector, a field, or a status.** Every one of those was a patch on the misplacement.
- **`handoff.md`'s return section stays** — its read-order, its *what is not re-done*, its *what is not
  yours*. Only the "find the newest REVIEW" step goes, replaced by "read the current TS revision".

## 7. Definition of Failure

- ❌ The coordinator's order is written into any artifact the coordinator does not own.
- ❌ Two TS revisions are simultaneously live, or an earlier revision is edited.
- ❌ A detector, field, status value or artifact type is added to compensate for placement.
- ❌ Any step of AC-14's chain requires a role to infer, ask, or read another role's artifact.
- ❌ `.tfw/scripts/` is touched. DoF 1 stands.
- ❌ The round count cannot be read from a directory listing.
