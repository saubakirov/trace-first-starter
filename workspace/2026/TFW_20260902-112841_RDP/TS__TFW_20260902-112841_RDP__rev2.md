## 1. Why this revision exists

**The task's goal was that after a review nobody guesses what to do. It was not achieved, and the reason is
mechanical rather than a matter of wording.**

Revision 1 built *the reviewer marks and proposes, the coordinator rules* — **for dispositions.** It did
not build it for **the round itself.** So the round's order — which items are commanded, on what basis,
after whom — was written by the coordinator into `REVIEW` §4 and §5: **the reviewer's artifact.** The
coordinator had no artifact of its own in which a round exists.

Every symptom of 2026-09-02 descends from that one misplacement:

```text
  the round's order sits in the reviewer's file
        │
        ├─► the executor needs a detector to find it        ← rev-1 item 6, a patch
        ├─► status.md cannot say a round is open            ← rev-1 item 7, a patch
        ├─► the owner cannot see the round in the directory  ← «раньше я видел rev1 rev2, теперь не вижу»
        └─► the coordinator explains it in chat, and chat does not persist
```

**Move the order into a TS revision and all four fall away.** The executor is *already* obliged to read the
TS — it is the declared input of `handoff.md`, held by Role Lock. A round's order placed there cannot be
missed without failing to read what must be read. No detector, no new field, no new entity.

**And it is now inside the contract rather than inside my reading of it.** A14 says so in §12, and A13
removed the instrument that made this round look like an exception to its own rule.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

### AC-13: The round's order is the coordinator's own artifact, and it is a TS revision

- [ ] A round is ordered by a **TS revision** — a **sibling** file; the earlier revision is never edited
- [ ] **The highest-numbered TS revision governs.** Stated once, in `conventions.md` §3, so two files can
      never both be live
- [ ] The TS revision states: the round, **who ordered it**, the prior review it answers, **each item's
      basis**, and what is not re-done
- [ ] `review.md` Step 6 no longer writes an ordered bound into the REVIEW. The reviewer **proposes** items
      in its own file and returns the work to the coordinator
- [ ] **The basis cell is the enforcement site for A13's citation bar** — an item that cites nothing has no
      cell to sit in. This is why the two amendments are one delivery and not two

Gate: read `conventions.md` §3 and `review.md` Step 6 against the five points; confirm Step 6 contains no
instruction to write an ordered bound.
Evidence: **this round is its own evidence.** This file is a TS revision written by the coordinator,
ordering nine items, each with a named basis. Record that in the RF.

### AC-14: After a review, no role guesses  [depends: AC-13]

The guarantee the owner asked for, stated as a chain in which every step is **a write into the role's own
artifact** — because writing into another role's artifact is the defect this closes:

- [ ] **Reviewer** finishes → `conventions.md` §15 tells it: state the verdict, state that the items are
      **proposals**, and **return the work to the coordinator.** It does not order a round and does not
      dispatch an executor
- [ ] **Coordinator** receives → §15's coordinator entry names the post-review act as exactly two writes:
      promote what needs its own contract, each with a PROPOSAL; and write the TS revision. `plan.md`
      points here in one line, so both halves of the handshake live in one file and cannot disagree
- [ ] **Executor** receives → reads the TS, **which it is already obliged to read**, and finds the round
      there. No detector, because nothing is hidden
- [ ] **Reviewer of round N** → reviews the RF's new section against `TS__…__rev{N}`, and writes
      `REVIEW__…__rev{N}`
- [ ] The round is visible **by listing the directory** — the TS and the REVIEW carry `rev{N}`
- [ ] **Who takes the round is deliberately not regulated, and `conventions.md` §5 says why**: the order may
      go to the same executor or to a fresh one, and the same for the reviewer, **because if a fresh
      executor cannot carry out the round from the artifacts alone, the order is incomplete.** Continuity of
      context is an optimization whoever dispatches may take; requiring it would move state into a session,
      and sessions do not persist

Gate: walk the chain on paper from a REVISE verdict to the next review, naming for each step the file read
and the file written. Any step where a role must infer, ask, or read another role's artifact fails this AC.
Evidence: walk it for **this** round and record it — which file each role read, which it wrote, and where
the coordinator's order lives. State plainly whether a reader who was not in the conversation could follow
it.

### AC-15: The revision grammar is stated once, and it generates all four artifacts  [depends: AC-13]

- [ ] `conventions.md` §3's naming table gains the revision forms, single-phase and phase:
      `TS__{ID}__rev{N}.md` · `REVIEW__{ID}__rev{N}.md` · `TS__phase-{x}__{title}__rev{N}.md`
- [ ] **The unsuffixed file is revision 1 and is never renamed.** No retroactive rename, ever
- [ ] **An ordinal is the only suffix the grammar admits**, and the rule says why: a *title* suffix
      duplicates what `status.md` already holds, so it creates two facts that must agree — an ordinal lives
      nowhere else, so the filename is its only home. The existing bar on title suffixes is left standing
- [ ] **One line generates the rest** — *sibling where exactly one must govern; appended where the record is

      **TS** sibling, highest governs · **REVIEW** sibling, highest is the live verdict · **RF** appended,

- [ ] The RF rule cites its measurement: `PROPOSAL__TFW-58__revise_protocol` — *"the TS was overwritten in

- [ ] **The round cycle is drawn once** in `conventions.md` §5 — REVIEW → TS revision → RF section → REVIEW
      of the round — with the three exits from the second verdict: approve · cite and order again · return
      to `owner`

Gate: `conventions.md` §3 read against the six points; the §5 diagram read against the four artifacts.

## 7. Definition of Failure

- ❌ The coordinator's order is written into an artifact the coordinator does not own.
- ❌ Two TS revisions are simultaneously live, or an earlier revision is edited.
- ❌ A detector, field, status value, template block, artifact type or configuration key is added.
- ❌ Any step of AC-14's chain requires a role to infer, to ask, or to read another role's artifact.
- ❌ The citation bar ships without its enforcement site — a rule stated in prose that no cell holds.
- ❌ `max_revision_cycles` survives anywhere outside `workspace/` and `tasks/`.
- ❌ `review.md` Steps 4–6 end above 483 words, or `Anti-patterns` above 163. **After AC-16 this is
  strictly easier than it was**, so missing it means words were added rather than moved.
- ❌ `.tfw/scripts/` is touched. DoF 1 stands, and A13 restored it to an absolute.
- ❌ The round cannot be read off a directory listing.
