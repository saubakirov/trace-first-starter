## 1. Why this round exists, and why it is the last one I can foresee

Round 2 delivered both owner amendments and the five repairs round 1 ordered. What is left is small and
one part of it is not a judgement call: **the project's own declared build gate is red.**

`docs/scripts/test_integration.py:938` asserts that nothing may follow `{ID}` in an artifact filename. That
was an absolute until A14 admitted exactly one suffix, and all twelve failing cases are the form
`conventions.md` §4 now **mandates**. So the canon and its own check disagree, the check is the side that is
wrong, and a task cannot close — nor 2.1.0 be tagged — with its declared gate failing.

The other five are one line each, and two of them are mine rather than the executor's finding.

**Why I can say this is the last round, and what would make me wrong.** The citation bar is the test: a
round exists exactly while a breached condition can be named. After this round the citable set is empty by
enumeration, not by hope —

```text
  REVIEW rev2's four proposals      ──►  ordered here as items 1-4
  the two I added when ruling       ──►  ordered here as items 5-6
  the four `not material` rows      ──►  three barred by DoF 8 / DoD 10 / §7.1, one not owed
  frozen claims needing change      ──►  none. The review proposed no amendment
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  what remains citable after item 6 ──►  nothing, unless this round breaches something itself
```

If round 3 does breach something, that is a new citation and a fourth round is legitimate. If it does not,
the next verdict is ✅ APPROVE with the remainder disposed — which is where the loop is supposed to end.
