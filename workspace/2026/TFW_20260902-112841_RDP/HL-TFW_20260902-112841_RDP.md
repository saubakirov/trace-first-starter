## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

A review produces two kinds of output — work ordered now, and work not done — and both become decisions by
a stated rule. One question decides whether a finding deserves work, and it is the project's own question:
*does leaving this undone damage purpose, inspectability, authority or continuation?* One role rules on
each kind of output, named before the autonomy is granted. And the loop ends in a decision rather than in
exhaustion: a third round is not a third attempt, it is the signal that the specification was wrong.

**Impact:** A reviewer stops inventing a private standard for each finding, and a coordinator stops
discovering at release time that the loop never closed. The word *revision* exists in the canon with a
meaning. A returning executor has a door to come back through. And `review.md` is shorter than it is
today while carrying all of this — because what was missing was terms, not paragraphs.

> "I stopped arguing about whether a finding matters and started answering one question about it. The
> loop closed twice and stopped, and the third time it stopped us instead."

### 2.9 The gap was in no hypothesis: `🔄 REVISE` has one door and `❌ REJECT` has three

Found by research iteration 1, in `conventions.md` §5:

| Verdict | Where the canon lets it go |
|---|---|
| ❌ REJECT | **three** routes — `HL_DRAFT` (rework the HL) · `RES` (new research) · `TS_DRAFT` (rewrite the TS) |
| 🔄 REVISE | **one** — *"back to execution (same task)"* |

A reviewer holding a finding that **only the coordinator can discharge** — one needing a TS change — has
no door that is not REJECT. So they write it into `### If REVISE — items to fix:`, a list **only the
executor reads**, and the executor may not amend a TS. The item survives every round.

```text

        │
        ▼
  no route exists  ──►  writes it into "items to fix"
                              │
                              ▼
                        only the executor reads that list
                              │
                              ▼
                        the executor may not amend a TS
                              │
                              ▼
                        the item comes back next round, unchanged
                        AFD-48 phase-a: rev2 · rev3 · rev4
                        "Obtain coordinator amendments" ×3, never logged
```

**And there is a within-task control, which is stronger than a second example.** `ai-first-devices` task
AFD-48, same reviewer, same week, same framework version, two rung-2 situations, two endings:

| | `phase-a` | `phase-b` · S3 Block 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Where the rung-2 item went | *"Items to fix"* — a list only the executor reads | an artifact **addressed to the coordinator** |

The variable is not the finding's difficulty and not the reviewer. **It is whether anything carried the item

```text
        │ value: proof that what was missing was terms, not paragraphs
        ▼

```

| Step | Input | Transformation | Value created |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ask | a finding | one NS1 question, asked once | the answer is checkable by someone who was not there |
| Route | an ordered fix | the ladder: TS-satisfiable → executor; TS change → coordinator; frozen → owner | authority is exercised only where it was granted |
| Rule | a deferred item | the coordinator's single act at review close | new work exists only when someone accountable said so |
| Terminate | a second return | accept-and-name, or reject-and-replan | the loop cannot consume a task quietly |
| Re-enter | a returning executor | a stated door, and a preserved predecessor artifact | the trace survives the round that corrected it |
| Compress | the files touched | the missing term replaces the paragraph standing in for it | the canon carries more and weighs less |

## 4. Phases 🔒 FROZEN

**Single phase, and deliberately no more.** The three mechanisms are one release surface: they land in
`review.md`, `handoff.md`, `conventions.md` §5 and §15, and the review templates — the same files, in the
same pass, in the same version.

**The owner ruled out a reserve phase on 2026-09-02, on grounds worth recording:** *«не будем себе стелить
соломку, это даст возможность агентам как бы расслабиться и не учесть ценности сразу»* — a phase held in
reserve is permission to defer accounting for values, and an agent that has a later round will use it. The
coordinator had argued the structural case for the same conclusion; the owner's reason is the stronger one
and is the one on record.

**Declared outcome:** every finding a review produces carries one stated basis, one named decider and one
terminating rule; a returning executor has an entry point; the artifacts of a revision round are named as
appended, sibling or untouched; and `review.md` ends at or under its own budget with all of it added.

**Deliverables:**

1. **The axis**, stated once, derived from `NS1` and cited to it: *does leaving this undone damage
   purpose, inspectability, authority or continuation?* It names **which harms count** and it replaces
   the contradiction in §2.4 — the entry filter tests whether an observation is real, and materiality is
   decided once, at disposition. *(Amended by A4: the four words name the axis; they are not the test.
   Scored adversarially against the complete nine-row population, all nine pass them — in a project whose
   product is documentation, every defect is an inspectability defect. A test that admits everything is
   DoF 3.)*
2. **The test is a named consequence, and the ruling states it.** What decided all nine real rulings was a
   named consequence or its named **absence** — *"renaming would break every existing citation"*,
   *"neither is edited again, so nothing can drift"*. So: `not material` names what will not happen, and
   it can be wrong later and be seen to be wrong; *"low priority"* cannot be checked and is not
   admissible. **And the ruling names which question it answers** — *not owed*, or *owed and forbidden to
   pay*, the clause that forbids it cited. On three of nine rows the item **was** owed and the fix was
   forbidden by a frozen acceptance criterion, by a DoF item, or by a named cost; filing those as
   `not material` makes the record say the reviewer thought it did not matter while they argued at length
   that it did. **No fourth outcome** — three stay. *(Amended by A5.)*
3. **The routing ladder needs a landing site, not better prose**, and every site already exists.
   `conventions.md` §5 gives ❌ REJECT a three-way route and gives 🔄 REVISE exactly one destination, so a
   reviewer holding a finding only the coordinator can discharge has no door that is not REJECT — §2.9.

   **The rung is a property of an item; `lifecycle` is a property of the task.** A REVISE carrying rung-1
   and rung-2 items together — the ordinary case, measured on AFD-48 rev2 at four and two — has no
   representable state if the rung is delivered by a lifecycle value: `status.md` holds one closed
   `lifecycle` field and `conventions.md` §5 is single-valued. So:

   - **rung 1** → execution, unchanged;
   - **rung 2** → `pending — coordinator` **in the REVIEW row, beside the item** — one channel per item —
     and `lifecycle: TS_DRAFT` is set **only when the TS is actually changed**, which is one act per round
     rather than one per item;
   - **rung 3** → an `amendment_escalated` journal event plus HL §12.

   Every site is already shipped — the `pending — {role}` state by TLD on 2026-08-30, the lifecycle states
   and the event kind by the canon: **zero new entities.** Mirrored in `review.md`, which uses the route
   rather than restating it. *(Refined from research A3, then corrected by research A6 after a replay of a
   real rung-2 finding showed the lifecycle delivery unimplementable. Both classified by the coordinator
   under `conventions.md` §3 rule 6 — see §12's note.)*
4. **Disposition authority moves to the coordinator** — one act at the close of review, not one per item.
   `review.md` Step 5 has the reviewer mark and propose; `conventions.md` §15 names acceptance authority
   so the grant is visible where the role is defined. **The site of that act is the disposition cell**, and
   `pending — coordinator` is how an item waits in it — the same shape TLD shipped as `pending — owner`, a
   pending state and not a fourth outcome. Two of the three outcomes **cannot be stamped**, because TLD
   made a disposition name an artifact that must exist: `paid — phase-x` is a directory that is there or is
   not, `promoted — {TASK-ID}` a task directory with its own `status.md` created then and there. The
   residual is `not material`, where eight of nine real rulings landed; A4 and A5 harden it and §9 names
   what is left. *(Refined from research A9(a).)*
5. **A revision is defined**, in the canon, using the word: repair of what was already specified. Work
   that cannot be accepted under the existing TS is new work and takes a new TS.
6. **Termination fires on an unchanged item, with two rounds as the ceiling.** *A round that returns an
   item the previous round already ordered is the terminating round* — the verdict must be ✅ APPROVE
   naming what remains, or ❌ REJECT to planning. Stated with its consequence, not as advice.
   *(Amended by A2. The round-count trigger and its stated rationale were refuted by this task's own
   corpus: four of five substantive deep rounds corrected real **work**, and two reviewers wrote into a
   round-3 verdict that the specification was right — so a round cap would have forbidden four rounds

   diagnosis. The unchanged item is the signal with a cause: a punt that survives a round is a finding

7. **A re-entry point in `handoff.md`** for an executor returning after a REVISE: what to read, what not

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **The axis is NS1's; the test is a named consequence.** Which harms count is quoted from the north star
   and cited to it — that citation is what makes a ruling refusable by someone who was not there. What
   decides an individual finding is the consequence the ruling names, or the named absence of one. A
   locally invented axis is what §2.4 produced eight times; an axis mistaken for a test is what admits all
   nine. *(Amended by A4.)*
2. **Authority is named before autonomy is granted.** NS2 principle 4. A role that rules on something the
   role definition does not mention is a grant nobody made.
3. **Proportional routing.** NS2 principle 6. The majority case pays nothing; only a change of
   specification or of contract costs an escalation.
4. **A loop ends in a decision, never in exhaustion.** NS2 principle 5 — finish with an inspectable result
   and an authoritative next step or an explicit close.
5. **A ruling states a prediction.** A refusal that cannot later be shown wrong is not a ruling, it is a
   preference. This is what makes `not material` honest rather than cheap.
6. **Subtraction is the proof.** Three mechanisms arrive and the file gets shorter. Where a paragraph is
   standing in for a missing term, name the term and delete the paragraph — bloat is a terminology failure,
   not a word count.
7. **A rule with no enforcement site is decoration.** `knowledge/process.md` F30. Where a rule can be
   structural — an artifact that must exist, a column that must be filled — it is structural, not an
   exhortation.

   authority, continuation* can be argued for any finding — and find the strongest case that it decides
   nothing.

### Why Not Just…?

- **Why not put the criterion in the template and leave the workflow alone?** A template carries format;
  the decision happens in the workflow, and F30 says capture without an enforcement site changes nothing.
- **Why not count rounds in a control file?** It is a new maintained artifact, which DoF 1 forbids — that
  half of the answer stands. ~~The round is already visible in the artifact names, and that is the
  structural home.~~ **Falsified by §2.10** *(R18)*: a filename is chosen by whoever writes the file, and
  `innoforce-ai-first` ran nine revisions as nine named surfaces with eight TS files and zero
  terminations. There is no structural home for a round count; that is why A7 changes what restarts a
  count rather than where it is kept.
- **Why not route every finding through the coordinator?** DoF 6. It taxes the majority case to fix the
  minority one, and the owner's own practice is the counter-evidence: he took the shortcut for months and
  the ladder says he was right to.
- **Why not raise the word ceiling instead of meeting it?** The ceiling would then have moved twice to
  accommodate the files that breached it, which is how a number stops being a constraint. Re-deriving it
  from the corpus is a legitimate question and it belongs to the compression task, with evidence.
- **Why not leave the reviewer ruling dispositions, since the first use went well?** One reviewer, nine
  rows, same session lineage as the executor. It proves the mechanism can produce reasoned refusals, not
  that the authority was granted.
