## 1. Objective

A review's output becomes decisions by rule. Each finding gets one axis from `NS1`, a named consequence as
its test, and one of five destinations that all already exist. The coordinator rules dispositions in one
act at the close of review. Correction inside an approved phase gets a configured budget — default two TS
revisions — and exhausting it stops the work and returns it to whoever initiated the task, because a loop
that cannot close is evidence about the HL and the agents in the loop are the ones who could not close it.
Six mechanisms arrive and the sections that carry them do not grow.
