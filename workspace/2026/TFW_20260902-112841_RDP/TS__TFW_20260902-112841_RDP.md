## 1. Objective

A review's output becomes decisions by rule. Each finding gets one axis from `NS1`, a named consequence as
its test, and one of five destinations that all already exist. The coordinator rules dispositions in one
act at the close of review. Correction inside an approved phase gets a configured budget — default two TS
revisions — and exhausting it stops the work and returns it to whoever initiated the task, because a loop
that cannot close is evidence about the HL and the agents in the loop are the ones who could not close it.
Six mechanisms arrive and the sections that carry them do not grow.

## 7. Definition of Failure

- ❌ The compressed draft misses 483 or 163 and the mechanism is thinned to fit instead of stopping.
- ❌ A second configuration key, or any new file, template, script or check.
- ❌ A section this task was not authorised to edit is rewritten to free up words.
- ❌ Any owner, person or handle is named in the canon as the escalation target.
- ❌ A journal `kind` is invented for the return.
- ❌ A fourth disposition outcome appears, under any name.
- ❌ A number is hard-coded where the config key should be read.
- ❌ Any file in any project other than this one is created, modified, moved or deleted.
- ❌ A `cmp` mismatch is left on any adapter copy, or a no-op is turned into an edit to tick a box.
- ❌ The scope budget is exceeded without stopping and reporting first.
