First phase of the frozen master contract, opened on owner instruction 2026-09-03. Derivation-only,
per `conventions.md` §3 rules 20–21: no §1, §5, §6 or §7, and no amendment log — an amendment against
a frozen claim goes to the master's §12.

Independent of every open question the research left. That is why it runs first: it needs no
principal, no dispatch grammar and no capability answer, while phases B through E all want a
repository that two sessions can write without corrupting each other's trace.

Two coordinator decisions are recorded in the phase HL so onboarding does not have to ask for them.
The fourth measured corruption — a foreign caller resuming *the last session* wrote into a session
nobody delegated — lands here as a §14 anti-pattern only; its positive rule, that a dispatch captures
the returned session id, belongs to Phase D where dispatch is specified, and the worktree protocol
deliberately claims nothing about a vendor's session store. And explicit-path staging is this phase's
deliverable rather than TFW-61's, which claimed the same words in its own proposal.

`review.md` is 1,699, both already past the ~1200 ceiling `constraint.md` F2 measured, so the staging
rule reaches them only by displacing something, and the RF must name what.

This phase also governs itself: the executor works in its own worktree and stages by exact path from
its first commit, before the rule it is writing exists in the canon.
