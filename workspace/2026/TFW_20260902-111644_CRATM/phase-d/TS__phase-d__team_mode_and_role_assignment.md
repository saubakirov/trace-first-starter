## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Define `AT (Agent Team)` in `conventions.md` §7. Entry requires all three facts: a Role Assignment
  exists, its master HL is owner-approved/frozen, and the freeze baseline is committed. A draft table
  starts nothing. With no table, existing CL remains the default; a separately explicit ordinary AG
  mandate remains valid and unchanged.
- State Coordinator→owner and delegate→Coordinator duties. The Coordinator preserves frozen scope,
  workflow Role Locks, human-rooted authority, direct routing, durable state and same-role continuity.
  A delegate accepts only its row and authoritative sources, stays within the workflow role, and
  reports gates/results directly without using identity metadata as permission.
- Carry the exact seven master §3.1 returns and their channels: self-proposer and reserved amendment
  through §12; Purpose contradiction through `judge.md`; `REJECT` through `review.md`; unavailable
  participant through §12 `SUPERSEDE`; budget excess under rule 19 to the owner; non-human chain by
  pre-work refusal. Degradation is an observable stop/wait or already-authorized CL/AG continuation,
  never a hidden helper, relay, replacement, runtime or weakened trace.
- Add the HL-template Role Assignment before freeze. Required columns are Participant (`team/`
  handle), Workflow role, Scope, Reports to, Semantic channel, and Autonomous from. `Autonomous from`
  is one existing lifecycle id or `—`, selected per task before freeze; the conservative documented
  example uses post-research `TS_DRAFT`, while `HL_DRAFT` begins only at the committed freeze.
- Extend `HL Contract` granularity: one row is one frozen claim; `EXTEND` adds a role row;
  `SUPERSEDE` replaces a participant or another mutually exclusive row value; a row is never deleted.
  A pre-freeze `Autonomous from` choice is ordinary task data. Moving it earlier post-freeze replaces
  the frozen row, widens autonomy, and is not `RESTRICT`; narrowing retains a visible row and follows
  the existing classifier.
- Use the existing task/phase-local `dispatch`: `writer` is the initiating Coordinator; its bounded
  summary names the destination; references point to the Role Assignment and governing TS scope.
  The receiver restates participant, role, reports-to edge, scope, semantic channel and source in its
  existing RES/ONB/REVIEW trace. No new event key, artifact, registry or transcript is introduced.
- At actual checkpoints: Plan declares/validates the table before owner approval and freeze; Handoff
  accepts the Executor row before ONB/work; Research accepts its row before Briefing and reports each
  gate/final RES to its Coordinator; Review accepts an independent Reviewer row before Map and reports
  verdict/proposals to its Coordinator. Non-AT CL/AG paths preserve current behavior and Role Locks.
- Add one Codex adapter profile in the managed root block: every long-lived role is a fresh,
  user-visible, independently addressable task; mutating roles use separate worktrees; coordination
  uses direct create/send/wait operations; the same Executor and Reviewer are reused on returns; no
  fork, subagent, relay or provider switch holds a Role Assignment row. Provider APIs and names stay
  inside the adapter source and its managed receiver.

  preflight, frozen unit, provisioning identity, nonce-bearing direct route, worktree isolation,
