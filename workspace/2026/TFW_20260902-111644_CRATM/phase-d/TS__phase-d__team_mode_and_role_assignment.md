## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Define `AT (Agent Team)` in `conventions.md` §7. Declaration requires all three facts: a Role
  Assignment exists, its master HL is owner-approved/frozen, and the freeze baseline is committed.
  Declaration does not activate row autonomy. A draft table starts nothing. With no table, existing CL
  remains the default; a separately explicit ordinary AG mandate remains valid and unchanged.
- State Coordinator→owner and delegate→Coordinator duties. The Coordinator preserves frozen scope,
  workflow Role Locks, human-rooted authority, direct routing, durable state and same-role continuity.
  A delegate accepts only its row and authoritative sources, stays within the workflow role, and
  reports gates/results directly without using identity metadata as permission.
- Carry the exact seven master §3.1 returns and their channels: self-proposer and reserved amendment
  through §12; Purpose contradiction through `judge.md`; `REJECT` through `review.md`; unavailable
  participant through §12 `SUPERSEDE`; budget decisions through §6, with rule 19 forbidding delegated
  self-acceptance; non-human chain by pre-work refusal. Under §6, necessary growth below the immutable
  multiplier may receive prospective Coordinator approval while all listed invariants stay fixed;
  growth at/above `32/1,280` or from planned zero returns to the owner. Degradation is an observable
  stop/wait or already-authorized CL/AG continuation, never a hidden helper, relay, replacement,
  runtime or weakened trace.
- Add the HL-template Role Assignment before freeze. Required columns are Participant (`team/`
  handle), Workflow role, Scope, Reports to, Semantic channel, and Autonomous from. `Autonomous from`
  is one existing lifecycle id or `—`, selected per task before freeze; the conservative documented
  example uses post-research `TS_DRAFT`, while `HL_DRAFT` activates only after owner approval and the
  committed freeze—not at the first draft. For `TS_DRAFT`, approval of the exact TS is also mandatory.
- Separate AT declaration from row activation. Before activation the Coordinator owns every bounded
  start/continuation decision and the participant reports then waits; owner-only gates remain owner-
  only. After activation the participant may proceed only inside its row scope and Role Lock, while
  all workflow hard stops, explicit TS/HL approvals and seven returns still bind. A subordinate `—`
  never activates by lifecycle and requires direct Coordinator dispatch for every continuation.
  Resolve the boundary from the same task/phase authoritative status plus its ordered journal and the
  existing gate artifact/approval. Re-evaluate on every dispatch/continuation and return. Never compare
  lifecycle ids ordinally: missing transitions, branch ambiguity or task/phase mismatch mean “not
  reached,” followed by stop/report to the Coordinator rather than self-authorization.

  `SUPERSEDE` replaces a participant or another mutually exclusive row value; a row is never deleted.
  A pre-freeze `Autonomous from` choice is ordinary task data. Moving it earlier post-freeze replaces
  the frozen row, widens autonomy, and is not `RESTRICT`; narrowing retains a visible row and follows
  the existing classifier.

  summary names the destination; references point to the Role Assignment and governing TS scope.

  accepts the Executor row before ONB/work; Research accepts its row before Briefing and reports each

  verdict/proposals to its Coordinator. Non-AT CL/AG paths preserve current behavior and Role Locks.

  user-visible, independently addressable task; mutating roles use separate worktrees; coordination
