Master HL written from the proposal plus five owner rulings taken on 2026-09-02, and authority moved
from the proposal to the HL.

What the owner decided, and what it changed in the draft:

- **Five phases, each with its own derivation-only HL**, so later detail stays discussable while
  Phase A proceeds. The master DoD therefore states each phase's outcome and never its
  implementation, keeping later specification inside rule 6 refinement rather than the amendment
  channel.
- **Isolation before discipline.** Phase A was rewritten from a staging rule into a worktree protocol
  built on `git worktree`, plus the landing-commit rule that isolation does not fix. The coordinator
  recorded a disagreement with the owner's initial framing: TFW writes the protocol, git keeps the
  mechanism, because a mechanism of our own would be either code in the repository or a worse copy.
- **Verdict authority is delegable to a named agent principal**, carried by the principal's name so
  two profiles differ by one grant. This replaces TFW-53's guarantee that no frozen claim moves
  without the owner; DoF 8 makes concealing that replacement the failure, not making it.
- **Only a coordinator initiates.** Recorded as a principle, and it removed the acyclicity rule the
  coordinator had planned for Phase C: with every edge starting at a coordinator, a cycle cannot be
  constructed.
- **Research is split by capability**, one iteration per tool measuring its own side, run parallel and
  independent rather than sequential.

Two facts established while drafting, both recorded in §2 as free-section findings rather than
asserted in the contract. `knowledge/constraint.md` F11 — peer sessions are Codex-only — was measured
false: seven peer Claude sessions were enumerable and addressable, though their durability as a
transport was not tested. And the canon carries eleven live references naming sealed TFW-54 as the
task that will deliver a named writer; Phase E owns the sweep.

The contract is not frozen. This event records the draft, not an approval.
