## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

Assisted 1.6 is a faithful lighter edition of TFW, not a separate product and not a redesigned interpretation of the field starter. The edition restores the proven Innoforce 1.0–1.6 lineage as its behavioral source, removes private organizational knowledge and branding through bounded edits, and derives identity and update semantics from the same canonical TFW mechanisms already used by Full.

> “This is not another product. Assisted is part of TFW, only lighter: keep what already works, remove our private knowledge, and use one shared mechanism.”

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Any product file outside `editions/` changes, including `.tfw/`, Full contracts, root guides, or `editions/01-light/`.
- ❌ 2. The implementation starts by editing source-derived files before the complete 28-file disposition ledger is reviewed.
- ❌ 3. A working field paragraph, lifecycle rule, skill algorithm, or template capability is shortened, generalized, or replaced without one of the five permitted hunk classifications in DoD 3.
- ❌ 4. Private Innoforce knowledge, people, project facts, paths, branding, identifiers, or populated defaults enter public files, task evidence intended for release, or rendered templates.
- ❌ 5. `tfw_identity.py`, another executable identity helper, an executable maintenance/update system, lifecycle hooks, or a separate Assisted machine registry ships under another name.
- ❌ 6. Assisted invents a second profile schema, binding location, identity vocabulary, or authority model instead of deriving from Full TFW.
- ❌ 7. The changelog collapses 1.0–1.6 into a synthetic release, presents private overlay history as public fact without qualification, or drops the migration information needed for future updates.
- ❌ 8. Useful field templates are replaced with placeholders, reduced examples, a new visual system, or an unnecessary abstraction instead of bounded neutralization.
- ❌ 9. Forward maintenance overwrites protected downstream content, reverse maintenance directly changes the public edition, or either direction is described as automatic/symmetric synchronization.
- ❌ 10. Any operation writes to the source or adjacent versions on `H:`.
- ❌ 11. An internal subagent performs implementation or review, or the independent review is replaced by the Executor's self-assessment.
- ❌ 12. The phase exceeds its path/LOC budget, requires a Full TFW change, or discovers a material common-mechanism incompatibility and continues without an owner verdict.

**On failure:** stop before further product writes, preserve the task trace and exact diff, return to the owner with the violated criterion, cost of correction, and the smallest considered alternative. Do not improve an unwanted subsystem merely because a reviewer found defects inside it.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Preservation before redesign** — field 1.6 is the behavioral source; a source hunk stays unless a named boundary requires change.
2. **One TFW, proportional editions** — Assisted is a lighter realization of TFW, not a separate product with competing concepts.
3. **Common identity, lighter expression** — Assisted reuses Full participant, accountability, and binding semantics through fewer files and Markdown procedures.
4. **No code in TFW skills** — agent behavior is carried by prompts and ordinary file operations; only the document artifact builder remains executable.
5. **Private overlay stays downstream** — organization knowledge, people, brand, and project state never become public core.
6. **Promotion, not mirroring** — public changes move forward under an exact gate; downstream learning moves back as a reviewed generic candidate.
7. **Visible role sessions** — execution and independent review use owner-visible Codex tasks, not opaque internal subagents.
8. **Subtraction over new machinery** — prefer the existing source files and common TFW contracts; add no manifest, registry, theme layer, or protocol without a unique necessary job.
9. **Human authority at consequential boundaries** — ambiguity, private/public classification, source drift, protected content, and final acceptance stop for explicit human judgment.
10. **Read-only field lineage** — all Innoforce releases are evidence and downstream products, never scratch space for this task.

### Risks of Not Researching

Skipping the bounded comparison could repeat either side of the prior failure: preserve a private/runtime dependency because it looked “field-proven,” or remove a useful algorithm because it looked organization-specific. The research subject is the exact mapping, not a new product design.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

1. **Gather**: produce the 28-file disposition ledger and a paragraph/hunk map for every non-identical source file without copying private content into public artifacts.
2. **Extract**: map field identity/update invariants against the canonical Full contracts and identify only genuine gaps.
3. **Challenge**: attempt to delete every proposed new maintenance artifact and every proposed source rewrite; keep only what has a unique necessary job.

### Why Not Just...?

- Why not copy field 1.6 verbatim? — it contains four private knowledge records, a company asset, populated assumptions, and a separate `people/`/`project_id`/`tfw-assisted` identity model despite already being prompt-only.
- Why not keep the current public result and fix it? — it is the rejected redesign: 26 files / 91,299 bytes versus the 28-file / 297,522-byte source, with zero exact shared-file matches.
- Why not make Assisted fully identical to Full? — proportional editions deliberately differ in artifact weight; mechanisms should share concepts and authority, not force the Full payload into the lighter package.
- Why not build automatic bidirectional synchronization? — it would create code, symmetrical authority, privacy risk, and a new product that the owner did not request.
