## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

Assisted 1.6 is a faithful lighter realization in the TFW family, not a redesigned interpretation of the field starter. Assisted and Full share TFW goals, values, and principles but remain technically independent systems. The edition restores the proven Innoforce 1.0–1.6 lineage as its behavioral source, removes private organizational knowledge and branding through bounded edits, preserves Assisted identity and lifecycle semantics, and uses common container terms only where they improve family-wide clarity without importing Full mechanics.

**Impact:** users regain the complete working Assisted behavior and practical templates that were lost in the rejected implementation; maintainers get one honest Assisted changelog and an explicit, human-gated path for moving universal changes from public TFW to the Innoforce overlay and reviewed generic improvements back.

> “Full and Assisted are different implementations. Their goals, values, and principles are shared; common terms may remain, while the systems themselves must not be merged.”

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Any product file outside `editions/` changes, including `.tfw/`, Full contracts, root guides, or `editions/01-light/`.
- ❌ 2. The implementation starts by editing source-derived files before the complete 28-file disposition ledger is reviewed.
- ❌ 3. A working field paragraph, lifecycle rule, skill algorithm, or template capability is shortened, generalized, or replaced without one of the six permitted hunk classifications in DoD 3.
- ❌ 4. Private Innoforce knowledge, people, project facts, paths, branding, identifiers, or populated defaults enter public files, task evidence intended for release, or rendered templates.
- ❌ 5. `tfw_identity.py`, another executable identity helper, an executable maintenance/update system, lifecycle hooks, a cross-system binding writer, or automatic Full↔Assisted profile conversion ships under another name.
- ❌ 6. Assisted imports Full's profile schema, lifecycle, artifact contract, or path-key identity semantics; or shared `team/`/`workspace/` vocabulary is presented as shared technical authority.
- ❌ 7. The changelog collapses 1.0–1.6 into a synthetic release, presents private overlay history as public fact without qualification, or drops the migration information needed for future updates.
- ❌ 8. Useful field templates are replaced with placeholders, reduced examples, a new visual system, or an unnecessary abstraction instead of bounded neutralization.
- ❌ 9. Forward maintenance overwrites protected downstream content, reverse maintenance directly changes the public edition, or either direction is described as automatic/symmetric synchronization.
- ❌ 10. Any operation writes to the source or adjacent versions on `H:`.
- ❌ 11. An internal subagent performs implementation or review, or the independent review is replaced by the Executor's self-assessment.
- ❌ 12. The phase exceeds its path/LOC budget, requires a Full TFW change, or discovers that safe same-device binding coexistence cannot be achieved within Editions and continues without an owner verdict.

**On failure:** stop before further product writes, preserve the task trace and exact diff, return to the owner with the violated criterion, cost of correction, and the smallest considered alternative. Do not improve an unwanted subsystem merely because a reviewer found defects inside it.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Preservation before redesign** — field 1.6 is the behavioral source; a source hunk stays unless a named boundary requires change.
2. **One methodology, independent realizations** — Assisted and Full share TFW goals, values, and principles while keeping their implementations, lifecycles, and schemas independent.
3. **Shared vocabulary, separate meaning** — `team/` and `workspace/` improve navigation across editions; Assisted retains its own participant roles, project identity, trace model, and binding records.
4. **No code in TFW skills** — agent behavior is carried by prompts and ordinary file operations; only the document artifact builder remains executable.
5. **Private overlay stays downstream** — organization knowledge, people, brand, and project state never become public core.
6. **Promotion, not mirroring** — public changes move forward under an exact gate; downstream learning moves back as a reviewed generic candidate.
7. **Visible role sessions** — execution and independent review use owner-visible Codex tasks, not opaque internal subagents.
8. **Subtraction over new machinery** — prefer the existing Assisted source files and the smallest backward-compatible coexistence layer; add no manifest, registry, theme layer, or protocol without a unique necessary job.
9. **Human authority at consequential boundaries** — ambiguity, private/public classification, source drift, protected content, and final acceptance stop for explicit human judgment.
10. **Read-only field lineage** — all Innoforce releases are evidence and downstream products, never scratch space for this task.

### Why Not Just...?
