## 4. Verdict

**🔄 REVISE**

The product itself passes every independent structural, behavioral, privacy, portability, rendering, regression, and scope check performed. Exact product arithmetic, the 136/205/1,900 preservation ledger, six exact copies, 24-file standalone package, provider/binding fixtures, 9/0 links, 322-test suite, clean control-byte census, and committed renders all hold. The recovered Python escape defect is absent from the final product and is proven by the amended-commit diff.

One criterion remains unproven. TS AC-1 requires pre-implementation and post-implementation manifests of all 28 source files to match on normalized paths, sizes, and SHA-256 values. The frozen research artifact records the full census and disclosed baselines and says all hashes were computed, while the Executor records two equal private-inclusive aggregates around the final commit. But the first durable private-inclusive aggregate appears only at pre-commit verification. For the four private rows, no exact pre-implementation value exists in the trace. Static no-write confinement is strong causal evidence; it is not the historical manifest comparison the accepted criterion requires. Calling that exact equality proven would invent evidence.

Public evidence may keep the four private per-row hashes undisclosed; the aggregate, file count, and byte count must cover them. Prove pre/post equality, zero source write targets, and byte/tree equality between the replayed product and `626d77b5…`.
