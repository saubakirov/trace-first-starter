## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Select a regression conclusion in advance, treat an owner's impression as a measurement, or use today's installation/version as proof of the rules governing a past episode.
- ❌ 2. Count an unfinished product task as a failed TFW cycle without examining the selected checkpoint; confuse substantive corrections, approval waits, external dependencies and unproductive circulation.
- ❌ 3. Recommend a cheaper topology by hiding failed work, unequal model/effort, missing usage, lost review independence, missing recovery or unmatched task difficulty.
- ❌ 4. Certify useful memory from record count, Applied/N/A markers, citation presence or invented reconstruction of unseen human context.
- ❌ 5. Expose private sources; inspect unrelated/raw global session state, another active unit's unreturned work or private reasoning; contact third parties or modify the studied projects.
- ❌ 6. Change code, shipped rules/configuration, official role modes or source-project memory; add permanent tests to satisfy diagnostic formality.
- ❌ 7. Continue research without a named unresolved question that can change a conclusion or recommendation; let a missing X1 source or optional measurement create an endless closing obligation.

**On failure:** stop only the affected operation, preserve valid evidence and privacy, record the material gap and responsible next decision, and return through the current route. Inconclusive evidence is an admissible diagnostic outcome when the method and limits are sound; fabricate neither certainty nor acceptance.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Purpose and completion before procedure.** Evaluate meaningful progress and usable decisions, while retaining independent assurance and human authority.
2. **Disconfirm both sides.** Test “TFW regressed” and “the user/agent caused it”; distinguish contradictions from coherent but difficult rules and incorrect operation.
3. **Contemporaneous semantics.** Judge historical work under its actual selected rules and authority; report modern compatibility separately.
4. **Economy includes quality and attention.** Few messages/tokens are a possible benefit, never the sole objective or proof of a better mode.
5. **Useful memory has a use chain.** Preservation, qualification, discoverability, correction and decision use are separate claims.
6. **Selected trace and confidentiality.** Retain the minimum public-safe explanation and exact private references; no archive of private projects or conversations.
7. **Finite, decision-linked inquiry.** Remove redundant control and repeated checks only when purpose, proof and necessary freedom survive.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

Three sequential iterations are the starting plan, respecting the configured minimum of two; this is not a required count of findings or a quota of adverse conclusions.

1. **Establish episodes and historical boundaries (H1–H3).** Gather exact returned artifacts/rules; extract transitions and alternative causes; challenge the claimed common regression and positive comparators.
2. **Compare operating modes and knowledge continuity (H4–H7).** Declare comparable case/checkpoint and knowledge samples before measuring; preserve coverage, model/effort, role separation and source independence; examine old/current human decision routes.
3. **Attempt falsification and integrate (H1–H7).** Independently challenge the strongest draft explanations, check public-safe evidence fidelity and decide whether any remaining gap changes a recommendation.

The Coordinator prepares each complete pending iteration before dispatch and closes it only after a durable return. A fourth or later iteration needs a named material gap and an expected decision; repeating a completed question, searching for X1 sources, polishing wording or collecting optional metrics is not a reason. Parallel inquiry is optional only when independent source/output ownership and the existing workflow support it; no parallel write to a research iteration or hidden role helper.

Measurements include transition/gate progress and repeated returns from available durable records; token categories and compatible native duration with known coverage; human interventions evidenced in selected sources; and material knowledge capture/disposition/retrieval/use denominators. Count messages only where an authorized complete source actually supports the count; artifacts provide a lower bound, never proof that no chatter occurred. No numeric target defines success.
