### E4 — Old and current human decision routes have different visibility

Gather establishes the functional change: a global digest/count trigger and two approval waits become selected producing-role handovers, qualification with actual human/valid-grant authority, source/currentness checks and material closing obligations. The human remains the source of human knowledge and reserved decisions. Existing authority can settle an already-decided effect, while a newly selected or changed claim still needs its real decision.

The newer route puts more weight on the producing role and Coordinator explicitly identifying material content and an honest disposition. It can reduce repeated generic approval and unnecessary topic/marker updates, but those are design possibilities, not measured savings here. Its visible risk is an omitted selection or an unjustified N/A: a final marker alone does not expose missing thought. The old pending count likewise cannot prove that a producer recorded the material insight in the first place. Restoring a count would not, by itself, demonstrate better capture.

A bounded diagnostic question therefore has two parts: **was this insight within an actual material handover/publication obligation, and where did it affect a later permissible decision?** K1–K4 supply different answers rather than a single publication score. H6's regression and H7's general reliability remain open, but a blanket claim that the present mechanism has no human gate or that published knowledge never supports continuation is contradicted by the selected chains.
