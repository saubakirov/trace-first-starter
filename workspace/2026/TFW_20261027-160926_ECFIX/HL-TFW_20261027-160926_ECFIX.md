## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

Claude Code and Antigravity users can evaluate a Daily result or Full task from an explainable account of its actual AI consumption. Agents find their own native sources, bind the task and its contributors, and return usable measurements without a second request to search harder. A genuine unavailable measurement states its checked cause and coverage instead of looking like zero or a completed whole-task account.

**Impact:** Owners spend less time rescuing missing economics and can compare task results against resources with visible limits. Provider-specific knowledge is available where each agent starts its collection, while shared accounting meaning remains consistent.

> **Explicitly imagined stakeholder quote:** “I no longer have to ask twice. I can see which task work was counted, which helpers contributed, and why any part is missing.”

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Documentation presence, an agent's assertion or a synthetic fixture is presented as proof of native agent behavior.
- ❌ 2. Copied history, parent-inclusive counters or cumulative revisions cause unexplained double counting, or task-related helper costs disappear.
- ❌ 3. “No source” lacks a reproducible search/binding account, or valid nonmeasurement is presented as zero/full coverage.
- ❌ 4. Provider-specific instructions are not discoverable from an affected Daily/Full entry, or require the owner to remember a private local path.
- ❌ 5. The result requires a new permanent service, raw chat archive or rewritten history to satisfy ordinary economics collection, or bypasses existing role/privacy boundaries.

**On failure:** Preserve actual observations and last-good records, return the material finding to the Coordinator, and correct within the approved TS. An architecture/scope change or an unavailable acceptance-critical native experiment returns to the owner; it is not silently downgraded to a documentation-only success.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Useful task evaluation:** Link supported resource facts to actual task work; preserve the distinction between API-equivalent valuation and billed subscription cost.
2. **Prove capability on the selected surface:** Paths, version mappings and child/inheritance semantics are observations to test, not provider-wide promises.
3. **One owner per meaning:** Shared accounting remains common; platform discovery and decoding guidance has a clear platform owner and an explicit entry route.
4. **Count work once, preserve unknowns:** Costs survive failure/retry; missing measurement never becomes zero, and overlap never becomes additive by convenience.
5. **Proportional evidence:** Use bounded, decision-changing native probes and temporary verification material. Do not add permanent tests without discussing them with the owner; preserve existing required tests.
6. **Exact access and durable portability:** Bind own or explicitly designated experimental sources; collect numeric evidence without searching unrelated chats or publishing conversations. Keep original sources unchanged and raw working material in the task's system-temporary directory.

- Actual copied-history boundaries and parent/child inclusion semantics on the installed Claude surface.

### Risks of Not Researching

Adding a path that already exists can leave the actual defect untouched. Relaxing the mixed-session guard can make a formerly visible failure into a silently wrong estimate. Treating a provider's private schema or an agent's own explanation as proof can reproduce the same gap under both Daily and Full.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

Two focused iterations, matching configured `min_iterations: 2`. Iteration 1 establishes current native capabilities, loaded instruction routes and source binding; reproduces the relevant failure classes on bounded designated sessions; and asks each native agent how it resolves its own economics source before checking its account against actual files/numeric metadata. Iteration 2 challenges the proposed accounting/discovery model with copied-history, child/helper, repeated-capture, genuine-unavailable and fresh-session Daily/Full cases. It must be decision-linked, not a quota of repeated runs.

Research retains its ordered stages and native boundaries. Official provider documentation may support claims, but native observations decide claims about this installed surface. Tests and raw logs remain temporary; returned research artifacts carry commands/actions, source versions, deciding observations and uncertainty. The exact original external journal is not sought through unrelated history.

### Why Not Just...?

| Approach | Value | Adoption / operating burden | Risks / tradeoffs | Decision and reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Add missing instructions only | Cheapest route if discovery is the sole cause | Small text/distribution change | Existing child path already exists; mixed-history reader behavior may persist | Plausible subset, insufficient as the untested whole answer |
| Verify and repair the existing collection chain | Connects source discovery, correct numbers and useful Daily/Full reporting | Two focused research iterations, bounded corrections and independent native verification | Private formats still require explicit version limits | Recommended; directly tests the reported classes and preserves the current architecture |
| Permanent automatic collector/service | Less reliance on the agent remembering capture | Installation, maintenance and platform integration | Adds a persistent product and failure modes without evidence it is necessary | Not selected; reconsider only with an owner-approved scope change supported by research |

**Leave unchanged:** Owners continue retrying collection manually and cannot reliably distinguish missing data from a failed search or duplicated work.

**Critical challenge:** Detailed personal instructions can become stale without fixing discovery. If fresh-session probes still cannot establish their own source reliably, the recommendation's instruction-centered component is inadequate; change the binding mechanism within proven scope or return the architectural decision to the owner. Economic importance does not justify claiming that inaccessible data was measured.
