Claude Code and Antigravity users can evaluate a Daily result or Full task from an explainable account of its actual AI consumption. ## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Documentation presence, an agent's assertion or a synthetic fixture is presented as proof of native agent behavior.
- ❌ 2. Copied history, parent-inclusive counters or cumulative revisions cause unexplained double counting, or task-related helper costs disappear.
- ❌ 3. “No source” lacks a reproducible search/binding account, or valid nonmeasurement is presented as zero/full coverage.
- ❌ 4. Provider-specific instructions are not discoverable from an affected Daily/Full entry, or require the owner to remember a private local path.
- ❌ 5. The result requires a new permanent service, raw chat archive or rewritten history to satisfy ordinary economics collection, or bypasses existing role/privacy boundaries.

**On failure:** Preserve actual observations and last-good records, return the material finding to the Coordinator, and correct within the approved TS. An architecture/scope change or an unavailable acceptance-critical native experiment returns to the owner; it is not silently downgraded to a documentation-only success.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Useful task evaluation:** Link supported resource facts to actual task work; preserve the distinction between API-equivalent valuation and billed subscription cost.
2. **Prove capability on the selected surface:** Paths, version mappings and child/inheritance semantics are observations to test, not provider-wide promises.
3. **One owner per meaning:** Shared accounting remains common; platform discovery and decoding guidance has a clear platform owner and an explicit entry route.
4. **Count work once, preserve unknowns:** Costs survive failure/retry; missing measurement never becomes zero, and overlap never becomes additive by convenience.
5. **Proportional evidence:** Use bounded, decision-changing native probes and temporary verification material. Do not add permanent tests without discussing them with the owner; preserve existing required tests.
6. **Exact access and durable portability:** Bind own or explicitly designated experimental sources; collect numeric evidence without searching unrelated chats or publishing conversations. Keep original sources unchanged and raw working material in the task's system-temporary directory.
7. **Saint-Exupéry in the delivered instructions:** Use the smallest complete instructions; remove replaced duplication and measure the actual change. Do not add a service, registry or blanket instruction layer to avoid diagnosing the observed cause.

Two focused iterations, matching configured `min_iterations: 2`.
