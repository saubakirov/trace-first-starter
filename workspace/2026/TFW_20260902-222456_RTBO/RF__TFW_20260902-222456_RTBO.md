## 2. Key Decisions

1. Keep ordinary Full as a complete prompt contract with no shipped executable or dependency; retain
   reusable diagnostics only as optional upstream maintainer tooling.
2. Make `.tfw/workflows/knowledge.md` the single complete Knowledge Gate authority and make Plan route
   to its unique heading instead of duplicating or invoking an implementation.
3. Generate one unlisted landing for every recognized task while removing both primary Tasks navigation
   and aggregate current-status output.
4. Preserve the known 123-code-point event and every protected historical tuple; remove the numeric
   validity rule rather than normalizing evidence.
5. Bind review to Candidate `fd1d29949f948d141b745dcc99d771b953fd75c9`; later task-local trace
   commits do not move the product snapshot.
