| D14 | Delete numeric validity for event `summary` and status `title`, `goal`, `value`, and `outcome`; remove the `lifecycle_verbatim` ceiling/truncation and unused `max_summary_length`, `max_index_lines`, and `max_index_facts_lines`; retain structural assertions and prompt-only concision guidance. | Controlled fixtures showed length findings can disappear while identity, authority, lifecycle, terminal/outcome, ref, time, and transition defects survive. `lifecycle_verbatim` is evidence and must remain exact. | D15 | Keep Assisted unchanged and add no RTBO-originating code, lifecycle command, migration dependency, collector, doctor, or docs-generation reference to it. | Repository search found no current coupling to these surfaces; the prompt-first edition already satisfies the boundary and has no demonstrated need for the new tooling. |
