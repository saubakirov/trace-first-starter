## Research Plan

### Gather
- Inventory the real task/identifier corpus, current Codex task titles, and the exact workflow checkpoints where work, task, phase, and hierarchy inputs become authoritative for new and resumed paths.
- Measure practical title length, uniqueness, abbreviation collisions, visible truncation/search signals, and character portability across ASCII, Unicode separators, and emoji without inventing evidence for other hosts.
- Collect official Codex evidence for automatic titles and rename capability, then distinguish documented terminal behaviour, enabled app tools, and directly observed sidebar/task state.
- Compare the minimum requested cue alternatives: formal role, exact skill, workflow function, hierarchy cue; full ID vs approved abbreviation; work-first vs task-first; bare vs marked phase.

### Extract
- Build a configuration space across work cue, task cue, ordering, delimiter, phase notation, hierarchy distinction, collision fallback, resolution point, and host capability.
- Derive one provider-neutral semantic grammar and map each task-bound, conditionally task-bound, and non-task-bound workflow to authoritative component sources and a satisfiable rename checkpoint.
- Separate title identity from authority, durable state, and command-entry proof; integrate Iteration 1's thin-proxy baseline without adding a preload or second algorithm.

### Challenge
- Stress-test finalists against master planning, phase planning, research, execution, review, resume, lead coordination, new/resumed tasks, no phase, complex phases, collisions, legacy identifiers, truncation, and search.
- Reject cues that imply false authority, separators that lose portability/searchability, abbreviations that become ambiguous without deterministic fallback, and placements that require guessing unresolved state.
- Test the non-blocking unsupported-host path and verify that absence of rename evidence is reported once while substantive work continues.
