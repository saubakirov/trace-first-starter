Counting method
===============

Words are whitespace-delimited tokens from UTF-8 repository files. Estimated tokens are
ceil(words * 4/3), calculated per command. They are context planning estimates, not CLI or
billing telemetry. A Codex current/strengthened route counts proxy plus canonical workflow;
full-copy/direct counts the canonical workflow once.
