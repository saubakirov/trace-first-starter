## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

Every TFW role enters each lifecycle checkpoint with the smallest sufficient, role-specific context. Each required read has one decision purpose, every normative concept has one authority, and agents encounter neither explanatory material for human readers nor a second statement of a rule they have already loaded. TFW's meaning, lifecycle algorithms, authority boundaries, and guarantees remain intact or become clearer.

**Impact:** Coordinator, Researcher, Executor, and Reviewer retain more attention for the selected task. Every lifecycle pass consumes fewer tokens, less time, and less money because role changes and status transitions stop paying a permanent context tax for historical explanations, obsolete mechanics, and duplicated instructions.

> “The agent reads one current rule once, when it needs it, and spends the remaining context on the task.”

## 5. Definition of Done (DoD) 🔒 FROZEN

- ✅ 1. Every canonical role and command has a transitive read graph covering session start, task selection, artifact authoring, handoff, revision, status transition, knowledge capture, and close.
- ✅ 2. Every graph edge names the decision that requires the read; readerless or purposeless active content is removed.
- ✅ 3. The before and after measurements use one reproducible whitespace-word method. A repeated mandatory read is counted each time it is instructed; section-level reads count only the named section.
- ✅ 4. The combined word count of `.tfw` files on canonical task-runtime read paths is reduced by at least 30% from the recorded baseline.
- ✅ 5. The mandatory fixed-context word count for Coordinator, Researcher, Executor, and Reviewer is reduced by at least 30% for each role, with no checkpoint regressing.
- ✅ 6. Every normative concept has exactly one declared authority. Any surviving inline repetition identifies its distinct enforcement site and why a reference would fail there.
- ✅ 7. The full-file preload of project knowledge is replaced by a relevance-preserving mechanism; current architecture decisions and project values remain discoverable and semantically verifiable.
- ✅ 8. Obsolete current instructions and readerless mechanisms are removed. Historical compatibility material may remain when it is outside ordinary runtime context.
- ✅ 9. Human-facing rationale already served by the essay or book is absent from mandatory agent context; no workflow depends on the book, a website, or an unpublished source.
- ✅ 10. Lifecycle states and transitions, role locks, HL freeze and amendment rules, research stages, TS/RF/REVIEW responsibilities, evidence, review routing, task-local authority, and knowledge ownership retain their meaning and observable behavior.
- ✅ 11. Every changed canonical behavior is reflected in its adapters, templates, tests, and receiving-project checks within the same phase.
- ✅ 12. Existing automated checks pass, and scenario evidence covers at least one clean-context run for each canonical role plus revision and knowledge-close paths.
- ✅ 13. No new persistent artifact, field, gate, or configuration key is added unless it removes more mandatory context than it adds and has a named reader and one job.
- ✅ 14. The result contains no minification, formatting tricks, or relocation performed only to improve the metric; reduction comes from deletion, consolidation, selective loading, or simpler architecture.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Any 30% result is obtained from byte size, minification, generated formatting, or excluding a mandatory transitive read from the baseline.
- ❌ 2. A lifecycle rule, gate, authority boundary, refusal condition, or compatibility guarantee changes without an explicit owner-approved amendment.
- ❌ 3. A concept still has two mandatory normative statements that can be read as independent authorities.
- ❌ 4. Compression turns a previously structural gate into prose advice or an unverifiable pointer.
- ❌ 5. The book, website, Git history, or vendor service becomes required to execute an ordinary TFW task.
- ❌ 6. A role must reconstruct missing operating rules from historical rationale or task traces.
- ❌ 7. A new summary, index, manifest, or generated view becomes a second source of truth.
- ❌ 8. A stale 1.x or pre-2.x mechanism remains in an active current path without an explicit compatibility reader.
- ❌ 9. A phase leaves a workflow changed while its adapter, template, or validation surface remains knowingly inconsistent.
- ❌ 10. Automated checks pass while the clean-context role scenarios reveal a changed decision, missed gate, or ambiguity.

**On failure:** reject the affected phase result, restore its prior working behavior, and return the architectural conflict to `/tfw-plan`; do not pay for the missing guarantee with another explanatory paragraph.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Context is a finite working resource** — measure what a role must load, not what the directory stores.
2. **One concept, one authority, one read** — duplication is admissible only when a distinct enforcement site makes reference insufficient.
3. **Task facts before framework history** — local state and current authority enter context before optional global rationale.
4. **Progressive disclosure without semantic loss** — load deeper material only when a concrete decision triggers it.
5. **Subtraction before addition or relocation** — first delete, merge, rename, or narrow; a new entity must make an older one leave.
6. **Meaning fixed, representation open** — files and boundaries may change radically; methodology meaning, algorithms, and guarantees may not.
7. **Algorithms in steps, forms in templates, terms in the glossary** — each information class has one job and one natural owner.
8. **History remains discoverable, not resident** — incident rationale can justify a rule without occupying every execution context.
9. **Measurements resist gaming** — count mandatory word exposure, repeated reads, and transitive dependencies.
10. **The framework must pass its own path** — validate both this self-hosting repository and a clean receiving project.

### 7.1 Quality Contract 🔒 FROZEN

1. Each phase records before/after role-context counts with the same command and includes raw output in evidence.
2. Each deletion maps the removed text to a surviving rule, test, historical source, or an explicit finding that it had no current reader.
3. Each deliberate duplicate states its separate enforcement purpose; “important” alone is not a purpose.
4. Each changed behavior path is complete within its phase, including adapters and receiving-project validation.
5. No phase creates a documentation layer whose maintenance requires agents to read both the layer and its sources.

## 10. RESEARCH Case 🟢 FREE

### Blind Spots

- The exact transitive read graph is not declared anywhere; current numbers are conservative lower bounds.
- We do not yet know which duplicated clauses are deliberate enforcement and which are accidental restatement.
- Automated tests cover file and parser behavior better than agent comprehension and decision equivalence.
- Radical options — a smaller canon, role-compiled views, generated routing, or elimination of the glossary as a mandatory read — have not been compared under the same constraints.

### Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Status |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Full mandatory reads of `conventions.md`, `glossary.md`, and `KNOWLEDGE.md` create more avoidable context than role-specific workflow prose. | confirmed in iteration 1 |
| H2 | The glossary can stop restating mechanics and become a compact term router without reducing agent comprehension. | confirmed for architecture/TS; implementation fixture remains mandatory |
| H3 | Incident narratives and historical justifications can leave active templates and conventions once their operative rule and structural test are explicit. | confirmed for architecture/TS; deletion ledger remains mandatory |
| H4 | Removing stale 2.x mechanics and consolidating authority can achieve 30% per role without changing any lifecycle algorithm or guarantee. | exact numerical design confirmed; phase behavior proof remains mandatory |

### Risks of Not Researching

Compression by intuition would repeat the framework's existing failure pattern: a local prose fix would be removed without identifying the structural guarantee it was carrying. It could produce shorter prompts and weaker behavior while every word metric appears green.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

1. **Gather:** reconstruct every role/checkpoint read path; attribute words to decision purpose, duplicate authority, triggered reference, history, or no current reader.
2. **Extract:** compare radical architectures — compact single canon, role-specific source files, generated read views, section-addressed progressive loading, and removal of obsolete artifact classes.
3. **Challenge:** test semantic equivalence, deliberate inline enforcement, clean receiver behavior, and the 30% threshold against counterexamples.

### Research Result

Research selected C9: workflows own role algorithms; addressed convention ranges own shared invariants; the glossary is a one-sentence term router with addressed PV as an operational exception; project knowledge is citation- or PV-driven; history is lookup-only; Knowledge Gate state is a task-to-selected-section SHA-256 map written after candidate markers; paired semantic fixtures and an evidence-only read manifest enforce equivalence. `research/iter2/3_extract.md` R01–R39 is the implementation catalogue. No research question remains before TS.

### Why Not Just...?

- Why not shorten sentences in every file? — It leaves duplicate reads, ambiguous authority, and stale algorithms intact.
- Why not delete `glossary.md` or `conventions.md` immediately? — Their overlapping content does not prove either whole artifact is redundant; their distinct readers and enforcement sites must be mapped first.
- Why not add word budgets to every artifact? — A file budget measures storage, not lifecycle exposure, and a checkpoint would itself add context unless it replaces a current mechanism.
- Why not move large files outside `.tfw`? — Location does not matter if the workflow still loads them; relocation without a changed read graph is metric theatre.
