## Conclusion

Iteration 1 found that the runtime-context problem is chiefly a read-selection and authority-topology defect, not a sentence-length defect. It produced an exact fixed read graph, classified active and non-runtime material, compared eight architectures, selected C8 with C2 and C6 as bounded alternatives, identified deliberate repetitions that must survive, exposed stale active mechanisms, and showed conservative reductions above 30% for every primary role and the complete minimum-two-iteration trajectory. What remains is deliberately narrow but essential: replace allowances with exact rule ranges and prove that the candidate makes the same decisions from clean context, including compatibility, closure and receiving-project behavior. The numerical case is sufficient for planning direction; the semantic case is not yet sufficient for an implementation TS.
