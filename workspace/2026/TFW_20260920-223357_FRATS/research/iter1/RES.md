## Conclusion

have explicit current-state and epoch limits; the retired RCFR audit reproduces exactly; a symmetric
ten-command bridge shows real but non-equal growth; current installed adapters are coherent; filename
ambiguity is localized to producer emission; and unconditional agent-principal selection survives

growth represents new controls rather than disposable repetition, that a complete receipt can coexist
with an uncommitted update, and that the identity interruption was rule-compliant rather than agent
negligence. Self-critique: the receiver corpus is intentionally small, RYC was moving, no native
Claude trial was run, and the bridge inherits a deleted historical parser; those limits are explicit
and make iteration 2 necessary rather than optional.
