## Conclusion

Iteration 2 selects a smaller complete interaction architecture without pretending partial native
evidence is reliability. Existing artifacts support activation-driven work: ordinary results return
through role-owned traces, one bounded control answer is recorded by the artifact owner with exact
provenance, and changes to authority move through HL/TS rather than chat or concealed cross-role
editing. A stable named principal remains optional and has one defensible job—reusable attribution
continuity for an explicitly selected LEAD—while task/unit, mandate, capability and approval remain
separate. The broad single-mutation-lane hypothesis does not survive; serialized landing/release per
shared target does, but implementing it and technical IAM/provenance controls belongs to separate
future tasks, not FRATS Phase A/B. Compression now has a six-edge semantic oracle. The unresolved
limits are honest: H1/H9 causation, Codex beyond P2, Claude beyond P0 and H8's concrete reduction
proof. Those are Phase A/B evidence obligations, not grounds for an automatic third research pass.
