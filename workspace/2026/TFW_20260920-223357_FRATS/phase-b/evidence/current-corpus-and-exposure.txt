parses persistent entry surfaces and the actual workflow Read Contracts; removes retired

are visible in the graph but charged as zero because their content is selected at runtime. The
unique active corpus counts charged `.tfw` sources/ranges once, removes a selected range contained
inside another selected range, and charges a whole file when `*` is selected. All reads use exact
Git refs; no working-tree content is counted.
