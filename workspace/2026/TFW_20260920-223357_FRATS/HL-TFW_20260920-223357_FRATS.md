## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

TFW has been refactored from field evidence rather than from isolated prompt preferences. Its
canonical artifacts are smaller, mutually consistent and behaviorally complete. Every activated
workflow unit is autonomous inside its Role Lock and approved scope; current task state says exactly
which Coordinator receives gates, whether iterative dialogue is allowed and who may activate the
next unit. Where iterative dialogue is explicitly allowed, a separate GATEWAY keeps the owner's
entry point free of operational chat.

**Impact:** The owner spends attention on purpose, mandate and corrections rather than relaying
routine messages. Agents start through one exact skill invocation, find their Coordinator and
communication boundary immediately, and hand work forward through durable state and role-owned
artifacts instead of prompt briefings, duplicate execution or token-heavy dialogue.

> “When the team needs me, I open one clean Gateway; the working conversation stays below it.”

### 2.6 Owner-selected coordination model after research

The owner accepted the research picture and then removed the remaining overloaded mode entity.
Autonomous workflow execution is the invariant after valid activation, not a selectable `AG` mode;
`CL`, `AG` and `AT` remain historical inputs but are not newly issued control values. Current work is
selected through five explicit task/phase state facts:

| Entity | Meaning | Current owner |
|---|---|---|
| `coordinator_route` | Exact Coordinator unit and provider-native address that receives declared TFW gates and durable returns. | selected task/phase `status.md` |
| `owner_gateway` | `owner:{handle}` or exact separate GATEWAY unit through which the Coordinator reaches the human owner. | selected task/phase `status.md` |
| `dialogue` | `tfw-gates-only` or owner-approved `iterative`. | selected task/phase `status.md`, bounded by exact authority |
| `activation` | `owner-only` or `delegated:{mandate-ref}`. | selected task/phase `status.md`, bounded by exact authority |
| `coordination_authority` | Exact frozen source and epoch for the four selections. | selected task/phase `status.md` |

The status carrier is a routing spine, not a participant roster. Exact provisioned unit, parent,
role/phase, native address/channel and dispatch source live in the immutable dispatch edge; the
actual producer repeats its own unit, parent and dispatch reference in Briefing/RES, ONB/RF or
REVIEW. A mismatch is an error, not a second truth.

`tfw-gates-only` permits only vertical adjacency: a working unit exchanges declared gates and
returns with its own Coordinator; that Coordinator escalates through its configured owner gateway.
Workers never contact siblings, foreign Coordinators, the owner or a GATEWAY directly. `iterative`
adds only exact owner-approved peer grants. It requires a separate GATEWAY and Coordinator; the
GATEWAY is the sole owner-facing ingress, never a workflow Coordinator or dialogue participant.

Provisioning a native window, activating a TFW role and continuing an existing unit are separate
acts. Valid activation invokes the exact `/tfw-*` skill with task/phase identity and its dispatch or
explicit owner-direct source. A role description, “wait” prompt or long briefing does not activate
work. The skill and task artifacts supply the mental model; activation supplies addresses, not
solutions. Distrust alone likewise creates no work: repeat another role only when the current
workflow requires independent verification or a named contradiction/evidence gap exists.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Word count or artifact count is treated as value and a required purpose, authority, evidence, review or continuation rule is removed to improve the number.
- ❌ 2. An operational unit contacts a sibling, foreign Coordinator, owner or GATEWAY under `tfw-gates-only`, or treats native messaging capability as route permission.
- ❌ 3. A GATEWAY also acts as Coordinator, receives raw operational chat, joins a peer dialogue or ceases to be the single obvious owner-facing ingress for iterative work.
- ❌ 4. A session title, profile, binding, provider identity or working-unit row is treated as authority.
- ❌ 5. Claude behavior is declared equivalent to Codex from translated prompts, hidden subagents or incomplete provider evidence.
- ❌ 6. A new carrier, registry, checklist row or runtime is added before naming the one job it performs and what existing mechanism leaves because it arrives.
- ❌ 7. A receiver is modified merely to make the framework task's evidence pass.
- ❌ 8. Historical receipts, rejected work, customized North Stars or project-owned adapter content are silently normalized or deleted.
- ❌ 9. Executor and Reviewer independence is weakened, or the independent Reviewer participates in dialogue that develops the result it later reviews.
- ❌ 10. A forced reduction percentage replaces semantic comparison, or a non-comparable denominator is presented as the old metric.
- ❌ 11. Canonical and installed adapter surfaces diverge, or a phase defers its own required synchronization to a later cleanup phase.
- ❌ 12. A material field failure is converted into a universal provider or methodology claim without a bounded source and epoch.
- ❌ 13. Historical task artifacts are renamed to create cosmetic consistency, or more than one filename grammar remains valid for newly issued artifacts.
- ❌ 14. A provisioned window begins role work without invoking the exact skill, or a long spawn briefing becomes an alternative task contract or solution bias.
- ❌ 15. Status becomes a mutable full participant roster, or a unit must search chats, titles or provider recency to discover its Coordinator and owner route.
- ❌ 16. Executor appears to answer its own ONB blocker, an answer owner edits ONB, or a gate event silently changes the governing HL/TS.
- ❌ 17. A unit repeats another role's work merely because it distrusts the producer, or “helpful” unrequested prework crosses a Role Lock and starts a correction dialogue.

**On failure:** stop the affected phase, preserve the honest trace and route the cited condition to
the Coordinator or owner under the existing authority rules. Do not compensate with more dialogue,
an unapproved carrier or an unscoped cleanup.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Autonomy follows activation, not a mode label** — an exact skill invocation starts one Role Lock; `CL`/`AG`/`AT` do not carry new-work semantics.
2. **Explicit routes beat remembered conventions** — current state says who receives gates, who faces the owner, whether dialogue is allowed and who may activate.
3. **Vertical by default** — ordinary units communicate only with their own Coordinator; provider reachability never grants a horizontal edge.
4. **GATEWAY protects owner attention** — iterative work has one separate, clean owner boundary that is neither Coordinator nor dialogue participant.
5. **Activation is an address, not a briefing** — skill, task/phase and dispatch source select authoritative context; sender summaries cannot become a second mental model.
6. **Roles contribute, they do not pre-solve one another** — prior artifacts carry role-owned results; distrust alone does not justify duplication or debate.
7. **One truth, one owner, one reader path** — duplicated normative prose and facts without a reader are defects, not safety.
8. **Subtraction without semantic loss** — terminology, topology and templates replace repeated explanation; numbers report the result but do not govern it.
9. **Structural enforcement** — important identity, route, activation, return and authority boundaries expose their own violation in state, files or native readback.
10. **Native evidence before provider claims** — each installed surface proves its own address, messaging, naming and routing limits.
11. **Independent review remains independent** — lower coordination cost may not merge proposal, execution, verification and acceptance.

### Why Not Just...?

- Why not cut every workflow by another 30%? — A percentage cannot distinguish duplicated prose
  from a rare but necessary authority edge; it would reward semantic loss.
- Why not tell agents “do not chat”? — The current topology and missing durable destinations may
  make dialogue the only executable route; exhortation would not alter that structure.
- Why not make renaming mandatory and block on failure? — Naming is navigation, not authority;
  unsupported native behavior must not stop substantive work.
- Why not add a message broker or coordination log? — TFW already has task-local state and owned
  artifacts, and the receiver contract forbids a new runtime tax without demonstrated necessity.
