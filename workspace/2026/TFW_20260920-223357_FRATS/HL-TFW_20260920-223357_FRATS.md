## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

TFW has been refactored from field evidence rather than from isolated prompt preferences. Its
canonical artifacts are smaller, mutually consistent and behaviorally complete; an autonomous
LEAD replaces the owner's mechanical routing work without turning the role chain into an agent
conversation, while every provider states honestly which navigation and routing behavior it has
actually demonstrated.

**Impact:** The owner spends attention on purpose, mandate and corrections rather than relaying
routine messages. Agents consume less repeated context, find their role and session immediately,
and hand work forward through durable state and owned artifacts instead of token-heavy dialogue.

> “I speak to the Coordinator; the rest of the chain should run from one activation to the next and leave its answer in the task.”

### 2.3 Observed behavioral mismatch

```text
CURRENT PRACTICE

owner ──message──> coordinator ──message──> executor
  │                     │                       │
  │                     └──re-check / clarify──┘
  └──manual relay──> reviewer ──message──> coordinator

Effects: repeated explanation · duplicated verification · unclear session navigation
         conversational AT · owner remains the mechanical router
```

Current rules already define task-local state, role-owned artifacts, a selected LEAD, working
units and provider-neutral session titles. They do not yet demonstrate that the ordinary happy
path can run without cross-role dialogue, and their direct question/result routes may encourage
exactly the conversational topology the owner rejects. Session renaming is fail-soft and
navigation-only, but field behavior is uneven: Codex usually attempts it after reminders, while
Claude has not reliably done so in the owner's projects.

Iteration 2 separates four traffic classes with different consequences:

| Class | Purpose | Durable expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | Start or resume one addressable role in exact scope. | Recoverable dispatch, inputs and return condition. |
| Bounded control exchange | One material question and one authoritative gate answer. | Artifact owner records source, epoch, governing baseline and effect. |
| Durable return | Deliver a completed role-owned result. | RES, RF/evidence, REVIEW or revised TS carries the answer and next trigger. |
| Iterative dialogue | Open-ended co-development whose meaning accumulates across turns. | Explicitly selected workshop plus later consolidation; never the default control plane. |

Existing artifact classes cover the happy path and named exceptions. The concrete carrier defect is
ONB §3: its template says “Coordinator fills in” while Coordinator Role Lock forbids ONB edits. The
surviving repair keeps Executor as writer: it records a bounded factual answer with answerer/source,
time or epoch, governing TS/HL reference and operational effect. Any answer changing scope,
acceptance, architecture or reserved authority routes through an existing HL amendment or TS
revision. No generic answer artifact or generalized cross-role append is justified.

### 2.5 Non-AT identity gate overreach

The owner personally launched the first FRATS Researcher outside AT. This repository has exactly one
human profile (`saubakirov`) and one agent profile (`robert`), but the machine binding contains no
Steps Framework mapping. The unconditional **Who Is Acting** checkpoint therefore stopped both the
Coordinator and, independently, the Researcher; the Coordinator had already asked once and received
`saubakirov`, while the new session correctly asked again under the current once-per-session rule.
The observed implementations are consistent. The unresolved design question is the identity model:
a binding has one principal value per project root and cannot simultaneously distinguish an
owner-launched operational session from a future selected-LEAD session on the same machine/root.
No named agent identity was material to this Researcher. The bounded current resolution is no agent
principal, optional `writer` omitted, `on_behalf_of: saubakirov`, and actual `via` preserved. Research
must test whether principal resolution should be conditional on stable attribution need while role,
accountable human and gate recipient remain independently resolvable, including multi-human and
shared-device counterexamples.

Iteration 2 supports that conditional model. A stable named principal is optional and has one unique
job: reusable declared attribution continuity across distinct explicitly selected-LEAD units or
resumed root sessions. It does not supply human identity, authority, approval, mandate,
authentication or technical capability. Ordinary owner-launched operational units need no invented
principal; ambiguous humans/shared devices and explicit selected-LEAD attribution retain their
separate resolution branches.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Word count or artifact count is treated as value and a required purpose, authority, evidence, review or continuation rule is removed to improve the number.
- ❌ 2. The design still requires routine free-form dialogue between Coordinator, Researcher, Executor or Reviewer on the artifact-complete happy path.
- ❌ 3. The LEAD becomes an additional conversational relay while the owner remains responsible for opening, prompting and forwarding every ordinary role result.
- ❌ 4. A session title, profile, binding, provider identity or working-unit row is treated as authority.
- ❌ 5. Claude behavior is declared equivalent to Codex from translated prompts, hidden subagents or incomplete provider evidence.
- ❌ 6. A new carrier, registry, checklist row or runtime is added before naming the one job it performs and what existing mechanism leaves because it arrives.
- ❌ 7. A receiver is modified merely to make the framework task's evidence pass.
- ❌ 8. Historical receipts, rejected work, customized North Stars or project-owned adapter content are silently normalized or deleted.
- ❌ 9. Executor and Reviewer independence is weakened in the name of lower message volume.
- ❌ 10. A forced reduction percentage replaces semantic comparison, or a non-comparable denominator is presented as the old metric.
- ❌ 11. Canonical and installed adapter surfaces diverge, or a phase defers its own required synchronization to a later cleanup phase.
- ❌ 12. A material field failure is converted into a universal provider or methodology claim without a bounded source and epoch.
- ❌ 13. Historical task artifacts are renamed to create cosmetic consistency, or more than one filename grammar remains valid for newly issued artifacts.

**On failure:** stop the affected phase, preserve the honest trace and route the cited condition to
the Coordinator or owner under the existing authority rules. Do not compensate with more dialogue,
an unapproved carrier or an unscoped cleanup.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Artifact-first, not dialogue-first** — durable state and role-owned artifacts carry ordinary work; conversation is reserved for material unresolved decisions.
2. **Activation is not dialogue** — one bounded role start is necessary control, while iterative context relay is a cost that requires justification.
3. **The LEAD replaces mechanical owner work, not human authority** — purpose, reservations, acceptance and stop responsibility remain human-rooted.
4. **One truth, one owner, one reader path** — duplicated normative prose and facts without a reader are defects, not safety.
5. **Subtraction without semantic loss** — terminology, topology and templates replace repeated explanation; numbers report the result but do not govern it.
6. **Structural enforcement** — important identity, return and authority boundaries must expose their own violation in state, files or native readback.
7. **Native evidence before provider claims** — each installed surface proves its own topology, naming and routing limits.
8. **Independent review remains independent** — lower coordination cost may not merge proposal, execution, verification and acceptance.
9. **Receivers are evidence, not fixtures to rewrite** — project purpose, customization and current work remain outside this task's mutation authority.
10. **No receiver runtime tax** — unknown projects must be able to follow the methodology from files without installing a framework service.

### 7.1 Quality Contract 🔒 FROZEN

- Every rule change names its normative owner, actual readers and all manifest-derived delivery sites.
- Every count names method, selector and immutable reference; movable counts are omitted when the sentence retains its meaning without them.
- A changed workflow remains algorithmic: numbered actions, exact gates and bounded references rather than explanatory accumulation.
- Provider-specific observations state version, surface, date, native action and missing claims.
- A phase that changes a behavior updates its canonical rule, required forms, adapters and evidence in the same accepted Candidate.
- Existing integration/documentation and blob-size tests are retained; additional permanent tests require owner discussion.

Skipping research would turn the owner's field observations into universal architecture, repeat the

solution that merely tells Claude to behave like Codex. It would also risk placing Coordinator
answers in artifacts the Coordinator does not own.

   bridge, reader/copy graph, filename issuance and conditional identity are recorded in RES.

### Why Not Just...?

- Why not cut every workflow by another 30%? — A percentage cannot distinguish duplicated prose
  from a rare but necessary authority edge; it would reward semantic loss.
- Why not tell agents “do not chat”? — The current topology and missing durable destinations may
  make dialogue the only executable route; exhortation would not alter that structure.
- Why not make renaming mandatory and block on failure? — Naming is navigation, not authority;
  unsupported native behavior must not stop substantive work.
- Why not add a message broker or coordination log? — TFW already has task-local state and owned
  artifacts, and the receiver contract forbids a new runtime tax without demonstrated necessity.
