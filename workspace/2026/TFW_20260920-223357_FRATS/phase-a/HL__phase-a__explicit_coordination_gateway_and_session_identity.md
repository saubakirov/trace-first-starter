## Parent Derivation

This phase implements master HL §4 Phase A and inherits these outcomes without creating a second
contract:

1. one explicit coordination model replaces new `CL`/`AG`/`AT` issuance;
2. every validly activated unit works autonomously inside Role Lock and approved scope;
3. current task/phase state exposes the Coordinator route, owner gateway, dialogue policy,
   activation policy and their authority;
4. ordinary communication is vertical and gate-bound, while iterative peer dialogue requires an
   exact grant plus a separate GATEWAY and root Coordinator;
5. provision, activation and continuation become distinguishable, reconstructable acts;
6. bounded answers are owned by their authority through `gate_answer`, not transcribed as if the
   blocked role answered itself; and
7. session identity, producer provenance and provider claims remain navigation/evidence rather than
   authority.

Vision, acceptance, failure conditions and principles remain solely in the frozen master HL.
