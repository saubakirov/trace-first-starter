# Reading an AI economics report — draft 1

Use these questions before turning a resource report into a management decision.

1. What intended result and actual achieved or accepted result does the report connect?
2. Which projects, tasks, phases, units and source revisions are included? Are cumulative
   snapshots and overlapping product interests counted once?
3. Do product labels have work/result grounds? If feature allocation is absent, are the
   figures clearly described as resources of associated tasks?
4. Does the date selection mean consumption in a period or an event-based task cohort
   with lifetime usage? Where is undated consumption kept?
5. Are total tokens, cache/reasoning subsets, dated API equivalents and compatible time
   kinds distinct? An API equivalent is not a subscription invoice.
6. What is missing, unpriced, excluded or failed? Is the latest precise cause visible,
   with usable last-good measurement and a finite cutoff/tail?
7. Which statements are observations, interpretations or causal hypotheses? What measured
   baseline supports any outlier, efficiency, ROI or savings claim?
8. What concrete next decision or check follows from the grounds, and who can authorize it?

Keep unsupported conclusions open. A polished report and a large token total do not prove
business benefit, waste or participant quality. Tables and prose should carry the complete answer.
