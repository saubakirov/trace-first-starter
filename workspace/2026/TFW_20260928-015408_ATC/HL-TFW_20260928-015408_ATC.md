## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

An owner approves the intended result and a bounded mandate, and TFW carries the authorized task through its roles and phases without the owner copying commands or repeatedly requesting continuation. Long tasks retain their strategic direction and can resume through another working unit from durable, lawful inputs, while consequential decisions remain with their actual authority. The arrangement remains proportionate for a small task and understandable at entry.

**Impact:** Preserve owner attention and strategic context; reduce unattended stalls, context accumulation, redundant communication and drift.

> **Explicitly imagined stakeholder quote:** “I no longer have to press continue between authorized steps; I can see which decisions still belong to me.”

## 3. Target State (To-Be) 🔒 FROZEN

1. Each ready, authorized next action has an accountable actor and a defined continuation mechanism.
2. Detailed work across a long task need not accumulate in one coordinator context.
3. Every wait identifies its reason, recipient, required condition and resumption responsibility; ambiguous delivery remains pending.
4. Roles send required questions, status changes and durable returns vertically to their coordinator. Transcript isolation and independent review remain enforceable.
5. A successor can recover the task's current intent, authority, state and next action from lawful durable inputs; inherited chat is never authority.
6. Small tasks use the smallest arrangement that satisfies the same goal, with no mandatory extra hierarchy solely for appearance.
7. Architecture alternatives and their costs, failure cases and evidence are shown to the owner before implementation. The selected arrangement is then made usable in the shipped workflow and receiving-project entry points.
8. Mixed-provider teams and one owner interface serving multiple tasks remain future compatibility considerations; this task does not implement either.

## 5. Definition of Done (DoD) 🔒 FROZEN

- ✅ 1. The owner receives a comparative architectural decision package: concrete role/context/continuation diagrams, viable alternatives, costs, failure cases, recommendation and explicit uncertainty.
- ✅ 2. The selected arrangement preserves task strategy and owner-reserved decisions while advancing ready authorized role and phase actions without routine owner command transfer.
- ✅ 3. Gate questions, status changes and durable returns have exact vertical recipients; missing or ambiguous delivery cannot masquerade as completed progression.
- ✅ 4. Long-task context is bounded through an explicit lifecycle and lawful handover; small-task entry stays proportionate.
- ✅ 5. Independent judgment, ONB questioning and review survive; reading unreturned role transcripts is not a continuation dependency.
- ✅ 6. A new receiving unit/project can identify goal, authority, state, next action and responsible actor without extra oral briefing; demonstrated scope and unsupported capabilities are stated.
- ✅ 7. Accepted behavior is reflected consistently in relevant shipped workflows, adapters and role endings, with independent review against the frozen purpose.
- ✅ 8. Verification addresses actual continuation risks; no new permanent tests are added without separate owner discussion.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. A ready authorized step silently waits for the owner to copy a command or say continue.
- ❌ 2. An agent expands scope or grants its own authority to bypass a reserved decision.
- ❌ 3. An architectural name, role or artifact adds cost without a distinct necessary responsibility or evidence.
- ❌ 4. A long-task solution depends on one indefinitely growing context, raw role transcripts or undocumented knowledge in the authoring chat.
- ❌ 5. Provisioning, message delivery or text-only compliance is claimed as proof of completed autonomous continuation.
- ❌ 6. Implementation begins before the owner sees and selects the architecture and approves its TS.

**On failure:** Preserve the actual nonterminal state and evidence, identify the responsible boundary or missing authority, and reroute only affected work. No hidden substitute or self-answered owner gate.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Goal and value precede topology.** Gateway, hierarchical coordinators, names, forks and recursion must earn their place by solving a demonstrated need.
2. **Separate causality.** Intent extraction/transfer, strategic drift, context accumulation and missing continuation may be different problems. Do not use one explanation as proof of the others.
3. **Authority stays human-rooted and bounded.** Autonomy advances existing permission; it does not create permission.
4. **Durable returns enable replacement.** Context inheritance may assist understanding but cannot replace current authority, state or independent questioning.
5. **Brief vertical communication is mandatory.** Keep required gates and returns; remove redundant commentary and transcript harvesting.
6. **Subtract without losing meaning.** Each entity and control must protect purpose, a boundary or necessary proof; compare the consequence of its removal.
7. **Portability is demonstrated at use.** Do not generalize a successful authoring chat to all receiving projects or platforms.
8. **Architecture remains open until the decision gate.** Current rules are the operational baseline, not a prohibition on researching better rules.

### Blind Spots

What must survive coordinator replacement? Which named entities have distinct jobs? Which guarantees require provider execution support rather than prose? No unmeasured token/time savings are promised.

### Hypotheses

| # | Decision-changing hypothesis | Plausible false case | Consequence if false | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Explicit continuation ownership and durable return routing may solve much of the babysitting without a mandatory extra Gateway. | A single unit cannot both retain task strategy and survive long phase traffic within its context. | Add a justified boundary or strategy carrier; do not assume a simpler topology suffices. | open |
| H2 | Task strategy and phase execution can be separated without making the owner interpret a coordinator-of-coordinators hierarchy. | A baton or phase-local planning loses cross-phase invariants or creates duplicate authority. | Retain a clearly scoped task-level strategy responsibility. | open |

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

1. **Gather:** Map concrete stop/start/return and authority sites in current TFW; distinguish observed mechanisms from causal hypotheses and provider constraints.
2. **Extract:** Use a morphological box and compatibility analysis to compare strategy carrier, continuation actor, context lifetime, input inheritance, owner interface and identity.
3. **Challenge:** Test surviving arrangements against one-phase, long sequential, branching, replacement, ambiguous delivery, reserved amendment and imperfect-HL scenarios; seek counterevidence.
4. **Iteration 2+:** Independently challenge the durable findings and close decision-changing evidence gaps before recommending an architecture.

**Iteration 2 decision focus:** Countercheck the shortest coherent direct route against a bounded task-strategy/phase or successor route. Seek primary or native evidence for idle-parent return-to-next-action, compare complete and omitted HL intent at receiving entry, make long/branch/replacement handover and ambiguous-delivery stops concrete, and state the smallest still-unverified condition for each surviving choice. A second iteration is justified by these unresolved acceptance claims, not by the stage count.

**Coordinator mode ruling for iteration 1:** focused. This first pass maps the current mechanisms and source boundaries with one bounded loop per stage. A second iteration is mandatory and may use deep mode for material counterevidence after the first RES identifies the actual gap. ### Risks of Not Researching

Select a pleasing name or hierarchy that repeats the existing failure, attribute unknown causes to context alone, or freeze a provider promise that cannot actually continue.

### Why Not Just...?

- Keep one coordinator forever: attractive for small work, but finite context and long-phase drift are unresolved.
- Require Gateway everywhere: it may protect owner context yet still need a strategy/continuation carrier; costs need evidence.
- Fork every agent: may improve shared context while inheriting bias, stale state and irrelevant history; no universal benefit is established.
- Recursively run full Plan for every phase: may localize work but still needs phase advancement and invariant ownership.
- Add more “must continue” text: worth comparing, but intent without an actual trigger/recipient is not a proven mechanism.
