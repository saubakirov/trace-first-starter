## 1. Objective

Ship the owner-selected A plus B/V coordination model into the canonical TFW rules, status contract and installed agent entry points. A bounded one-phase task uses one direct task Coordinator. Long multi-phase work has a task Coordinator responsible for strategy, dependencies, phase readiness and the owner route, with separate phase Coordinators for bounded local cycles; an exact successor can replace the task Coordinator at a safe checkpoint. Required vertical gates and independent roles continue without routine owner command transfer. The result must retain the role mental models, purpose, value, principles and terminology behind TFW rather than reducing it to a routing diagram.

## 2. Scope

### In Scope

- Define the distinct duties and limits of the human owner, task Coordinator, phase Coordinator, Researcher, Executor and independent Reviewer. The original planning Coordinator remains the task Coordinator while capable. `Coordinator` is the Role Lock; task/phase is its scope. `COORD · TASK` and `COORD · TASK · PHASE-A` are navigation titles only.
- Retire separate GATEWAY as a required or offered new-work role. Leave historical GATEWAY and `LEAD` records readable without issuing `LEAD` anew. A named agent principal remains optional attribution, never a prerequisite for a unit, route or mandate.
- Specify exact next-action ownership, phase-level returns, successor handover, status and mandate validation, and default gates-only vertical messages. A role learns only its authorized edge; it never needs another role's unreturned transcript. Manual owner-transfer remains an explicit exceptional choice, separate from owner-assisted task creation.
- Replace the misleading `owner_gateway` status field for new work with one upward route: `owner:{human}` for the task Coordinator and `coordinator:{native-address}` for a phase Coordinator. Preserve old carriers and actual active routes under their governing epoch; migrate a live carrier only at a safe gate with a known recipient, without retroactive rewrites or silent reparenting.
- Propagate the same contract to canonical workflows, templates, three selected Coordinator profiles, persistent adapter sources and installed Codex/Claude/Antigravity receivers. Keep Cursor common-only. Update the maintainer status reader for both old and new carriers.
- Provide task-local evidence and an independent review. Before merge, present exact changed paths, why each changed, fixed-tokenizer before/after counts, what Saint-Exupéry removed or retained, actual runtime observations and unsupported claims, and a renewed check of goals and values.

### Out of Scope

- Shared-computer identity, device names, multi-human sessions, new `bindings.yaml` semantics and account/subscription policy: [separate proposal](PROPOSAL__shared_host_identity.md). In this task only remove any inference that a principal or machine binding supplies a route or mandate.
- A mandatory MCP server, a second source of state, autonomous supervisory daemon, cross-provider team execution, cross-task owner interface, universal forking, and recursive full Plan for every phase. A read-only route checker may be evaluated as a later transport, not required for acceptance.
- Release, publishing, push or merge; the owner sees the reviewed local branch first.
- New permanent tests. Use bounded existing checks and task-local probes; any additional permanent test requires a separate owner discussion.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

### AC-1: The mental model remains intact

- [ ] A visible task/phase role map states each role's purpose, value, owned decisions, parent and forbidden authority. The human owner's strategic discussion, goal, value, principles and reserved judgments remain explicit in HL, workflow entry and final explanation.
- [ ] New work does not issue `LEAD` or require a GATEWAY or named agent. Historical terms stay interpretable at their original epochs; names and titles remain navigational or attribution data.

Gate: inspect the updated canonical rule, glossary, HL/status templates and entry profiles against HL §§1, 3, 5–7 and the selected architecture in §10.  
Evidence: actual fixed Candidate diff and independent Reviewer purpose check.

### AC-2: A, B and V advance with one accountable next-action owner

- [ ] A direct task Coordinator performs the full one-phase TFW cycle without an extra phase Coordinator.
- [ ] For long work, the task Coordinator alone owns cross-phase strategy, dependencies, ready-phase launch, owner decisions and successor selection; each phase Coordinator owns only its phase TS, worker gates and durable phase return. Concurrent phases have separate scoped routes.
- [ ] V names a safe checkpoint, current artifact/authority reconstruction, exact successor address and dispatch, acknowledgement and route switch. If a unit is unavailable or delivery ambiguous, the affected action remains pending with a named actor and condition.

Gate: trace one-phase, sequential, parallel and successor cases through the rules and complete status examples; reject cycles, self-grants, hidden helpers or an owner asked to copy a routine command.  
Evidence: task-local scenario transcript/table with observed versus specified claims kept separate.

### AC-3: Routing status is unambiguous and compatible

- [ ] New task statuses use `upstream_route: owner:{human}`; new phase statuses use `upstream_route: coordinator:{native-address}`. `coordinator_route` remains the exact recipient for lower role gates. The selected field set is closed and rejects missing, both-name, malformed or mismatched routes.
- [ ] Existing `owner_gateway` carriers remain readable at their actual authority epoch. A live migration requires an actual recipient and safe checkpoint; no mass rewrite or inferred reparenting. The maintainer reader reports the exact old/new condition.

Gate: run bounded Python status-validation probes for a valid new root, valid new phase, valid legacy task, partial or double route, wrong ancestor and unknown parent; inspect actual ATC status separately.  
Evidence: captured commands/results in EV; no new permanent test.

### AC-4: Required gates reduce chatter and bias

- [ ] Researcher, Executor and Reviewer send status changes, material questions and durable returns only to their own Coordinator; a phase Coordinator returns phase-level items only to the task Coordinator. The owner receives only decisions, material blockers, completed results or requested status.
- [ ] A gate message contains the required checkpoint, neutral minimum facts and immutable artifact reference. Roles do not read another unit's unreturned transcript, expose a global roster, relay preferred solutions or turn a wait snapshot into evidence. Explicit two-peer dialogue remains bounded separately; manual owner-transfer requires a human choice.

Gate: inspect all role endings, root adapters and one actual Executor/Reviewer return.  
Evidence: exact sent envelope, addressed receipt when observable and artifact ref; unavailable wake or delivery is reported rather than inferred.

### AC-5: Entry points and capability claims match reality

The Codex role receives a command-only first message and can reach current task files in its primary project. Claude and Antigravity limits remain stated; no capability is inferred from a copied adapter.

A separate receiving checkout or project probe identifies the loaded instructions, current authority, responsible unit and next act; failed or unobserved activation is reported. ## 6. Technical Guidance

- Treat the authoritative task/phase `status.md`, journal and frozen HL as inputs. A route helper, if useful, derives a local answer and stores no parallel mandate. Native sender and wake capability must be observed independently.
- Prefer the smallest coherent edits to canonical files, followed by exact receiver synchronization. Keep historical migration texts and old task artifacts immutable.
- For an active legacy status with `owner_gateway`, allow continuation under its original authority and migrate at a safe gate; new work emits only `upstream_route`. Do not rename an active phase's parent behind its back.
- Existing maintainer helpers may verify structure but cannot authenticate owner consent or invent next-action authority.

## 7. Definition of Failure

- ❌ A ready authorized step has no named next actor, recipient or trigger, or is quietly left for the owner to restart.
- ❌ A status/title/principal/host or MCP result is treated as authority; a phase or successor grants itself scope.
- ❌ GATEWAY survives as a required new-work unit, or task and phase Coordinators are semantically indistinguishable.
- ❌ Researcher, Executor or Reviewer work is collapsed into Coordinator work, peer chatter, transcript harvesting or a non-independent verdict.
- ❌ A legacy carrier is silently rewritten, a provider wake is claimed without observation, or token savings are claimed from copied-file totals.
- ❌ Implementation or merge proceeds without the respective exact approval gate.
