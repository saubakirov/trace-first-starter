## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

Every completed Full TFW task now carries a compact, inspectable account of the tokens, time, quality observations, and money attributable to it, with each number tied to its source and calculation method. The same evidence supports careful comparison across product areas, teams, TFW releases, and coordination modes, so the company can improve practice and resource allocation without rewarding cheap but ineffective work.

**Impact:** An owner can see the economics of one task at close; a team lead can identify comparable work, distinguish measured consumption from allocated subscription cost, and share effective practices with teams whose results cost more or need more rework.

> **Imagined stakeholder quote:** “For comparable work, this team reduced elapsed time without increasing revisions; here are the task reports and the limits of the comparison.”

## 5. Definition of Done (DoD) 🔒 FROZEN

- ✅ 1. A new Full task has one final economics report at close, including all roles, the selected product/feature classification, quality outcome, and explicit data coverage; unsupported values display as unknown rather than disappearing.
- ✅ 2. Supported token and time figures can be reproduced from named source observations and epochs. At least one real provider/task path demonstrates measured role-level token capture or the owner rules a changed target before implementation continues.
- ✅ 3. Money is labeled as actual variable billing, explicit subscription allocation, estimate, or unknown; the method, price/currency date, and coverage are visible wherever a dollar figure is shown.
- ✅ 4. A reader can generate a comparison for selected comparable tasks across team, product area, TFW version, and mode, with quality and revision evidence, denominator, and missing-data counts next to efficiency figures.
- ✅ 5. An independent Reviewer verifies the implemented contract and a field pilot; the owner accepts the actual result before task close or wider adoption.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. An inferred or allocated dollar amount is presented as an actual provider bill or exact marginal task cost.
- ❌ 2. Missing token/time observations are filled with invented zeros or silent estimates, or a number cannot be traced to a method and source epoch.
- ❌ 3. A team or coordination mode is ranked from raw spend or speed without task comparability, quality, revision, and coverage context.
- ❌ 4. The report becomes a second authoritative lifecycle state, a raw conversation archive, or a mandatory cross-task mutable index.
- ❌ 5. Instrumentation requires private reasoning, credentials, unrelated chat inspection, or unsupported provider behavior.
- ❌ 6. Provider-level token capture is unavailable in every investigated route, yet the product claims measured per-task token economics without an owner-approved change of scope.

**On failure:** Preserve the evidence, state the unsupported claim or missing source, return to the owner for a bounded scope decision, and revise the approved contract through §12 if its frozen outcome must change.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Measure the decision, not activity for its own sake.** Every collected field must support a task-close or management decision that the owner named.
2. **Provenance before precision.** Distinguish measured, derived, allocated, estimated, and unknown values; record source epoch and method for every numeric conclusion.
3. **Quality and purpose alongside consumption.** Savings are beneficial only when the accepted result still serves its purpose and its evidence is adequate.
4. **Comparable cohorts before conclusions.** Control or disclose task type, size/complexity, quality, and missingness before comparing teams, releases, or modes; do not claim causation from a simple before/after table.
5. **Task-local truth, portable comparison.** Keep authoritative records with their tasks and derive cross-task views on demand from inspectable, provider-independent sources.
6. **Proportionate capture.** Prefer existing durable task and provider observations; do not add reporting ceremony whose cost defeats the information value.

### 7.1 Quality Contract 🔒 FROZEN

Both phases preserve the distinction between accepted purpose, verified quality, cost, and time. Reports show data coverage and do not compress these into a single team score. A provider integration may have narrower coverage than the provider-neutral report contract and must state that boundary.

## 10. RESEARCH Case 🟢 FREE

### Blind Spots

- Which current Codex and other target surfaces expose task- and role-level token counts, timestamps, effective model settings, and usage records with stable identifiers?
- How should fixed subscriptions, API billing, currency conversion, and shared hardware be kept separate in an honest task report?
- What minimal product/feature and task-complexity classification permits comparisons without a maintained portfolio index?

### Hypotheses

| # | Decision-changing hypothesis | Plausible false case and consequence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | At least one target provider exposes measured task/role token usage that can be bound to a TFW task. | Only a shared quota or incomplete conversation usage is visible; the token capture outcome and first supported route need owner redecision. | Proposed for owner review |
| H2 | Time and monetary bases can be reported without treating calendar waiting or subscription allocation as provider processing time or actual task billing. | Timestamps or price basis are missing; affected fields remain unknown and comparisons narrow. | Proposed for owner review |
| H3 | A task can be assigned to a product/feature with little owner effort while shared work remains explicit. | Cross-cutting tasks dominate; the classification must permit shared/unassigned work and perhaps a different reporting unit. | Proposed for owner review |
| H4 | There is enough quality and task-context evidence to make a useful comparison among teams, releases, and modes. | Task mix and coverage differ too much; the product should report descriptive case studies, not rankings or causal gains. | Proposed for owner review |

### Risks of Not Researching

A mandatory close report could promise exact tokens or dollars that the provider cannot expose, while aggregate comparisons could favor teams with easier tasks or weaker review.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

1. **Gather:** Map actual usage, timing, billing, role-identity, and task-linking sources in the chosen pilot surface; distinguish documented capability from a live observation.
2. **Extract:** Define reproducible measures, source epochs, missingness, cost categories, and a minimal product/feature classification.
3. **Challenge:** Try false attribution, mixed subscriptions, multi-feature work, incomparable tasks, and mode changes; state which conclusions survive.

### Why Not Just...?

- Why not count text in chat files? — Chat text is not provider token accounting and would pressure TFW toward transcript storage.
- Why not divide the monthly subscription by task count? — The quotient is an allocation policy, not observed marginal cost; it may still be useful if labeled and justified.
- Why not publish a team efficiency leaderboard? — Raw cost and time omit task difficulty, purpose, quality, and missing data.
