## 3. Target State (To-Be) 🔒 FROZEN

| Owner question | Proposed output |
|---|---|
| Which project consumes more? | A selected-period comparison of helpdesk and afd with token volume, money basis, aggregate agent execution intervals and coverage. |
| What consumes time, money or tokens within a project? | Three separate rankings, each expandable by primary product area → task → phase/role/model. Known revision cycles and execution/wait intervals provide evidence; a causal explanation is not invented from totals. |
| What does one helpdesk task cost? | Complete task consumption across its lifetime, including all phases, roles, retries/revisions and reporting overhead. An available data subset is labeled partial. |
| What is a typical helpdesk task's cost? | Mean and median for explicitly selected completed tasks, their count and range; rejected/cancelled work remains visible. These are descriptive figures, not a universal price or an efficiency ranking. |
| Which product functionality received the work? | Stable product identity, one primary product area for additive totals, and 3–5 product-specific domain/feature keywords proposed at close and correctable without a new approval gate. |

**Artifact contract:** A completed task has one `economics.md` beside `status.md`. Each completed phase has its own `economics.md`; the task-level file combines phase consumption and task-level coordination without double-counting. The same Markdown file contains a structured, machine-readable numeric/identity block and a human-readable report; a second JSON report copy is unnecessary. `status.md` remains the lifecycle/routing authority. Collection happens during work; finalization produces the terminal report. Source observations and partial ongoing-task coverage must remain available for the period view; their capture/storage mechanics are a research outcome.

**Time contract:** Calendar elapsed time runs from task start to terminal result. Agent time is the sum of defined, observed execution intervals by role; parallel role intervals may overlap, so role-hours can exceed elapsed hours. Time spent in tools may be inside an agent interval and must not be added to it a second time. Idle/wait classifications need actual events; elapsed minus summed role time is not a valid waiting-time formula.

**Money contract:** Show observed provider charges where available and a dated tariff-based estimate where calculable. Subscription expenditure is a separate period-level fixed cost; a project's/task's share is an explicit allocation policy. Never add tariff estimates and allocated subscription expense into one total for the same consumption. An allocation across projects needs a declared eligible cost pool and coverage; unobserved other use must not silently be charged to the two pilot products.

**Period contract:** Period spending uses observations whose consumption occurred in the period, including ongoing and unsuccessful tasks. A completed task's full cost uses all its lifetime observations, even across month boundaries. Dividing this month's spending by this month's closed-task count is not generally a task unit cost.

**Attribution contract:** One primary product area receives an ordinary task's full cost; genuinely cross-cutting work uses an explicit shared category. Keywords are overlapping search filters, not additive allocations. Routine percentage splitting is deferred. Product identity and the product vocabulary are separate from TFW version, coordination mode and model metadata. Every numeric observation belongs once in a parent task total; summing a parent aggregate and its child rows is invalid.

## 5. Definition of Done (DoD) 🔒 FROZEN

- ✅ 1. A real supported pilot path yields reproducible task-bound token and execution-time observations; unavailable fields are explicit. Task identity survives phase/role separation and totals contain each observation once.
- ✅ 2. Each completed task/phase has one `economics.md` beside `status.md`, containing structured data and its readable report, result/revision evidence, 3–5 supported domain/feature keywords and an explicit primary area or shared/unclassified state. The root file reconciles child phases and its own coordination once.
- ✅ 3. The owner can compare helpdesk and afd over a chosen period and inspect a helpdesk task's lifetime costs; ongoing and unsuccessful work remains visible in period spending. The pilot demonstrates both product identities without claiming unsupported historical recovery.
- ✅ 4. Money is labeled as observed charge, tariff estimate, allocation or unknown; its source, currency/date, method and coverage are visible. Parallel agent-time sums and calendar elapsed time are separate.
- ✅ 5. Capture and classifier overhead are visible; an independent Reviewer verifies the implemented bounded contract and the owner accepts the delivered result. No personal effort or activity monitoring is collected in this first version.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. Task economics is reconstructed from text length or guessed counters and presented as observed consumption.
- ❌ 2. Parent/child totals, cumulative snapshots or overlapping cost bases are counted more than once.
- ❌ 3. Missing data is silently zero, tariff estimates are represented as actual bills, or partial project coverage is represented as a complete spending comparison.
- ❌ 4. Tags multiply costs, a period/task denominator mixes incompatible windows, or summed role time is represented as the owner's labor or calendar duration.
- ❌ 5. A universal team score or productivity ranking substitutes for the selected project-cost questions.
- ❌ 6. The pilot requires raw private reasoning/transcript harvesting, unrelated role history, production access or a mandatory mutable portfolio authority.
- ❌ 7. The proposed route cannot produce real task-level usage, yet implementation proceeds claiming the measurement outcome has been met.

**On failure:** Preserve the observed limitation and return the affected product promise for discussion. A different provider/surface or narrower capture target needs an explicit scope decision before it replaces the intended result.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Business questions select fields.** Start with the owner's project/task decisions; keep future comparison metadata small.
2. **Three resources, three answers.** Tokens, money and time have independent units and may rank projects differently.
3. **Product vocabulary belongs to the product.** Share measurement semantics across installations and reuse existing domain/feature terms locally.
4. **Evidence before precision.** Preserve measurement method, source epoch, coverage and unknown states.
5. **Task-local truth, derived overview.** On-demand project views are disposable projections of inspectable task records.
6. **Proportionate operating cost.** One bounded close-time classifier is the initial recommendation; measure its overhead before optimizing the inference infrastructure.
7. **Quality stays visible.** Preserve result, review and rework alongside spending; failed work still consumes resources.

|---|---|---|

### Why Not Just...?

| Approach | Value | Adoption / operating burden | Risks / tradeoffs | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Final reports only; manually compare completed tasks | Fastest way to understand individual task cost | Small close integration; manual comparisons | Hides ongoing project consumption and makes period comparisons awkward | Useful fallback, but insufficient for the owner's first project-spending question |

**Leave unchanged:** Account invoices and quota indicators remain detached from product functions and full tasks; comparisons rely on recollection.

**Critical challenge:** A simpler final-report-only design is cheaper, but may never answer which active project is consuming the budget. The recommended design becomes wrong if obtaining period observations requires a large permanent service on the working platforms. In that case, discuss a bounded export/snapshot cadence and visibly partial coverage instead of silently building infrastructure.

A hook is a trigger, platform events are measurement sources, and models classify meaning. They are separate responsibilities. The classifier uses purpose, final result, task-owned changes and vocabulary; final files alone miss deleted work, investigation and rejected approaches.
