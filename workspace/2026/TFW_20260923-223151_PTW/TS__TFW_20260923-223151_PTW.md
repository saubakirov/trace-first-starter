## 1. Objective

Deliver a portable optional `tfw-daily-task` skill. One clear bounded request leads to project/current-source/prior-work inspection, purposeful execution, a checked usable result and selected Trace, without a second approval exchange. Another authorized participant can recover purpose, source, result, check and the authoritative next step without the original chat.

The first version retains the dated-folder default, a Daily-owned template for new projects, and existing local record splits. It does not claim a universally cheaper one-file form or measured team-scale retrieval.

**Finished-state rendering, illustrative rather than observed:** A user asks to fix login without changing registration. The worker inspects current login sources and a relevant earlier repair, restores login, observes the focused check, and leaves `task.md` linking the changed product file. A colleague can inspect the repair and see that release still belongs to its named authority.

- A clear request stalls on a redundant approval ritual, or the worker edits before relevant current-source/authority inspection.
- A well-formed result loses actual purpose, material human instructions, old meaning, real source/worker or authoritative continuation.
- Daily skips formal Full duties after entering formal work, or uses urgency/name/title as permission for a reserved effect.
- Installation overwrites local practice/history, modifies Full HL/RF templates, adds a Full command, leaks field content, or creates another semantic source.
- Evidence claims unobserved execution, host behavior, acceptance, release or universal performance. Folder count alone cannot establish lower cost.
- A failed package proof or unapproved A2 is treated as approved implementation/installation readiness.
