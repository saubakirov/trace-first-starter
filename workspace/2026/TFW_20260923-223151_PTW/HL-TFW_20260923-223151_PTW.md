## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

The same Trace-First Workflow ideas serve an ordinary bounded Task: a person keeps purpose, legitimate authority, acceptance and responsibility; an agent understands Goal, Value, Question, Context and Boundaries, produces a usable result, checks it, and leaves a selected Trace with the current state and continuation point. For work whose risk permits it, one agent can perform these actions in one working session. The user encounters the same meanings taught by the TFW philosophy, essay and SenseLab, without learning a second method for small work.

**Impact:** Another authorized participant can see why the work existed, who requested and performed it, what was decided and produced, what was actually checked, and who owns the next decision. The agent spends less effort on ceremony while preserving the meaning of the work.

> **Imagined stakeholder quote, not testimony:** “This is the same TFW I learned: the goal and value are clear, the result is usable, and the trace lets my colleague continue.”

| Established TFW concept | Required behavior in a small Task |
|---|---|
| Goal and Value | State what should change and for whom before choosing files or actions. Use the result-first questions already present in HL and Assisted planning. |
| Question, Context and Boundaries | Expose uncertainty that changes the result; cite relevant source material; say what the agent may decide, what is outside the Task, who accepts the result and when to stop. |
| Task and role | One bounded piece of work has a human accountable for purpose and an actual named worker. A working unit, model, profile or session title is never a grant of authority. |
| Trace | Preserve selected material words of the human with their author and source, material decisions, result or actual state, verification and the continuation point. Existing project identity rules apply; absence of a profile never authorizes an invented identity. |
| Evidence and acceptance | Check the claim that matters in the real task; report what was observed and what remains uncertain. Prepared, checked, accepted and released are not interchangeable claims. |
| Continuation | End with an accepted close or the exact next human/agent decision. Preserve earlier material meaning when it changes, using ordinary file history or a justified version. |

For a small reversible Task, an agent may plan, perform and check within one authorized session only after the governing project rules admit that form of work. The upstream Full `Trace Discipline` currently says every task has RF, `status.md` and journal, while SenseLab's local Daily rule uses other records. That conflict is a design question, not an implicit exception granted by the field skill. The final design must either establish an explicit coherent small-Task contract in Full or retain a clearly project-local practice without claiming upstream compatibility. Formal Full Tasks keep their existing Coordinator, Executor, Reviewer, status and authority boundaries. Consequence and uncertainty determine whether stronger Evidence or independent review is needed.

The location of a small Task's Trace is a project storage decision. `daily/` is the SenseLab field choice and a candidate for Full projects if an explicit coherent small-Task contract admits it; `work/` is the existing Light example. The skill must follow the receiving project's storage rules, avoid collisions with formal Full task discovery, and preserve existing identifiers. One `TRACE.md` plus result files is an illustrative minimum drawn from Light, not an approved replacement for Full task artifacts; additional files require a real need.

### 3.2 Value Flow

`human purpose → Goal / Value / Question / Context / Boundaries → bounded Task → result + proportionate Evidence → selected Trace → accepted close or authoritative continuation`.

At each arrow, the record preserves only the information needed to understand purpose, inspect the result, or make the next legitimate decision.

- ✅ 1. The skill uses existing TFW concepts with their established meanings. Its user-facing request, plan, result and next action are recognizable to someone taught by the Project North Star, Light and SenseLab; no new lifecycle vocabulary is required.
- ✅ 2. One bounded Task yields a usable result and selected Trace with Goal, Value, consequential Question, Context, Boundaries, actual human and worker, material decisions, Evidence, state and authoritative continuation or close.

- ✅ 4. Authorized ordinary work proceeds without redundant owner gates; a formal Task, missing authority or material risk invokes the relevant existing boundary and proportionate assurance.
- ✅ 5. A neutral skill and bounded examples work in at least two project contexts without SenseLab identities or content, a required service, or a second semantic source for provider copies.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. New labels, statuses, document roles or a different user ritual duplicate Goal, Value, Task, Trace, Evidence, acceptance or the existing edition's authority model.
- ❌ 2. A small-Task record writes or claims a Full task's `status.md`, journal, HL/TS/RF/REVIEW verdict, or one worker crosses an active Full Role Lock.
- ❌ 3. A title, profile, thread address, chat wording or urgency is treated as authority for scope, release, publication or communication.
- ❌ 4. Source text is fabricated, a material contrary instruction is omitted without disclosure, or secret material is copied into a shared Trace.
- ❌ 5. A consequence-bearing change is waved through because it is called Daily or touches few files; necessary verification, review or a reserved human decision is skipped.

**On failure:** retain the actual source and state, stop the affected action, name the missing authority or assurance, and route to the governing project workflow or human owner. Do not reclassify a formal task silently.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Purpose before activity** — state Goal and Value before optimizing a Task; registration without a useful result is failure.
2. **Questions before premature answers** — expose decision-changing uncertainty and investigate enough to act honestly.
3. **Selected Trace, not transcript** — preserve material human source, context, decisions, actual state, result and continuation without elevating chat chronology to authority.
4. **Human authority, bounded delegation** — distinguish accountable person, actual worker and acceptance authority; name the Boundaries and stop condition.
5. **Continuation over isolated output** — leave a usable result and enough Trace for another authorized participant to continue.
6. **Assurance proportional to risk** — choose checks, Evidence and independent review for the consequence, not for the adjective “daily” or a file count.
7. **Naming Creates Behavior and Portability** — prefer the method's existing terms and ordinary project files; any proposed new name must cue the same action across providers and editions.

### Risks of Not Researching

Copying the field skill verbatim would make SenseLab's profile system, complete message log and file triad appear universal. Importing a new vocabulary into Full would create a second authority or user method. Source comparison establishes the conceptual correction for this HL, but exact name, placement and installation compatibility remain decision-changing questions for the next Plan/research gate.

### Proposed RESEARCH Focus

After HL review, reconcile Full's task and Role Lock rules with the field practice; evaluate the smallest coherent canonical change, candidate names under `Naming Creates Behavior`, storage and distribution. Use Light and Assisted as sources of existing meaning and forms, not as installation targets assumed in advance.

### Why Not Just...?

- Copy the SenseLab skill to every project? It preserves a useful local implementation, but its project-specific identity and complete-message duties would become false universal rules.
- Install TFW Light wholesale into a Full project? Light's Trace and work cycle are relevant; replacing a project's installed edition or introducing another root state model is a different act.
- Send every small task through the formal Full lifecycle? It preserves formal assurance but spends separate-role and evidence costs even when a direct bounded check is sufficient.
