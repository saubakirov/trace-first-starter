### E4 — The economic choice needs a separate explicit statement

The service route changes the *candidate payment model* to an eligible PAYG workspace, not a proved single flat-rate subscription. Preserve four separate quantities: human operator/member costs, any service-identity fees or minimums, metered workload cost, and operational overhead. Each can be unknown; none is assumed zero.

For a selected period and comparable capability:

`service-route total = required human charges + service-account charges/commitments + metered usage at actual credit price + taxes/operation costs`

The number of service identities (S1/S2), required human seats and token count must not be assumed to be the same billing unit. Compare the resulting actual total with separate subscriptions and the API alternative under the same workload/capability assumptions. Public standard Business catalogue prices do not fill these service-specific variables.

A choice of transport within the existing purpose can be a refinement. A concrete accepted change from “one shared paid subscription” to a materially different PAYG commitment, a lower capability, or a waived real pilot may alter frozen claims and must be classified by the Coordinator/owner. No such amendment is proposed or approved merely because this alternative is documented.
