## Findings

### C1 — Service management and launch evidence have different subjects

Rechecked [Service accounts](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/service-accounts), especially assigned roles and account lifecycle. Human management roles and the service account's own workspace grants are separate. The page states that service runs receive service attribution; possible token-creator/settings audit evidence is different. Disabling/deleting a service account revokes its active tokens.

Consequences for S1/S2: two tokens for one identity do not prove project-specific authority, and disabling the shared identity can affect both workloads. Separate identities can bound a failure or grant only if the actual assignments/storage/operator reach do so. Their count is not a proxy for isolation. Retain the actual human request, task/unit/mandate and provider context through E2; do not add a per-token TFW principal or copy credential values into a trace.

The [User lifecycle management](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/user-lifecycle) guide separately warns that removing a person does not replace reviewing automation credentials and connected-system access. This supports an actual later credential-owner/revocation check, not a new TFW account registry or automatic claim that every membership change revokes all access. No revocation or account administration was performed.

### C2 — Native Full role behavior remains a hard boundary

The CLI and SDK material gathered in Extract proves programmatic execution/continuation features.
