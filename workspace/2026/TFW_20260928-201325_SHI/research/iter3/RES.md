### Supported surfaces and their limits

[Service accounts](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/service-accounts) documents dedicated non-human workspace identity, management from operators' own accounts, separately assigned service permissions and CLI support from version 0.142.0 using an access token. [Access tokens](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/access-tokens) describes trusted noninteractive CLI/app-server automation for Business and Enterprise; the service-account page separately restricts service identities to PAYG plans. Personal member tokens and service-account tokens are distinct cases.

| Surface | Evidence-backed conclusion | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Trusted CLI runner | Documented headless service-account route; `codex exec` and exact session-ID continuation are documented. | No actual installed runtime, authentication or Full-role operation was tested. |
| Local app-server client | Generic access-token support plus dedicated service tokens creates a conditional combined-documentation fit. | Exact service-token/client compatibility and full operating behavior need actual verification. |
| Remote WebSocket | The app-server documentation explicitly calls this surface experimental/unsupported. | Do not report it as a production-supported route. |
| Ordinary desktop | Normal personal ChatGPT/API authentication is documented. | Inspected documentation does not establish service-account-token desktop sign-in; absence of a documented procedure is not proof of impossibility. |

Sources: [Non-interactive mode](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/non-interactive-mode), [Codex SDK](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/codex-sdk), [App-server](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app-server), [Authentication](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/auth). A client-to-server transport credential is separate from the Codex credential used for provider calls. No token value belongs in TFW evidence.

**Full-role gap:** the selected actual surface must provide distinct user-visible, directly addressable Coordinator, Researcher, Executor and Reviewer tasks; exact addressed sends; event-driven gated readback; title write/readback; and separate worktrees for mutating units. CLI jobs, SDK threads and resumable IDs alone do not establish these capabilities. Hidden helpers, guessed addresses, “last session” routing or a provider switch cannot fill the gap. Missing capability requires a concrete Coordinator/owner operating decision. A narrow successful authentication smoke test would still not establish the whole pilot. Available JSON reasoning/tool streams do not authorize collecting or relaying unreturned role content.

Compare required human charges, service-identity charges/commitments, metered usage at the actual credit price, taxes and operating costs under the same workload, concurrency and capability assumptions.
