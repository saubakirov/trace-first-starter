Official sources, current TFW carriers and bounded read-only host observations narrowed the design without changing the host, subscription, product, HL or control files. The important negative finding is that sharing an individual OpenAI account is not a permitted interpretation under the inspected public terms. A permitted economic arrangement actually available to the pilot remains unverified.

## Conclusion

This iteration prevented a technically convenient shared-account setup from being mistaken for permitted multi-person use and showed why launch declarations, host naming and project access need separate evidence. The existing Linux computer already runs two XRDP-managed user sessions, while TFW's present artifacts offer plausible places for the missing launch facts without a global registry. These are bounded findings: cached entitlement is not current billing proof, design challenges are not a real-host trial, and no price saving, final architecture or implementation has been approved. The useful next step is a second focused iteration on actual arrangement input, minimum carrier coverage and the later trial design.
