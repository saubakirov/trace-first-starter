## 1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN

A product lead prepares an HL on a personal computer and publishes it with the project. A colleague opens that project on the named, powerful Linux computer, declares the launch, and starts approved Full TFW work; another project can run there at the same time in a separate workspace. The team aims to pay for one shared AI subscription only where the selected provider and plan permit that use. The task trace lets the owner see who authored the HL, who declared each launch, where agents ran, which exact units acted, and which approved mandate allowed them.

**Impact:** One host can carry long-running computation for several projects; an approved HL can move between people without re-explaining the plan; separate traces make parallel work inspectable. A single-subscription saving is a target to verify against an actual paid plan, not an established saving. Self-declared human identity is useful provenance with weak assurance, not proof of who physically pressed a button.

> **Explicitly imagined stakeholder quote:** “Anna wrote the plan on her laptop, Bek declared its launch on saubakirov-home-linux-01, and Dana's other project ran there separately. I can see the approved authority and both results without asking them to reconstruct their chats.”

- ✅ 2. Personal-computer and shared-computer rules coexist. Existing bindings and historical task traces remain readable without assigning a new human or host retrospectively.

- ✅ 4. Sessions, workspaces, access and launch/result lineage are inspectable; any unavailable client device is shown as unknown.
- ✅ 5. Independent review can reproduce the attribution and isolation claims from the recorded output; the owner sees the actual Plan passages and decides final acceptance.

## 6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN

- ❌ 1. A host name, project binding, account display, OS user, title or self-declaration is presented as proof of a human identity or as a TFW mandate.
- ❌ 2. Shared use of a paid subscription is claimed permissible without checking the actual provider/plan terms, or a prohibited use is implemented as the accepted pilot.
- ❌ 3. Two projects share mutable working state, credentials or authority in a way that can attribute an act or result to the wrong project/person.
- ❌ 4. A migration rewrites historical initiators, breaks personal-machine use, adds a mandatory vendor runtime to Full TFW, or changes ATC's role routes by implication.
- ❌ 5. A diagram or simulated record is reported as a successful real-host trial.

**On failure:** stop the affected launch or claim, preserve the actual evidence, and return the smallest decision-changing issue through the task Coordinator to the owner. A provider restriction may require a new economic arrangement or a frozen-HL amendment; it is not waived by changing the transport.

## 7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN

1. **Separate facts by subject.** Person, declared principal, client device, execution host, provider account, working unit and mandate each have a different job. Neither naming nor colocation merges them.
2. **State confidence.** A person's self-declaration is useful provenance, but its weak assurance is explicit; an absent or conflicting declaration is unknown and supplies no authority.
3. **Keep authority local and exact.** The approved HL/TS, task-local status and immutable dispatch/selection lineage decide permission. Device identity is never a route, approval or grant.
4. **Preserve portability and history.** TFW rules live in inspectable project files; provider-specific connection details stay at their selected adapter or receiving environment. Do not rewrite old traces to suggest new knowledge.
5. **Prove the central claim.** A real host, two projects and the actual subscription terms are necessary evidence for the stated shared-computer objective; disclose limits rather than substitute a mockup.
6. **Record only material provenance.** Capture launches, authority-bearing transitions and durable writes needed for accountability; avoid a new all-actions telemetry system without demonstrated value.

### 7.1 Quality Contract 🔒 FROZEN

Across phases, use the same subject names and confidence meanings. A phase may refine storage and transport but may not turn declared identity into authentication, host name into authority, or subscription access into a license right.
