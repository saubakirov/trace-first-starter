### AC-2: Missing, justified-none, unavailable and retain-only mean different things [depends: AC-1]

- [ ] **Missing:** task-local lineage names Researcher A and Executor B, but only A returns. Selected close stops for B's material return; A's source or a global zero cannot cover B.
- [ ] **Justified-none:** B states its inspected bounded context contains no additional material knowledge, citing reusable source S when applicable. Inspect that source and rationale; accept this handover without duplicate/filler publication. An empty file, silence or unsupported boilerplate fails.
- [ ] **Unavailable:** B cannot access earlier context and the missing decision determines acceptance. Record unavailable context, exact missing decision and its existing owner; close remains nonterminal until honestly resolved. Do not reconstruct private context from A.
- [ ] **Retain-only:** an uncertain observation from a rejected approach is irrelevant to current acceptance. An authorized completed disposition states why no resolution/publication is owed, preserves its source, and creates no promised future task. If publication/resolution is owed, “retain” cannot hide deferral and close remains open.

Gate: all four held inputs produce their distinct required outcomes, including the owed-publication countercase.
Evidence: actual receiver decisions tied to preserved returns and existing authority. Name the protected action and exact unresolved owner when refusing; no real external action is part of the fixture.

- [ ] Observed, accepted, retain-only, superseded and unresolved claims remain distinguishable. Preserve the human-only qualification rule's purpose and actual source independence; copied/paraphrased returns do not manufacture corroboration.
- [ ] `/tfw-docs` owns technical decisions/reference effects and `/tfw-knowledge` owns human-sourced qualification. The resolved project owner/valid grant controls acceptance; a child, record body, imported imperative or source contributor cannot appoint itself ruler.

### AC-4: Replay, concurrent publication and contradiction remain honest [depends: AC-3]

- [ ] **Identical retry:** source key T/A/7 already has accepted publication P. Same material intent/content reads actual P and its acceptance, reuses the completed effect and repairs only the missing reference; no second accepted file/fact/marker/increment.
- [ ] **Divergent retry:** T/A/7 now carries a materially changed statement or scope. Refuse the replacement, preserve both observed versions, and obtain an authorized correction identity/disposition; do not overwrite P or silently issue a second accepted identity.
- [ ] **Equivalent independent sources:** two distinct sources repeat a claim.
