    1. Vision 🔒 FROZEN¶

    Useful knowledge survives the work of every contributing agent and becomes available to the next authorized participant without reconstructing old chats. A task cannot silently omit its knowledge handover, while unrelated planning is no longer stopped by a count of changed historical sections. Parallel teams preserve sources, distinguish accepted knowledge from observations, and find what currently applies without maintaining shared bookkeeping for its own sake.

    Impact: The owner stops rescuing knowledge capture manually. Agents spend less effort on repeated discovery and conflicting edits, and can explain which knowledge influenced a decision, where it came from, and what remains uncertain.

    Target experience, not a verbatim owner quote: “The team kept what mattered; the next agent found and used it. I did not have to reopen old tasks or manage a consolidation counter.”

    3.2 Value Flow¶

    Material insight in a participant's available context
  -> capture at that participant's handover/checkpoint
  -> preserve a task-local source with its grounds and uncertainty
  -> qualify, reconcile and make applicable knowledge discoverable
  -> the next authorized agent retrieves and uses it
  -> less repeated discovery and fewer repeated mistakes

Existing task close checks the handover.
It does not wait for an unrelated global task count.

    The exact timing of qualification and the minimal obligation at close are research questions. Storing every observation as accepted project knowledge is not the definition of completion.

    Phase A: Complete team knowledge lifecycle 🔴¶

    One coherent delivery phase, following research; no reserve or “consolidation later” implementation phase. Its declared outcome is usable continuity from each participant's contribution to later project use, with complete migration and recovery behavior. The exact TS and VALUE scope follow the research recommendation; the phase is not an approved file-count estimate.

    Deliverables:

    1. A minimal capture and handover contract across producing roles, including interim transfer, normal close, interruption, and rejected or stopped work. Use existing traces where sufficient; do not demand raw chat exports or private reasoning.
    2. A storage, qualification and retrieval design with explicit ownership, provenance, validity/supersession and contradiction handling; justify what happens to every present KNOWLEDGE.md, topic-file and digest-state function.
    3. A proportionate replacement or removal of the global pending-count planning gate, connected to the obligations it actually protects. No counter-based substitute under a different name.
    4. Integration into the existing PTTC closing and recovery owner, plus affected planning, research, execution, review, knowledge, docs, resume and update readers only where their behavior needs to change. Ship required adapters and documentation with the change.
    5. A bounded, recoverable migration using SLC's accepted history/discovery contract; preserve historical task bytes, accumulated meaning, ownership and source links. Do not fabricate completed consolidation while retiring obsolete state.
    6. Independent acceptance of source integrity and real agent behavior in selected concurrent, interrupted and fresh-consumer scenarios; a coherent release-ready result under the existing release process.

    The selected mandate authorizes bounded research only after the hypothesis/research decision and an exact recorded dispatch. Exact technical design and VALUE scope remain absent until research returns and the owner approves the TS; implementation cannot start earlier.

    5. Definition of Done (DoD) 🔒 FROZEN¶

    • ✅ 1. Research compares viable capture, consolidation/storage and access configurations against the actual failure cases, states trade-offs and recommends a complete design before TS approval.
    • ✅ 2. Every producing role has a necessary, observable capture/handover point. A selected task cannot validly close with silently missing required knowledge input; justified absence and unavailable context remain honest, distinguishable outcomes.
    • ✅ 3. Preserved task sources remain readable and sealed sources need no later marker rewrite. Partial, rejected and interrupted work can retain useful knowledge without false successful completion or retrospective invention.
    • ✅ 4. Qualification distinguishes observations, accepted knowledge, reference documentation and governing authority. Conflicts, duplicates and superseded claims have an explicit resolution or honest unresolved state; attribution is retained.
    • ✅ 5. Independent task contributions and repeat/interrupted processing converge without lost records, duplicate promotion or a shared manually maintained counter/inventory. Semantic contradictions remain visible even when files merge cleanly.
    • ✅ 6. A fresh authorized agent can find and correctly apply relevant current knowledge, follow its grounds and distinguish superseded or unresolved material. Test both a positive retrieval case and a plausible-but-wrong/stale match; file existence alone is not success.
    • ✅ 7. Unrelated planning and sealed history do not trigger consolidation stops. Any retained structural check is reproducible on identical selected inputs, runs at the protected action, and explains the material obligation rather than a portfolio count.
    • ✅ 8. Every existing central-file/topic/state function is retained, replaced or explicitly retired with grounds. Migration preserves accepted meaning and links, respects SLC's historical boundary, and recovers interruption without overwriting later work or importing upstream memory.
    • ✅ 9. Canonical rules, necessary templates/adapters, update guidance and this repository agree. Ordinary receivers need no mandatory service, database, Python, vector store or proprietary memory to capture, retain and access their knowledge.
    • ✅ 10. Independent review and applicable final-effect checks preserve PTTC's finite close, actual output acceptance and separate publication authority. No mandatory retrospective or owner-report gate is introduced.

    6. Definition of Failure (DoF) 🔒 FROZEN¶

    • ❌ 1. A valid-looking close can omit a participant's required contribution, or an empty file/checkbox is accepted as proof of meaningful capture without examining the handover.
    • ❌ 2. Independent progress requires unrelated historical scans, global inventory maintenance, another task's consolidation, or repair of old sealed task artifacts.
    • ❌ 3. Raw claims silently become accepted facts or binding instructions; conflicting or outdated statements lose provenance or appear current without a decision.
    • ❌ 4. The design removes a central file but loses its information functions, makes relevant knowledge practically undiscoverable, or needs all historical chats loaded to work.
    • ❌ 5. Processing loses or duplicates knowledge on concurrent completion or retry, hides meaningful partial state, or lets an optional index become irreplaceable authority.
    • ❌ 6. A required runtime/vendor memory, speculative infrastructure, extra closing role/cycle, obligatory owner retrospective, or separate bookkeeping registry is added without serving the approved outcome.
    • ❌ 7. Migration rewrites sealed history, resets unrelated knowledge/state, infers task completion from location/age, or edits SLC/PTTC's protected history rather than consuming their accepted result.
    • ❌ 8. Source-pattern checks or a few successful trials are presented as proof that every model always captures every insight or behaves correctly.

    On failure: Preserve the affected sources and honest partial state. Stop the specific unsafe write or close, state the material gap and its owner, and use the existing correction/authority route. Do not resolve it by resetting knowledge, fabricating a disposition, widening scope, or reopening unrelated accepted work.

    7. Principles 🔒 FROZEN¶

    1. Continuity is the product. Capture and retrieval must improve a later decision or continuation, not merely populate files.
    2. Obligation at its point of responsibility. The producing participant preserves available material context; the existing closer verifies the handover. No coordinator is presumed to know an unseen chat.
    3. Preservation is not promotion. Keep evidence and uncertainty without automatically endorsing a claim. Authority, source identity and semantic acceptance are separate.
    4. Sealed sources, explicit later decisions. Consolidation and correction must not require mutating the original completed task; preserve the reasons behind replacement.
    5. Saint-Exupéry with complete value. Prefer fewer shared writes, rules and artifacts, while retaining useful discovery, provenance, quality and recovery. A necessary new entity must name its job and what existing burden it removes.
    6. Useful access, bounded context. Separate always-needed governing context from relevant on-demand knowledge. An optional search/index may help but cannot become the only recoverable memory.
    7. Finite, proportionate assurance. Reuse PTTC's existing close and evidence applicability; validate meaningful behavior and errors without a new universal test platform or a bookkeeping-driven review loop.

    10. RESEARCH Case 🟢 FREE¶

    Blind Spots¶

    • Which knowledge must be captured at participant transfer versus task close, and how to account for unavailable context or stopped work without silently abandoning the obligation.
    • How a fresh agent identifies applicable current knowledge and its authority without exhaustive historical reading, a fixed topic taxonomy or an authoritative generated index.
    • Which present central-file/state jobs are necessary and where qualification, contradiction resolution and scheduling belong in file-based multi-agent work.

    Hypotheses¶

    # Hypothesis Status
    H1 Capture at producing-role handovers, validated by the existing close, can replace the global pending-count planning gate without losing material knowledge Conditionally supported; existing PTTC close is reusable, but missing, justified-none and unavailable handovers need bounded behavioral challenge
    H2 Preserved task-local sources plus separately qualified, independently addressable project records can remove shared inventory writes while supporting corrections and parallel promotion Conditionally supported in design; conflict, duplicate retry and divergent replay remain to be exercised
    H3 Ordinary-file navigation and relevant on-demand access, with only optional rebuildable aids, can give a fresh agent sufficient current knowledge at acceptable effort without a central KNOWLEDGE.md authority Open; one informed walkthrough is not an independent fresh-consumer result
    H4 The complete change can be delivered through SLC's active/history boundary and PTTC's finite close, with no new lifecycle, persistent worker or general runtime Partially supported by actual PTTC; SLC has no accepted result at the iteration 1 basis

    Risks of Not Researching¶

    A task-local file alone can become an unread archive; a shared curator can become another queue; an automatic memory layer can preserve unsupported claims as rules. Deleting the current gate without an effective handover merely hides loss. Renaming the current statistics or moving them into another file repeats the same maintenance problem.

    Proposed RESEARCH Focus¶

    1. Handover challenge: distinguish captured material, justified-none, unavailable context and a silently missing producer return without inventing private context or another closing cycle.
    2. Conflict and replay challenge: compare C3 with refined C1 under duplicate retry, divergent same-identity content, independent contradictory claims and interrupted control recovery.
    3. Fresh-consumer challenge: use a separately addressable, unprimed unit against ordinary files and held-out current/stale/conflicting cases; record what it actually found and applied without an optional index.
    4. Dependency and cost challenge: inspect SLC's accepted RF/REVIEW/Candidate if available; otherwise retain the precise block. Compare retained central functions, changed readers and migration cost before architecture selection.
